Trustee Horowitz made a motion to approve the vouchers; seconded by Trustee Simon and
approved with a 5-0 Vote.
The Village Board proceeded to conduct a Local Waterfront Revitalization Program (LWRP)
Consistency Review for Local Law Introductory No. 5 of 2022. Upon reviewing the
memorandum submitted by the Waterfront Advisory Committee, the Village Board found that no
policies of the LWRP were applicable. The Board then proceeded to complete the Environmental
Assessment Form (EAF) Part II for Local Law Introductory No. 5 of 2022. The Village Board
responded “no, or small impact may occur” to all 11 questions of the EAF Part II. A formal
resolution to adopt these decisions will be drafted for the next Village Board meeting.
Also discussed at this work session was the proposed adopted of the NYStretch Energy Code,
revising the Volunteer Firefighter/EMS tax exemption, possible changes to the mailing
requirements for public hearings and a review of options for distributing the archeological
mitigation funds currently held in escrow.
