The following resolution was adopted by the Board of Trustees at the work session:
On motion of TRUSTEE GALLELLI, seconded by TRUSTEE SIMON, the following resolution
was adopted by the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, New York, with a 4-0
vote:
Resolution #47-2023
WHEREAS, the New York State Emergency Tenant Protection Act of 1974 (ETPA) enables
municipalities in New York State to opt-in to rent stabilization and other tenant protections
predicated on a continuing housing emergency constituting of a vacancy rate of less than 5%; and
WHEREAS, the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson held a Public Hearing on
May 5, 2003 to consider whether an emergency exists; and
WHEREAS, the Public Hearing on May 5, 2003 was also held to consider whether the
provisions of the Emergency Tenant Protection Act of 1974 should be invoked in regard to the
Village of Croton-on-Hudson; and
WHEREAS, on May 5, 2003, the Village Board declared that an emergency exists and declared an
emergency as to all buildings with fifty (50) or more housing units built prior to 1974; and
WHEREAS, before making any changes to the existing regulations, a vacancy survey must be
undertaken; and
WHEREAS, in October of 2022, the Village conducted a survey of all residential properties with six
or more units which were built before 1974 to determine the current vacancy rate in Croton-onHudson; and
WHEREAS, the survey results indicated that the Village has a current vacancy rate of 2.3%; and
WHEREAS, the Village Board wishes to consider whether the provisions of the Emergency Tenant
Protection Act of 1974 should be expanded to all properties built before 1974 with six (6) or more
housing units in the Village of Croton-on-Hudson; and
WHEREAS, municipalities that wish to adopt the regulations under ETPA must formally adopt a
resolution declaring a housing emergency under the ETPA, after a public hearing has been held with

a minimum of ten days’ notice and finding that the jurisdiction has less than 5% vacancy of
applicable rental units,
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED that the Village Board of Trustees hereby schedules a
Public Hearing for March 27, 2023, at 7:00 PM in the Georgianna Grant Meeting Room of the
Stanley H. Kellerhouse Municipal Building to consider declaring a housing emergency under the
Emergency Tenant Protection Act and expanding ETPA protections to all properties built before
1974 with six or more housing units in the Village of Croton-on-Hudson.
