An Executive Session of the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, NY was held on
Monday, March 22, 2021 via Zoom videoconference, pursuant to Executive Order 202.1 issued by
Governor Andrew Cuomo on March 12, 2020.
On a motion by Trustee Gallelli, seconded by Trustee Simon, the Board voted unanimously to enter into
an executive session at 6:16 PM for discussions related to matters of public safety.
The following officials were present:
Mayor Pugh
Trustee Gallelli
Trustee Horowitz
Trustee Simon
Village Manager Janine King
Chief of Police John Nikitopoulos
Superintendent of Public Works Frank Balbi
Secretary to the Village Manager Bryan Healy
No action was taken.
On a motion by Trustee Simon, seconded by Trustee Gallelli, the Board voted unanimously to adjourn the
Executive Session at 6:38 PM.
Submitted by,
Bryan Healy, Secretary to the Village Manager
