The following resolution was adopted by the Board of Trustees:
On motion of TRUSTEE GALLELLI, seconded by TRUSTEE SIMMONS, the following
resolution was adopted by the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, New York
by a vote of 5-0:
Resolution #99-2020
WHEREAS, the Village Board of Trustees would like to amend the May 3, 1993 resolution
authorizing a permanent special permit to Village merchants for outdoor displays and/or dining;
and
WHEREAS, this was discussed at the June 8, 2020 work session,
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED: that a permanent special permit to the Village
merchants is hereby approved to permit outdoor displays and/or dining subject to the following
conditions:
1. The special permit is effective for all business owners who wish to have outdoor displays
and/or dining.
2. The applicant must file a sketch plan to the Engineering Department for its approval prior
to setting up displays and/or tables and chairs.
3. The business owner must maintain at least a four-foot-wide space to allow for pedestrians
to pass.
4. Emergency egress out of each business establishment must not be blocked off in any
way.
5. Displays and/or outdoor dining will be permitted seven days a week but only between the
hours of 7 a.m. to 10 p.m.
6. Displays must be stored daily and cannot be left in any public area.
7. Display and dining areas must be kept clean, neat and litter free.
8. The business owners must discourage loitering.
9. Violation of any of the above conditions by any business owner will result in the
suspension and/or revocation of the permit for that particular merchant for such a period

of time as determined by the Village Manager based on the recommendation of the Code
Enforcement Officer.
Also discussed at this work session was the Van Cortlandt Manor Entrance Improvements
Project by Historic Hudson Valley, possible solar projects which could be done at the CrotonHarmon Train Station, COVID-19 guidance from New York State on utilizing certain Village
streets for outdoor dining and an update on anti-bias training in the police department from the
chief of police.
