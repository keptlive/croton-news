A Work Session of the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, NY was held on
Thursday, July 18, 2024, at 7:00 PM in the Georgianna Grant Meeting Room of the Stanley H.
Kellerhouse Municipal Building.
The following officials were present:
Mayor Pugh
Trustee Murtaugh
Trustee Simon
Trustee Nicholson

Village Manager Healy
Village Attorney Linda Whitehead

The following official was absent:
Trustee Politi
On a motion by Trustee Simon, seconded by Trustee Nicholson, the Village Board voted
unanimously to approve the request by Franquin Leon to use a portion of the Croton-Harmon Train
Station Parking Lot for a music festival on August 25, 2024, as detailed in the memo from Village
Manager Bryan Healy dated July 15, 2024.
In addition to Gouveia Park, the following items were discussed at the work session: a presentation
on the updated Housing Needs Assessment for Croton-on-Hudson from Westchester County Dept.
of Planning, a presentation from Village Historian Marc Cheshire on Revolutionary Westchester
250, a presentation from 1 HMB Property Owner LLC regarding their zoning petition to permit
multifamily housing at 1 Half Moon Bay Drive, a review of the May 2024 housing forum and a
review of the reservation of parkland regulations in the Zoning Chapter of the Village Code.
Respectfully Summitted,
Bryan Healy, Village Manager
