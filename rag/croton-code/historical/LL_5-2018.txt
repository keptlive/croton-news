A LOCAL LAW TO REPEAL LOCAL LAW NUMBER 3 OF 2018 AND TO REPEAL THE OVERRIDE OF
THE TAX LEVY LIMIT ESTABLISHED iN GENERAL MUNICIPAL LAW §3-c
Be it enacted by the Village of Croton-on-Hudson as follows:
SECTION 1: LEGISLATIVE INTENT: This Local Law shall be known as "Local Law. Number 5 of
2018". It is the intent of this Local Law to repeal Local Law Number 3 of 2018, and to thus
reinstate the limits on the amount of real property taxes that may be levied by the Village of
Croton-on-Hudson pursuant to General Municipal Law §3-c.
SECTION 2: EFFECT: This Local Law hereby thus repeals in its entirety said Local Law Number 3
of 2018, and declares the same of no force or effect, arid thus the Village of Croton-on-Hudson
repeals the prior tax cap override local law ofthe Village of Croton-on-Hudson and reinstates
the tax levy limits imposed by General Municipal Law, §37C.
SECTION 3: SEVERABILITY: If any portion this Local Law, or the application thereof to any
person, entity, or circumstance, shall be determined by any court or tribunal of competent
jurisdiction to be invalid or unenforceable, such deterriiination shall be confined in its operation
to the invalid part hereof, or in its application to such person, entity, or circumstance as is
directly Involved In the controversy in which such determination shall have been rendered, and
the remainder of this Local Law shall not be impaired thereby and such determination shall not
be deemed orconstrued to apply to other persons, entities, pr circumstances.
SECTION 4: EFFECTIVE DATE: This Local Law shall take effect immediately. .
