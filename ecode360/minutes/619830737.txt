An Executive Session of the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, NY was held on
Monday, August 23, 2021 at the Stanley H. Kellerhouse Municipal Building, 1 Van Wyck Street, Crotonon-Hudson, NY 10520.
On a motion by Trustee Gallelli, seconded by Trustee Murtaugh, the Board voted unanimously to enter
into an executive session at 7:02 PM for discussions on lease negotiations for real property.
The following officials were present:
Mayor Pugh
Trustee Gallelli
Trustee Horowitz
Trustee Murtaugh
Trustee Simon
Village Manager Bryan Healy
Village Attorney Linda Whitehead
Assistant to the Manager Thomas Morzello
No action was taken.
On a motion by Trustee Murtaugh, seconded by Trustee Gallelli, the Board voted unanimously to adjourn
the Executive Session at 7:39 PM.
Submitted by,
Bryan Healy, Village Manager
