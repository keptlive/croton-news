Hudson Village Code

Be it enacted by the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton on Hudson as follows:

SECTION I. PURPOSE AND INTENT
The purpose of the Parks, Recreation, and Education (PRE) District is intended
to preserve natural resources, scenic beauty and other land and community
resources whose retention is necessary for the continued maintenance of the
quality of the environment. It is designated to provide for public parks;
recreational activities including all types of athletic activities; schools and other
educational facilities; nature preserves; bird and wildlife sanctuaries; and similar
uses.
The PRE district zoning provisions are intended to ensure that no building or
other structure, facility or premises or any part thereof shall be constructed or
used which is arranged, intended or designed to be used, in whole or in part, for
any uses except passive open space, active open space, or educational
buildings.
Zoning of the specified parcels in both the Croton Gorge and Kaplan's Pond as
PRE will enhance and protect the valuable open space qualities of these lands
and fulfills a key policy recommendation of the Conservation Advisory Council's
adopted Open Space Report. Also designation of these lands under the Village's
PRE District is the best means to ensure the preservation of these lands in their
natural state.

sentence, subsection, word or part thereof is held illegal, invalid or
