of this Local Law.to repeal Local Law Number 1 of 2016, and to thus reinstate the limits on the amount of real
property taxes that may be levied by the Village of Croton-on-Hudson pursuant to General Municipal Law §3-c.
. SECTION 2: EFFECT: This Local Law hereby thus repeals in its entirety said Local Law Number 1_ of 2016, and
declares the same.of no force or effect, and thus the Village of Croton-pn-Hudson repeals the prior tax cap override
local law of the Village of Croton-oh-Hudson and reinstates the tax levy limits imposed by 'General Municipal Law §3-

c.

On motion of TRUSTEE.GALLELLI seconded by TRUSTEE WALSH, the
following resolution was adopted unanimously by the Board of Trustees of the
Village of Croton-on-Hudsori, New York; with a 5-0 vote.
WHEREAS, the State Legislature and the Governor enacted legislation that
establishes a "property tax cap" on the arnount that a local government's property
tax levy can increase each year; and WHEREAS, the state legislation provides for local governments to override the cap
to protect the Village from Unforeseen financial circumstances; and
WHEREAS, Local Law No. 1 of 2016 to override the "property tax cap" law was
adopted by the Village Board of Trustees at the January 4, 2016 Board Meeting;
and
.
' '
WHEREAS, the Village of drotoh-on-Hudson is not anticipating adopting a budget
exceeding the "property tax cap" law enacted by State Legislature and repeals Local
Law No. 1 of 2016, and
WHEREAS, a Public Hearing was held and closed on March 21, 2016,
NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED: that the Village Board of Trustees hereby
adopts Local Law Introductory No. 6 to repeal the prior tax cap override Local Law
1 of 2016 and reinstates the tax levy limits imposed by General Municipal Law §3-c
which upon adoption will become Local Law No. 2 of 2016.
