An Executive Session of the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, NY was held on
Thursday, August 13, 2020 via Zoom videoconference, pursuant to Executive Order 202.1 issued by
Governor Andrew Cuomo on March 12, 2020.
On a motion by Trustee Habib, seconded by Trustee Simmons, the Board voted unanimously to enter into
an executive session at 5:03 PM to discuss matters of personnel.
The following officials were present:
Mayor Pugh
Trustee Gallelli
Trustee Habib
Trustee Horowitz
Trustee Simmons
Police Lt. John Nikitopoulos
Lt. Nikitopoulos left the meeting at 5:30 PM.
No action was taken.
On a motion by Trustee Simmons, seconded by Trustee Horowitz, the Board voted unanimously to
adjourn the Executive Session at 6:17 PM.
Submitted by,
Ann Gallelli, Village Trustee
