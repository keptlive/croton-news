The following resolution was adopted by the Board of Trustees:
On motion of Trustee Simon and seconded by Trustee Murtaugh, the following resolution was
unanimously adopted by the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, New York
with a 5-0 vote.
Resolution #150-2021
WHEREAS, the Village of Croton-on-Hudson has been working with Sol Systems of
Washington, DC for the installation of solar canopies at the Croton-Harmon Train Station
parking lot; and
WHEREAS, on March 3, 2021, the Village entered into a lease agreement with Sol Systems for
the use of the Train Station Parking Lot in order to install solar canopies; and
WHEREAS, the Village Board hereby issued a special permit for the installation of solar
canopies at the Croton-Harmon Train Station parking lot on August 30, 2021; and
WHEREAS, there have been continued negotiations that have resulted in changes to the lease
agreement; and
WHEREAS, these changes have involved the need for solar battery incentive and contaminated
soil removal,
NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED: that the Village Board of Trustees hereby authorizes
the Village Manager to execute the amended lease agreement with Sol Systems to account for
the aforementioned renegotiations.

On motion of TRUSTEE HOROWITZ, seconded by TRUSTEE SIMON, the following
resolution was unanimously adopted by the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-onHudson, New York with a 5-0 vote.
Resolution #151-2021
WHEREAS, the Village of Croton-on-Hudson has owned a fire boat used commonly in the
Croton and Hudson rivers for rescues over the last 30 years; and
WHEREAS, the fireboat has recently been placed out of service and the Village desires to
replace the fireboat with a new piece of apparatus; and
WHEREAS, the Dormitory Authority of the State of New York (DASNY) recently announced a
new funding round for the State and Municipal Facilities Program (SAM), which allocates at
least $50,000 and no more than $100,000 to qualified applicants applying for capital purposes;
and
WHEREAS, the proposed purchase of a fire boat meets all the eligibility requirements of the
grant program, and would fund the following tasks for participating municipalities:
•
and

Acquisition of equipment and other capital assets including vehicles in support of health,
safety, technology, or innovation;

WHEREAS, the Village is applying for the maximum award amount of $100,000; and
WHEREAS, program guidelines require the Village Board to pass a resolution supporting
participation in the grant application,
NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED: that the Village Board of Trustees hereby supports
the submission of a grant application under the State and Municipal Facilities Program for the
purchase of a new fire boat.
Respectfully Submitted,
Pauline DiSanto, Village Clerk
