WHEREAS pursuant to § 459-c of the Real Property Tax Law, the Village of Crotonon-Hudson maintains a tax exemption for persons with disabilities at the maximum
level of 50% for qualified persons earning $19,500/year or less, and
WHEREAS on August 8, 2022, New York State adopted legislation increasing the
amount of income property owners may earn for the purpose of eligibility for the
property tax exemption for persons with disabilities, and
WHEREAS the option to increase the income eligibility level for the tax exemption
for persons with disabilities must be exercised through adoption of a local law,
ordinance, or resolution after public hearing, and
WHEREAS Local Law Introductory No. 16 of 2022 has been drafted to reflect such
language; and WHEREAS, a Public Hearing on Local Law Introductory No. 16 was
opened and closed on November 21, 2022,
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED: that the Village Board of Trustees hereby
adopts Local Law Introductory No. 16 of 2022 to increase the income eligibility level
for the property tax exemption for persons with disabilities, which upon adoption
becomes Local Law No. 15 of 2022.
DISCUSSION
Village Manager Healy advised that the new income level is $50,000, and
applications are due by the end of December.

WHEREAS on November 15, 2010, the Village Board of Trustees approved the
renewal of a special permit for the operation of a day care center at 114 Grand
Street at the Holy Name of Mary Church for a 5-year period, and
WHEREAS this special permit has expired and must be renewed in accordance with
the Zoning Chapter of the Village Code, and
WHEREAS the Church of the Holy Name of Mary has submitted a special permit
renewal application and a request to waive the renewal fee, and
8

WHEREAS renewal of the special permit does not require an environmental review,
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED that the Village Board hereby refers the special
permit application to the Village Planning Board in accordance with Village law, and
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that after consideration of the permit applicant’s
request, the permit fee of $250 is hereby waived.
DISCUSSION:
Village Attorney Whitehead responded to a question asking why this particular
permit has to be renewed every five years and stated that technically Special
Permits should run with the land, but the Board can put expirations on them if they
choose to do so. Village Attorney Whitehead added that she believes it is a good
practice to have Special Permits renewed especially if there might be a change in
that use or if the use has not been used for a while.
