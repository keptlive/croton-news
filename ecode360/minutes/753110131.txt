Croton Pride Day Event
The subcommittee (including Croton Pride) reported that the proposed
Senasqua location was not available on the preferred date of Oct. 19.
Committee members unanimously agreed to continue efforts for
coproducing the event, and the next steps were discussed.
Croton Welcomes Everyone
Subcommittee members shared plans to present COH WE at the next All
Business Meeting. The revised web page wording (submitted 8/01) was still
awaiting the Village lawyer’s review. The committee reviewed BOT feedback and
voted favorably to changes simplifying business participation
IDEA Youth Committee Members
CHHS Principal Dubak and Mayor Pugh had received youth membership idea
favorably. After discussion, the following guidelines were agreed upon:
● November 1 – June 30 term with option to remain active through summer
● High school juniors and seniors would be eligible for 4 spots
All agreed the on next steps as creating application process and publicizing at
the CHHS Volunteer Fair.
Brainstorm
The committee discussed upcoming national and global days of interest. A
village-wide read around the International Day of Disabled Persons garnered the
most interest.
