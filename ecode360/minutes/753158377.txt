4. Repair Café - John Ealer reported
-- Reasons for 3/29 cancellation – a new M.O.U. required by the umbrella organizaiton led the
Village legal team to investigate, they determined that hosting a repair café is not a function
enabled by state . The work, money, and location can continue to be provided by the CAC, but a
local non-profit must be a co-sponsor.
-- Next steps – talk to Sustainable Hudson Valley and potential co-sponsors (churches, library);
can Westchester Co. help?; aim for a date in early June. That will be a new fiscal year, so money in
this year’s budget for repair café to be redirected to Earth Day.
