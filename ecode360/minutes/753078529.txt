MEETING SUMMARY

Croton IDEA Advisory 07/10
10 July 2024 / 7 PM / MUNICIPAL BUILDING CONFERENCE ROOM
The committee reviewed Juneteenth events, discussed next steps for Croton
Welcomes Everyone and considered future programs.
Juneteenth
Positive reflections included good turnout at all three events despite afternoon heat
(with especially strong family turnout at Bennett), Therese Plair’s remarkable
singing and storytelling performance, Mayor Pugh’s nuanced and informative
words, positive environment at spoken word event, high school StAR (Students
Against Racism) club participation and two benefits of having multiple events
through the day: (1) increased and varied attendance and (2) full commemoration of
the holiday.
Lessons learned included having an indoor venue (initially or as contingency) and
increasing publicity (although there were flyers, social media and village media).
Expansion could involve reaching out to specific audiences, and also inviting groups
to educate and address intersecting concerns.
Croton Welcomes Everyone
Website is being revised to emphasize the opt-in premise of the program. Wording
should accurately convey the non-coercive approach intended. Goal is
engagement with all businesses keenly interested in providing inclusive spaces.
A shared brainstorming document was created for ideas to share during the
onboarding process.
Future Programming
Possible programs discussed include an anti-hate forum, civic education program,
calendar-driven events and Croton Pride Day. The committee voted in favor of
endorsing Croton Pride Day as presented by Croton Pride’s cofounder.
