An Executive Session of the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, NY was
held on Monday, April 17, 2023, in the Georgianna Grant Meeting Room of the Stanley H.
Kellerhouse Municipal Building.
On a motion by Trustee Simon, seconded by Trustee Gallelli, the Board voted unanimously to
enter into an executive session at 8:22 PM to discuss the proposed sale of real property.
The following officials were present:
Mayor Pugh
Trustee Gallelli
Trustee Horowitz
Trustee Nicholson
Trustee Simon
Village Manager Healy
No action was taken.
On a motion by Trustee Horowitz, seconded by Trustee Simon, the Board voted unanimously to
adjourn the Executive Session at 9:01 PM.
Submitted by,
Bryan Healy, Village Manager
