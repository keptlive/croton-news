INTRODliCTORY LOCAL LAW NO. 1 OF 2017

A LOCAL LAW AMENDING CHAPTER 76 OF THE VILLAGE CODE OF THE
VILLAGE OF CORNWALL-ON-HUDSON, NEW YORK AUTHORIZING THE
VILLAGE BOARD TO ADOPT A SCHEDULE OF FEES AND ESCROW
DEPOSITS ANNUALLY OR MORE FREQUENTLY IF REQUIRED
Be it enacted by the Village Board of Comwall-on-Hudson, County of Orange,
State of New York, as follows :
Section 1.

Amendment to Chapter 76 Section 3.A

Section 76-3. A of .the Village Code of Comwall-on-rHudson, entitled "Establishrrient by
Resolution" is repealed and replaced with the followirig text:
§ 76-3. Schedule of Fees Escrow Deposits
A. The Village of Comwall-on-Hudson shall adopt a Schedule of Fees and Escrow
Deposits resolution at the annual reorganization meeting. Ihe resolution shall be
revised and adopted from year to year, or more frequently i f required, and shall be
incorporated by reference into this chapter and shall have the force of law. The
resolution may add or delete categories, of fees or escrow deposits as may be
necessary to effectuate the purposes df this chapter. Any resolution setting a
Schedule of Fees and Escrow Deposits shall remain in frill force and effect until
superseded by a subsequent resolution adopting a modified Schedule of Fees and
Escrow Deposits. The current Schedule of Fees and Escrow Deposits shall be on
file at the office of the Village Clerk.
Section!.

Separability

If any part or provision of this Local Law or the application thereof to any person
or circumstance be adjudged invalid by any court of competent jurisdiction, such judgment
shall be confined in its operation to the part or provision or application directly involved in
the confroversy in vvhich such judgment shall have been reridered arid shall not affect or.
impair the validity ofthe remainder of this Local Law or the application thereof to other
persons or circumstances, and the Village Board of the Village of Comwall-On-Hudson
hereby declares that it would have passed this Local Law or the remainder thereof had
siich invahd application or invalid provision been apparent.
Section 3.

Repeal

All ordinances, local laws and parts thereof inconsistent with this Local Law are
hereby repealed.
