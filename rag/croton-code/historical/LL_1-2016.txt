"property tax cap" on the amount that a local government's property tax levy can increase each
year; and

NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED: that the Village Board of Tmstees hereby adopts
Local Law Introductory No. 1 of 2016 to override the "property tax cap" law enacted by State
