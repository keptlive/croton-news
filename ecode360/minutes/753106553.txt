A Special Meeting of the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, NY was held
on Wednesday, November 13, 2024 in the Georgianna Grant Meeting Room at the Stanley
Kellerhouse Municipal Building, 1 Van Wyck Street, Croton-on-Hudson, NY 10520.
Present:
Mayor Pugh
Trustee Simon
Trustee Murtaugh
Trustee Nicholson
Trustee Politi
Village Manager Bryan Healy
Mayor Pugh called the meeting to order at 6:08 PM.
A motion to close the special meeting and enter into executive session to discuss matters of
personnel involving specific individuals was made by Trustee Simon. The motion was seconded
by Trustee Politi and approved with a 5-0 vote. The meeting was adjourned at
6:09 PM.
Submitted by:
Bryan Healy, Village Manager
