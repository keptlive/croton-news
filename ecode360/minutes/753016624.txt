A Work Session of the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, NY was held on
Wednesday, January 31, 2024, at 7:00 PM in the Georgianna Grant Meeting Room of the Stanley
H. Kellerhouse Municipal Building.
The following officials were present:
Mayor Pugh
Trustee Murtaugh
Trustee Simon
Trustee Nicholson
Trustee Politi

Village Manager Healy
Emily Mancini, Assistant to the
Village Manager
Taryn Ramey, Associate Village
Attorney

On a motion by Trustee Nicholson, seconded by Trustee Simon, the Village Board voted
unanimously to refer the question of leashed dogs, short-term recreational activities and port-ajohns at Gouveia Park to the Recreation Advisory Committee for review and comment.
On a motion by Trustee Murtaugh, seconded by Trustee Politi, the Village Board voted
unanimously to endorse the memo submitted by the Gouveia Park Working Group.
In addition to Gouveia Park, the following items were discussed at the work session: the rules and
regulations for Senasqua Park, a proposed local law to increase the real property tax exemption for
qualifying Cold War veterans and to provide a tax exemption for Gold Star parents, and a proposed
local law to repeal the existing regulations on adult entertainment use businesses and adopt new
regulations regarding same.
Respectfully Summitted,
Bryan Healy, Village Manager
