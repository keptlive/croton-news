An Executive Session of the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, NY was held
on Thursday, April 4, 2024, in the Georgianna Grant Meeting Room of the Stanley H. Kellerhouse
Municipal Building.
On a motion by Trustee Simon, seconded by Trustee Murtaugh, the Board voted unanimously to
enter into an executive session at 6:11 PM to discuss the proposed acquisition, sale or lease of real
property when publicity would substantially affect the value thereof.
The following officials were present:
Mayor Pugh
Trustee Murtaugh
Trustee Nicholson (via phone)
Trustee Politi (via phone)
Trustee Simon
Village Attorney Whitehead
Village Manager Healy
No action was taken.
On a motion by Trustee Murtaugh, seconded by Trustee Simon, the Board voted unanimously to
adjourn the Executive Session at 6:46 PM.
Submitted by,
Bryan Healy, Village Manager
