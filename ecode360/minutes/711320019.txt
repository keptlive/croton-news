APPROVED Minutes of Croton Fire Council Regular Meeting
Tuesday, December 27, 2022
Roll Call: J. Munson (221), J. Karpoff (487), C. Colombo (133), J. Streany (275), M. Feminella
(162), J. Delaney (350), B. Powers (474), W. Skiba (267), K. Russell (576)

Department Members: T. Dinkler (152), P. Dinkler (150)
Others in Attendance: Mayor Brian Pugh
Meeting Called to Order without the Pledge of Allegiance or a Moment of Silence for
Deceased Members by Chief Munson at 19:02 due to the meeting being held at Fino
Restaurant.
Special Orders of Business / Reports: Mayor Pugh thanked the membership for all that
they do and looks forward to the year ahead.
Approval of Previous Months Meeting’s Minutes:
Motion: J. Streany / 2nd K. Russell to approve the minutes of the 11/29/2022 Council Meeting –
CARRIED.
Bills and Communications:
• Motion M. Feminella/2nd J. Streany to approve Excuses – CARRIED.
• Application for PAF Membership with Washington Engine received from Patrick Lally.
Motion M. Feminella/2nd J. Streany to approve – CARRIED
• Application for Active Membership with Croton Fire Patrol received from Sydney McCabe,
a previous PAF. Motion M. Feminella/2nd K. Russell to approve – CARRIED
• Letter from Fire Patrol listing elected Officers for 2023. Discussion about election of K.
Russell as a Lieutenant whom is not an Interior Member. Council concurred she cannot hold
this position until becoming Interior certified.
• Question from M. Feminella about election of John Delaney as a Lieutenant at Harmon
Engine and whether he is qualified. Per Chief Munson he has not completed proper training
to hold position, Council concurred he cannot hold this position until becoming Interior
certified.
