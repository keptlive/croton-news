An Executive Session of the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, NY was held on
Wednesday, May 5, 2021 at the Stanley H. Kellerhouse Municipal Building, 1 Van Wyck Street, Crotonon-Hudson, NY 10520.
On a motion by Trustee Gallelli, seconded by Trustee Simon, the Board voted unanimously to enter into
an executive session at 5:30 PM for discussions related to personnel.
The following officials were present:
Mayor Pugh
Trustee Gallelli
Trustee Horowitz
Trustee Murtaugh
Trustee Simon
Secretary to the Village Manager Bryan Healy
No action was taken.
On a motion by Trustee Murtaugh, seconded by Trustee Horowitz, the Board voted unanimously to
adjourn the Executive Session at 6:55 PM.
Submitted by,
Bryan Healy, Secretary to the Village Manager
