An Executive Session of the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, NY was held on
Monday, August 17, 2020 via Zoom videoconference, pursuant to Executive Order 202.1 issued by
Governor Andrew Cuomo on March 12, 2020.
On a motion by Trustee Gallelli, seconded by Trustee Horowitz, the Board voted unanimously to enter
into an executive session at 5:01 PM to discuss matters of personnel.
The following officials were present:
Mayor Pugh
Trustee Gallelli
Trustee Habib
Trustee Horowitz
Trustee Simmons
Village Manager Janine King
Chief of Police Russel Harper
No action was taken.
On a motion by Trustee Simmons, seconded by Trustee Horowitz, the Board voted unanimously to
adjourn the Executive Session at 5:57 PM.
Submitted by,
Janine King, Village Manager
