A Work Session of the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, NY was held on
Wednesday, December 6, 2023, at 7:00 PM in the Georgianna Grant Meeting Room of the Stanley H.
Kellerhouse Municipal Building.
The following officials were present:
Mayor Pugh
Trustee Murtaugh
Trustee Simon
Trustee Nicholson
Trustee Politi

Village Manager Healy

Systems, amending the Zoning Code to provide a regulatory scheme for Battery Energy Storage
Systems, and creating Guidelines for Trustee Liaisons, Board, and Committee Members.
