LOCAL LAW INTRODUCTORY NO. 5-2019
A LOCAL LAW TO REPEAL LOCAL LAW NUMBER 2 OF 2019 AND TO REPEAL
THE OVERRIDE OF THE TAX LEVY LIMIT ESTABLISHED IN GENERAL
MUNICIPAL LAW §3-c
Be it enacted by the Village of Croton-on-Hudson as follows:
SECTION 1: LEGISLATIVE INTENT: This Local Law shall be known as "Local Law
Number 5 of 2019". Il is the intent of this Local Law to repeal Local Law Number 2 of 2019, and
To thus reinstate the limits on Ihe amount of real property taxes that may be levied by the Village
of Croton-on-Hudson pursuant to General Municipal Law §3-c.
SECTION 2: EFFECT: This Local Law hereby thus repeals in its entirety said Local Law
Number 2 of 2019, and declares the same of no force or effect, and thus the Village of Crotonon-Hudson repeals the prior lax cap override local law ofthe Village of Croton-on-Hudson and
reinstates the tax levy limits imposed by General Municipal Law §3-e.
SECTION 3: SEVERABILITY: If any portion this Local Law, or the application thereof lo
any person, entity, or circumstance, shall be determined by any court or tribunal of competent
jurisdiction to be invalid or unenforceable, such determination shall be confined in its operation
to the invalid part hereof, or in ils application to sueh person, entity, or circumstance as is
directly involved in the controversy in which such determination shall have been rendered, and
the remainder ofthis Local Law shall not be impaired thereby and such determination shall not
be deemed or construed to apply to other persons, entities, or circumstances.
SECTION 4: EFFECTIVE DATE: This Local Law shall take effect immedialely.

On motion of TRUSTEE OLVER, seconded by TRUSTEE GALLELLl, the following resolution was
adopted by the Board ofTrustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, New York with the following vole:
5-0.
Resolution #57-2019
WHEREAS, the State Legislature and the Governor enacted legislation that establishes a "property tax
cap" on the amount that a local government's property tax levy can increase each year; and
WHEREAS, this law has been effective for local governments since 2012; and
WHEREAS, the state legislation provides for iocal governments to override the cap to protect the Village
from unforeseen financial circumstances; and
WHEREAS, Local Law No. 2 of 2019 to override the "property tax cap" law was adopted by the Village
Board ofTrustees at the January 22, 2019, Board Meeting; and
WHEREAS, the Village of Croton-on-Hudson is not anticipating adopting a budget exceeding the
"property tax cap" law enacted by State Legislature and would like to repeal Local Law No. 2 of 2019,
and
WHEREAS, a Public Hearing was held and closed on April 15, 2019,
NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED: that the Village Board ofTrustees hereby adopts Local Law
Introductory No. 4 of 2019 to repeal the prior tax cap override Local Law 2 of 2019 and reinstates the tax
levy limits imposed by General Municipal Law §3-c which upon adoption will become Local Law No, 5
of2019.
