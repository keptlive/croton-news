# Mt. Airy Road Subdivision Faces Pushback Over Environmental Concerns

Croton-on-Hudson's Zoning Board of Appeals heard concerns from neighbors regarding a proposed two-lot subdivision at 52 Mt. Airy Road, citing potential impacts on neighborhood character, tree removal, and stormwater runoff. The applicant is seeking area variances to subdivide the property, which is currently a single-family home, into two lots that would each be approximately 281 square feet short of the required 25,000 square feet in the RA25 Residence District.

Attorney Kory Salomone, representing the applicant 52 Mt Airy Rd, LLC, argued that the one-percent deficiency was not substantial and that the proposed lots were similar in size to others in the vicinity. He stated that a Stormwater Pollution Prevention Plan (SWPPP) was being prepared to address stormwater management and related environmental concerns. Project engineer Michael Mastrogiacomo estimated that 26 to 30 trees would need to be removed to grade the site and establish the building footprint for the new residence.

Several residents voiced opposition during the public hearing, including Claire Hilbert of 60 Mount Airy Road, who read a letter from neighbors expressing concerns about tree removal, steep slope disturbance, potential flooding, traffic safety, and wildlife habitat loss. Deborah Schpack of 16 King Street presented a letter signed by 45 residents also opposing the variances, citing worries about stormwater runoff, erosion, and the belief that the variances would allow intensified development on environmentally sensitive land.

The Zoning Board of Appeals has requested additional information from the applicant, including updated survey information, a steep slope analysis, architectural elevations and renderings of the proposed new residence, and clarification of grading and retaining wall details. A site visit will be scheduled once weather permits.

## What's Next
The public hearing for 52 Mt. Airy Road will remain open at a future Zoning Board of Appeals meeting, allowing for additional public comment.
