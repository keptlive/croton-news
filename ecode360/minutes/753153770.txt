Croton Police Advisory Committee
April 22, 2025, 6:30 pm meeting minutes
Attendance: Chief Nikitopoulos, Carolyn Whiting, Miles Kafka, Ian Murtaugh, Teri
Lukin, and Trustee Slippen.
Absent: Jim Miller, Marie Considine, Paige Jones.
1. Bicycle giveaway.
Croton Caring member Molly Lemeris joined the committee for this part of
the meeting.
PAC members signed up to assist with various work details for the event.
2. Transparency.
A resident asked Chair Whiting to discuss police policy for press releases
and transparency. Chair Whiting advised that the Police Department will
soon have a full review of all policies, and it would be best to wait until the
completion of that review to discuss this topic.
3. Police Reform Task Force Priorities.
As part of a yearly review the PAC discussed the status of various
recommendations.
4. The PAC will send a letter to the editor of the Gazette for Police Memorial
Week.
5. The PAC will assist the police at the Summerfest police table as PAC
members’ availability allows.
6. The PAC will not meet in June.
7. Meeting schedules. The PAC will investigate the possibility of reducing PAC
meetings to fewer meetings.
8. National Night Out is August 5th, time to be determined. PAC will assist the
PD as requested.
9. The Special Olympics Torch Run is May 19th, time to be determined.
10.The PAC will host a Town Hall on May 29 at 7 pm in the Georgianna Grant
meeting room. The event will be televised on the government channel and
via the village website. Residents will be encouraged to submit questions
