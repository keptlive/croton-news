The Following is the current and proposed By-Law amendment concerning PAFs as
discussed for Companies to approve/disapprove for the 3/26/24 Fire Council meeting:
CURRENT BY-LAW
10.6
PAFs which have completed High School will have no curfew. PAFs that are still in High School must follow the curfew
rules in Article 13.4 of these By-Laws.
Any PAF that has completed the minimum required firefighter training per Article 7 of these By-Laws may respond to
incidents and/or standbys at any time, so long as it does not interfere with school or falls on an evening or morning prior
to a school day.

10.6
PAFs which have completed High School will have no curfew. PAFs that are still in High School must follow the curfew
rules in Article 13.4 of these By-Laws, except where the PAF has submitted a written parental consent form allowing
said PAF to respond during curfew hours.
Any PAF that has completed the minimum required firefighter training per Article 7 of these By-Laws may respond to
incidents and/or standbys at any time, so long as it does not interfere with school or falls on an evening or morning prior
