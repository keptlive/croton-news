Resolution #194 of 2022
On motion of TRUSTEE HOROWITZ, seconded by TRUSTEE SIMON, the following
resolution was adopted by the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-onHudson, New York with a 4-0 vote.
WHEREAS the Village of Croton-on-Hudson has owned the parcel located at 41- 51
Maple Street (“the Property”) since 2008, and
WHEREAS the Property has not been used and is not needed for municipal purposes
and is therefore surplus property which the Village now desires to sell, and
WHEREAS the Village issued an RFP for the Property on September 21, 2020, and
received six proposals by November 16, 2020, and of those proposals, five were
considered to be compliant, and
WHEREAS on February 17, 2021, the Village Board approved a term sheet with
Regan Development Corporation for the sale and development of this property, and

3

Village Trustees Board Meeting
December 5, 2022
Page 4

WHEREAS on May 17, 2021, the Village Board as lead agency has adopted a
negative declaration under SEQRA in connection with the sale and development of
the property, and
WHEREAS the Village Board authorized the Village Manager to execute the Purchase
and Sale Agreement with Regan Development Corporation by Resolution #90-2021,
and the Purchase and Sale Agreement was executed on July 2, 2021 (the
“Agreement”), and
WHEREAS as anticipated by the Agreement, Regan Development Corporation is
obtaining New Homes Land Acquisition Funds from Westchester County which
requires that the Village sell the Property to Westchester County and thereafter
Westchester County will convey the property to Regan Development Corporation or
its assignees, and
WHEREAS the Agreement will be terminated as of the closing date and the Village
will need to enter into a new Purchase Agreement with Westchester County,
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED that the Village Board hereby authorizes the
Village Manager to terminate the Purchase and Sale Agreement with Regan
Development Corporation and execute the Purchase Agreement with Westchester
County, subject to the Village Attorney’s final approval, and
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Village Board hereby authorizes the Village
Manager to execute all documents necessary in connection with the closing of the
sale of the Property to Westchester County, subject to the Village Attorney’s final
approval.

Resolution #199 of 2022
On motion of TRUSTEE HOROWITZ, seconded by TRUSTEE GALLELLI, the
following resolution was adopted by the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton- onHudson, New York, with a 4-0 vote.
WHEREAS the Croton Trails Committee was established in 1992 in order to identify
existing, potential, and connecting trails, trail linkages, and explore other trail
concepts in the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, and
WHEREAS the Croton Trails Committee was initially a sub-committee of the Croton
Conservation Advisory Council and was later transitioned to a standalone
committee, and
WHEREAS the Croton Trail Committee now primarily helps to maintain the Jan
Wines Memorial Trail System, meeting every second Saturday to clean up targeted
sections of the trail system, and
WHEREAS considering that the Croton Trails Committee no longer meets at the
Municipal Building, Trails Committee Chairman Terrence McGarty has made a
formal request to the Village Board to approve the transition of the Croton Trails

Conservation Advisory Council, and
WHEREAS this request was reviewed by the Village Board at a regular meeting on

Croton Trails Committee as a sub-committee of Croton Conservation Advisory
Council.

authorizes the Village Manager to accept the proposal from DTS Provident Design
Engineering in the amount of $4,400; and

holiday party last Friday, tomorrow evening the Police Advisory

to discuss the inclusion of the arts in the Village’s upcoming plans for its
125 Anniversary events.

about all the wonderful events that have taken place in their Village.

interviewing candidates for the vacant seat on the Board and we hope to
