Fund Drive info received from T. Dinkler – total in account is $19,034.79.
LOSAP – W. Vlad reports there is an online / Zoom meeting with Penflex to discuss how
agencies can manage LOSAP awards due to COVID-19 pandemic this Thursday night for

Investigation Committee – M. Feminella asked the Council to vote on the information
presented regarding the Company Officers of TL44 in 2018, whether Carlton Sargeant
earned Ex-Captain status and whether Chris Caterino and Tom Kaplan earned their
Lieutenant credit.
M. Feminella asked the Council to accept the report of the committee regarding the
Company Officers of Hook & Ladder in 2018, whereas that Company is expected to
provide their own investigation as to whether their members were qualified or not and
report back to the Council. Motion: D. Vlad / 2nd R. Cusano to approve their time served
– CARRIED. Motion: D. Vlad / 2nd D. Bennett to approve committee’s recommendation
and report – CARRIED.
Fire Prevention Committee – T. Kaplan reports that the committee met and agreed that
due to ongoing public health concerns related to the COVID-19 pandemic, Fire Fair will
not be held in 2020. Similarly, no visits to CET will occur and the committee is working
on alternative ways to reach the public during Fire Prevention Week this year.
