Be it enacted by the Village, of Croton-on-Hudson as follows:
SECTION 1: LEGISLATIVE INTENT: This Local Law shall be known as "Local Law Number 2 of 2015". It is the intent
ofthis Local Law to repeal Local Law Number 1 of 2015, and to thus reinstate the limits on the amount of real ^
property taxes that may be levied by the Village of Croton-on-Hudson pursuant to General Municipal Law §3-c.
SECTION 2: EFFECT: This Local L.aw hereby thus repeals in its entirety said Local Law Number 1_ of 2015, and
declares the same of no force or effect, and thus the Village of Croton-on-Hudson repeals the prior tax cap override
local law ofthe Village of Croton-on-Hudson and reinstates the tax levy limits imposed by General Municipal Law §3cJ.
SECTION 3: SEVERABILITY: If any portion this Local Law, or the application thereof to any persbn, entity, or
circumstance, shall be determined by any court or tribunal of competent jurisdiction to be invalid or unenforceable,
such determination shall be confined in its operation to the invalid part hereof, or in its application to such person,
eritity, or circumstance as is directly involved in the controversy in which such determination shall have been
rendered, and the remainder of this Local Law shall not be impaired thereby and such determination shall not be
deemed or construed to apply.to other persons, entities, or circumstances.
SECTION 4: EFFECTIVE DATE: This Local Law shall take effect immediately.
