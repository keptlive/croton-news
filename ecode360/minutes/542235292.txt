An Executive Session of the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, NY was held on
Monday, April 27, 2020 via Zoom videoconference, pursuant to Executive Order 202.1 issued by
Governor Andrew Cuomo on March 12, 2020.
On a motion by Trustee Horowitz, seconded by Trustee Simmons, the Board voted unanimously to enter
into an executive session at 6:40 PM to discuss the proposed acquisition, sale or lease of real property on
Hollis Lane.
The following officials were present:
Mayor Pugh
Trustee Gallelli
Trustee Habib
Trustee Horowitz
Trustee Simmons
Village Manager Janine King
Village Clerk Paula DiSanto
Secretary to the Manager Bryan Healy
Village Treasurer Sandra Bullock
Village Engineer Daniel O’Connor
Village Attorney Linda Whitehead
No action was taken.
On a motion by Trustee Habib, seconded by Trustee Simmons, the Board voted unanimously to adjourn
the Executive Session at 7:00 PM.
