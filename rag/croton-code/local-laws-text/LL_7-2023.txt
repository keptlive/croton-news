VILLAGE OF CROTON-ON-HUDSON
BOARD OF TRUSTEES
LOCAL LAW NO. 07-2023
A LOCAL LAW TO AMEND CHAPTER 230 ZONING OF THE CODE OF
THE VILLAGE OF CROTON-ON-HUDSON TO MODIFY REQUIREMENT
FOR TIER 3 SOLAR SYSTEMS
Be it enacted by the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson as follows:
Section One. Section 230-48.1 Solar Energy Systems, Subsection G(3) is amended to add new
subsection (l) to read as follows:
§230-48.1.G(3)(l) Steep Slopes. Tier 3 solar energy systems shall not be permitted to be located on
areas of Steep Slopes as defined in Chapter 195.
Section Two. Section 230-48.1.G(3)(l) Conditions shall be renumbered as subsection (j).
Section Three. Severability
If any section, subsection, clause, phrase or other portion of this Local Law is, for any reason,
declared invalid, in whole or in part, by any court, agency, commission, legislative body or other
authority of competent jurisdiction, such portion shall be deemed a separate, distinct and independent
portion. Such declaration shall not affect the validity of the remaining portions hereof, which other
portions shall continue in full force and effect.
Section Four. Effective Date
This local law shall take effect immediately upon filing in the office of the New York State
Secretary of State in accordance with section 27 of the Municipal Home Rule Law.

On motion of TRUSTEE SIMON, seconded by TRUSTEE GALLELLI, the following resolution was adopted by the
Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, New York, with a 5-0 vote.
Resolution #89-2023
WHEREAS the Village of Croton-on-Hudson would like to amend Section 230-48.1 of the Zoning Chapter of the Village
Code to prohibit the construction of Tier 3 solar systems on steep slopes, and
WHEREAS the Village Board is considering the adoption of Local Law Introductory No. 4 of 2023 which has been
drafted for such purposes, and
WHEREAS on March 20, 2023, the Village Board determined that the Proposed Action is an Unlisted Action, declared
itself to be the Lead Agency for SEQRA purposes, and issued the Short Environmental Assessment Form (EAF) and a
Coastal Assessment Form (CAF) in connection with the Proposed Action, and
WHEREAS the Westchester County Planning Board, Village Planning Board, and the Waterfront Advisory Committee
have submitted memoranda in support of the local law, and
WHEREAS a public hearing was opened and closed on April 10, 2023, and
WHEREAS on April 24, 2023, the Board of Trustees issued a negative declaration under SEQRA and adopted a statement
of consistency with regards to the Village's LWRP,
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED that the Village Board of Trustees hereby adopts Local Law Introductory No.
4 of 2023 to amend Chapter 230, Zoning, of the Village Code to prohibit Tier 3 solar systems on steep slopes, which
upon adoption becomes Local Law No. 7 of 2023.
