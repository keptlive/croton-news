An Executive Session of the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, NY was held
on July 17, 2024, in the Georgianna Grant Meeting Room of the Stanley H. Kellerhouse Municipal
Building.
On a motion by Trustee Murtaugh, seconded by Trustee Simon, the Board voted unanimously to
enter into an executive session at 10:02 PM to discuss matters of personnel involving specific
individuals.
The following officials were present:
Mayor Pugh
Trustee Murtaugh
Trustee Nicholson
Trustee Politi
Trustee Simon
Village Manager Healy
No action was taken.
On a motion by Trustee Simon, seconded by Trustee Murtaugh, the Board voted unanimously to
adjourn the Executive Session at 10:25 PM.
Submitted by,
Bryan Healy, Village Manager
