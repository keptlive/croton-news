202.1 issued by Governor Andrew Cuomo on March 12, 2020.

The following resolution was adopted by the Board of Trustees:
On motion of TRUSTEE HABIB, seconded by TRUSTEE GALLELLI, the following resolution
was adopted by the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, New York with the
following roll call vote: Trustee Habib Aye, Trustee Simmons Aye, Trustee Gallelli Aye, Trustee
Horowitz Aye, Mayor Pugh Aye.
Resolution #152-2020
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED: that the Village Board of Trustees hereby exercises
the authority vested in Section 86-9(I), Building Construction, of the Village Code, to permit the
issuance of a building permit to the property owner of the parcel located at 63 Thompson
Avenue, designated on the tax maps as Section 79.09, Block 002, Lot 076; and
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED: that such issuance is contingent on the payment of the taxes
owed for 2017.
Also discussed at this work session was the proposed limitations on the use of gas-powered leaf
blowers by the Conservation Advisory Council.
Respectfully Submitted,
Bryan Healy, Secretary to the Village Manager
