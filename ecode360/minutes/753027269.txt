A Special Meeting of the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, NY was
held on Thursday, February 29, 2024 in the Georgianna Grant Meeting Room at the
Stanley Kellerhouse Municipal Building, Van Wyck Street, Croton-on-Hudson, NY
10520.
The following officials were present:
Mayor Pugh
Village Manager Bryan Healy

Trustee Murtaugh
Trustee Simon
Trustee Nicholson

The following official was absent:
Trustee Politi
Mayor Pugh called the meeting to order at 6:30pm.
On motion of Trustee Simon, and seconded by Trustee Murtaugh, the following
appointments were approved by the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton- onHudson, New York with a 4-0 vote.
Marlena Horton – IDEA Committee
Faith Rivers – IDEA Committee
Riley Moeller – Bicycle and Pedestrian Committee
There being no further business to come before the Board a motion to close the
special meeting and enter into executive session to discuss the proposed acquisition,
sale or lease of real property when publicity would substantially affect the value
thereof was made by Trustee Simon. The motion was seconded by Trustee Nicholson
and approved with a 4-0 vote. The meeting was adjourned at 6:32pm.
Respectfully Submitted,
Bryan Healy
Village Manager
