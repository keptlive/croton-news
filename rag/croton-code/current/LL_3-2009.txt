Section One. Section 172-2(A) of Chapter 172 of the Code of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson is amended by
adding new subparagraph (7) to read as follows:
Section 172-2(A)(7). Any person, entity or organization authorized by the Board of Trustees of the Village of
Croton-on-Hudson to conduct sales within any park or recreation area of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson.
Section Two. This local law shall take effect upon filing by the Secretary of State.

On motion of TRUSTEE Olver, seconded by TRUSTEE Restuccia, the following
resolution was unanimously adopted by the Board of Trustees of the Village of Crotonon-Hudson, New York:
WHEREAS, the Village Board of Trustees would like to exclude from applicability sales
in Village Parks and Recreations areas unless authorized by the Board; and,
WHEREAS, Local Law Introductory No. 3 of 2009 has been drafted amending Chapter
172 Section 2(A) ofthe Village Code adding subparagraph (7) allowing for sales in the
Villages Parks and Recreation areas by authorization of the Village Board, and
WHEREAS, a public hearing was held and closed on May 4, 2009,
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED: that the Village Board of Trustees hereby
adopts Local Law No. 3 of 2009, amending Chapter 172-2(A) ofthe Village Code.
AND BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED: that this local law shall take effect upon filing by
the Secretary of State.
