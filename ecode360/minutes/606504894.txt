An Executive Session of the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, NY was held on
Monday, May 17, 2021 via Zoom videoconference, pursuant to Executive Order 202.1 issued by
Governor Andrew Cuomo on March 12, 2020.
On a motion by Trustee Horowitz, seconded by Trustee Gallelli, the Board voted unanimously to enter
into an executive session at 5:30 PM for discussions regarding personnel.
The following officials were present:
Mayor Pugh
Trustee Gallelli
Trustee Horowitz
Trustee Murtaugh
Trustee Simon
Village Treasurer Sandra Bullock
No action was taken.
On a motion by Trustee Simon, seconded by Trustee Gallelli, the Board voted unanimously to adjourn the
Executive Session at 5:57 PM.
Submitted by,
Ann Gallelli, Deputy Mayor
