Reports of Committees

Fund Drive – T. Dinkler submitted a report, stating 2021 Fund Drive is active and has seen a
return of $13,604.00 (account has $13,564.60 due to fees, etc.).

LOSAP – The Village adopted the amendment to our LOSAP system for 2020 related to the
COVID Pandemic on 3/15/2021. A list of those who will receive the credit (47 points) will be
forwarded to the village in next 24-48 hours. A discussion followed. Motion J. Streany/2nd D.
Vlad that those that met parameters of a Member in Good Standing adopted in 2019 and are
Active or Active Life Members that completed all requirements (PESH training, Physical, Etc.)
will receive the points – CARRIED.

Parade – H. Lockwood reports he has bands contracted for the Montrose (VFW), Ossining
(Regulators) and Yorktown Parades (Yonkers Military Band).

By-Law – J. Streany requests a vote on the proposed changes previously sent to the Companies
for Article 2.1 to make the nomination cutoff from June to September. Motion J. Streany/2nd W.
Skiba to approve – CARRIED.

Motion by W. Skiba / 2nd J. Munson to approve the propolsed changes in Articles 2 & 3 to add
“Member in Good Standing” to all positions as previously sent to the companies for approval –
CARRIED.

Big Bucks – J. Munson reports the Village approved allowing us to have a Big Bucks Raffle this
year. A discussion followed and a motion by J. Munson/2nd M. Feminella to hold the raffle with
a drawing date of 10/9/2021 was CARRIED.

Discussion held regarding the proposed changes from Chemical Engine related to out-of-town
Chiefs. P. Dinkler directed By-Law committee to have a proposal ready at the next Fire Council
Meeting to send to the companies to discuss and vote upon.

Unfinished Business:

Discussion held regarding the proposed changes from Chemical Engine related to out-of-town

New Business:

J. Streany provided a By-Law change from WECO regarding Driver Training/Qualification for the
By-Law Committee to review.

J. Munson states that the department lacks a formal policy for firefighter physicals/return to

be provided for discussion at next Fire Council meeting.
