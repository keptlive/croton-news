APPROVED Minutes of Croton Fire Council Regular Meeting
Tuesday, December 26, 2023
Roll Call: J. Munson (221), J. Karpoff (487), C. Colombo (133), M. Feminella (162), J. Streany (275), D. Vlad
(318), B. Powers (474), K. Goldstein (576), G. Tucci (284)
Department Members: None
Others in Attendance: Village Manager Bryan Healy, Village Mayor Brian Pugh, Village Trustee Len Simon
Meeting Called to Order without the Pledge of Allegiance due to meeting at Tavern at Croton Landing at
19:04.
Special Orders of Business / Reports: Mayor Pugh thanked all for their service over the past year.
Manager Healy wished all a Happy New Year, reports some members eligible for Volunteer Tax Credit have not
submitted for it yet and have to do so by 1/2/24. Trustee Simon wished all a Happy New Year & Happy
Holidays, LOSAP update approved and Penflex approved as our LOSAP vendor again.
Approval of Previous Months Meeting’s Minutes:
Motion J. Karpoff / 2nd K. Russsell to approve November Fire Council Minutes – CARRIED.
Bills and Communications:
• Excuses received from E. Carlino, C. Sarcone, W. Skiba, T. Kaplan & Alt. J. Delaney for this evening’s
meeting – Motion D. Vlad / 2nd B. Powers to approve – CARRIED.
• Application for Active Membership with Harmon Engine Company received from James Pettit, a past
member. Motion D. Vlad/2nd J. Streany to approve pending favorable background check – CARRIED.
• Letter from Washington Engine indicating James Tipa is changing his status from Active to Social
Membership. Motion J. Streany / 2nd B. Powers to approve status change – CARRIED.
• Letter from Washington Engine indicating Bryce Laemmel has changed his status from a PAF to Active
Member due to reaching his 18th Birthday. Motion J. Streany / 2nd D. Vlad to approve – CARRIED.
• Letter from Columbian Hook & Ladder indicating their elected Officers for 2024.
