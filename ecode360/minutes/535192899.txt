An Executive Session of the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, NY was held on
Thursday, March 12, 2020 at the Stanley H. Kellerhouse Municipal Building, 1 Van Wyck Street, Crotonon-Hudson, NY 10520.
On a motion by Trustee Simmons, seconded by Trustee Horowitz, the Board voted unanimously to enter
into an executive session at 7:00 PM to discuss matters relating to the employment of a particular person.
The following officials were present:
Mayor Pugh
Trustee Gallelli
Trustee Horowitz
Trustee Simmons
The following official was absent:
Trustee Habib
No action was taken.
On a motion by Trustee Simmons, seconded by Trustee Horowitz, the Board voted unanimously to
adjourn the Executive Session at 9:27 PM.
