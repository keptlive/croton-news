4. PUBLIC HEARING:
A motion to open a Public Hearing to consider Local Law Introductory No. 18
of 2023 to amend Chapter 223, Water, of the Code of the Village of Croton on
Hudson to clarify the responsible party for curb valves and curb valve boxes
on private property was made by Trustee Simon. Motion was seconded by
Trustee Nicholson and approved with a 5-0 vote.
Village Manager Healy advised that these changes were recommended by the
Village Engineer and our Water Department Foreman. Village Manager Healy
explained that most curb valves and curb valve boxes are located in the

Village Board of Trustees
October 16, 2023
Page 2

Village’s public right-of-way, but we have found that there are properties
where these devices have been installed on private property and therefore
not the responsibility of the Village’s Water Department.
There being no further comments to come before the Board, a motion to close
the Public Hearing was made by Trustee Simon. Motion was seconded by
Trustee Nicholson and approved with a 5-0 vote.
Resolution 212 of 2023
On motion of TRUSTEE SIMON, seconded by TRUSTEE GALLELLI, the following resolution
was adopted by the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, New York with
a 5-0 vote.
WHEREAS the Village would like to amend Chapter 223, Water, of the Code of the Village
of Croton-on-Hudson to clarify the responsible party for curb valves and curb valve boxes
on private property, and
WHEREAS Local Law Introductory No. 18 of 2023 has been drafted for such purposes, and
WHEREAS a public hearing was opened and closed on October 16, 2023,
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED that the Village Board of Trustees hereby adopts Local
Law Introductory No. 18 of 2023 to amend Chapter 223, Water, of the Code of the
Village of Croton-on-Hudson, which upon adoption becomes Local Law No. 18 of 2023.
DISCUSSION:
Trustee Horowitz asked if those residents are aware that their curb valves and curb
valve boxes are on their private property.
Village Manager Healy stated that he does not believe we have an actual list, this is
something that gets discovered when a homeowner needs to have work done that
requires their water service to be turned off, but residents should be aware of the
location of the curb valve and curb valve box, and they can certainly reach out to the
Water Department.

Village Manager Healy explained that, per State Regulations and the Village’s
current Zoning Law, the business would be allowed to operate in this location
as it is not within five hundred feet of a school, two hundred feet of a house of
worship and is located within the C2 Zoning District. Village Manager Healy
stated that the Village has thirty days to provide comments.
Trustee Nicholson asked if this business would need to go before the Planning
Board.
Village Manager Healy stated that it would depend on what it was replacing, if
it were going from one retail use to another it would not need to go before the
Planning Board unless they were going to do some significant alterations to the
existing facility.

Village Board of Trustees

Page 5

Trustee Simon stated that the Cannabis Law governs certain things that we
cannot touch, but generally speaking our Zoning Laws and Business Regulations
would apply.
Trustee Nicholson asked when we should begin to submit our concerns to the
Regulatory Board since this is the first application in this area and they do
attract quite a lot of traffic.
Associate Village Attorney Taryn Ramey stated that any comments that the
Board submits to the Office of Cannabis Management will become part of the
applicant’s license record and considered by the “OCM.” Ms. Ramey additionally
stated that the review of traffic and the effect on the neighborhood and
pedestrians is already built into the process, but if the Board has concerns it is
important to put those concerns on record.
Mayor Pugh stated that since this is in a large private shopping complex the
owner of the complex really has discretion and power over the management of
parking, but the Board should include in our comments that a traffic/parking
management plan by the applicant and the property owner should be
performed.
Village Manager Healy stated that the areas that we are permitted to regulate
are the hours of operation, visual and architectural integrity of the building if
located in an historical district, parking, traffic control, odor, noise and distance
requirements between other dispensaries, micro businesses and public youth
facilities.
Mayor Pugh asked Village Manager Healy to prepare a draft of
comments/questions to share with the Board at our Work Session next week.

On motion of TRUSTEE HOROWITZ, seconded by TRUSTEE GALLELLI, the following
resolution was adopted by the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, New
York with a 5-0 vote.
WHEREAS the Village would like to amend and update the definition of family as stated in
Chapter 230 Zoning, of the Code of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, and
WHEREAS the Village Board is considering taking an action to adopt Local Law
Introductory No. 19 of 2023 which has been drafted for such purposes, and
WHEREAS the Village has completed Part 1 of a Short Environmental Assessment Form
(EAF) and a Coastal Assessment Form (CAF),
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Village Board hereby determines that the Proposed Action is an Unlisted
Action.

2. The Village Board hereby declares itself to be the Lead Agency for SEQRA
purposes in connection with the Proposed Action.

3. The Village Board hereby issues the EAF and CAF which it has before it in
connection with the Proposed Action.

4. The Village Board hereby refers to the Village Planning Board the Draft Law for a
report back to the Village Board in accordance with Village law.

5. The Village Board hereby refers the Draft Law, the EAF and the CAF to the
Village Waterfront Advisory Committee for a recommendation back to the
Village Board in accordance with Village Law.

6. The Village Board hereby refers the Draft Law, the EAF and the CAF the
Westchester County Planning Board for review in accordance with law.

7. The Village Board hereby directs Village staff to make any other circulations and
notifications regarding the Proposed Action as may be required by law.
