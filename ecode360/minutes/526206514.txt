An Executive Session of the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, NY was held on
Thursday, January 2, 2020 at the Stanley H. Kellerhouse Municipal Building, 1 Van Wyck Street, Crotonon-Hudson, NY 10520.
On a motion by Trustee Simmons, seconded by Trustee Habib, the Board voted unanimously to enter into
an executive session at 7:32 PM to discuss matters relating to the employment of a particular person.
The following officials were present:
Mayor Pugh
Trustee Gallelli
Trustee Habib
Trustee Horowitz
Trustee Simmons
Village Manager Janine King
Village Clerk Pauline DiSanto
No action was taken.
On a motion by Trustee Simmons, seconded by Trustee Habib, the Board voted unanimously to adjourn
the Executive Session at 8:03 PM.
