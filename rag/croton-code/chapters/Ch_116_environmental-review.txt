§ 116-1.              Additional Type II actions.


     [HISTORY: Adopted by the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson 3-20-2006 by
     L.L. No. 5-2006;47 amended in its entirety 11-7-2011 by L.L. No. 5-2011. Subsequent amendments
     noted where applicable.]


     § 116-1. Additional Type II actions. [Amended 4-9-2025 by L.L. No. 6-2025]

     Consistent with the New York State Environmental Quality Review Act (Article 8 of the Environmental
     Conservation Law), and the regulations promulgated pursuant to that statute (6 NYCRR Part 617,
     hereinafter "Part 617"), the Village of Croton-on-Hudson determines the actions listed in Attachment 1 of
     this chapter to be additional Type II actions. This list may be updated from time to time via resolution by
     the Village Board of Trustees.


     47. Editor's Note: This local law also repealed former Ch. 116, Environmental Quality Review, adopted 4-4-1977 by L.L. No. 7-1977, as
         amended.
