Croton Welcomes Everyone
BOT liaisons indicated the Village’s preference for business self-certification. All
agreed, and the web page content was revised accordingly. Village Manager
approval/disapproval is expected after Nov. 21.
Community Read
The subcommittee reported a challenge in finding an appropriate
speaker-facilitator for the village-wide reading on Disability rights and
experiences. The subcommittee will continue the search with the Croton Free
Library.
Village Information Access
The committee was informed of a new full-time Spanish-English speaker at the
Village Clerk’s office. The committee discussed the subcommittee’s workshop
proposal to increase access to Village website content. An informational video
was also favorably discussed.
Cultural Night
We heard a proposal to collaborate on Cultural Night with Croton Schools. The
discussion included concerns about feasibility due to village restraints. BOT
liaisons proposed the collaboration to Village Trustees, but the proposal was
declined.
