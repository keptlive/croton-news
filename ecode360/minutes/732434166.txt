The following resolution was adopted by the Board of Trustees at the work session:
On motion of TRUSTEE SIMON, seconded by TRUSTEE HOROWITZ, the following resolution
was adopted by the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, New York, with a 5-0
vote:
Resolution # 124-2023
WHEREAS, the Village of Croton-on-Hudson has been working with Sol Systems of Washington,
DC, on the installation of solar canopies at the Croton-Harmon Train Station parking lot; and
WHEREAS, on February 17, 2021, the Village Board of Trustees approved a lease agreement with
Sol Systems for this project; and
WHEREAS, Sol Systems has provided an amendment to the existing lease agreement to cover the
installation of a stormwater management drainage system for the solar canopies,
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED: that the Village Manager is hereby authorized to execute
an amendment to the lease agreement with Sol Systems of Washington, DC, for the construction
and installation of a stormwater management drainage system for the solar canopies at the CrotonHarmon Train Station parking lot, subject to the final approval of the Village Attorney.
