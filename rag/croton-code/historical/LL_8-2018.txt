Section Two. A new Section 3 of Chapter 123 entitled "Exemptions" is hereby added to the Code of the
Village of Croton-on-Hudson. Jt shall read as follows:

§ 123-3. Exemptions.
This chapter shall not apply to:
A. The authorized use of a pistol, rifle or target range regularly operated and maintained by a law
enforcement agency.

B. Law enforcement officers in the discharge of their duties or other persons in the defense of person
or property.
