An Executive Session of the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, NY was held on
Wednesday, December 30, 2020 via Zoom videoconference, pursuant to Executive Order 202.1 issued by
Governor Andrew Cuomo on March 12, 2020.
On a motion by Trustee Habib, seconded by Trustee Gallelli, the Board voted unanimously to enter into
an executive session at 11:30 AM for discussions regarding the proposed acquisition, sale or lease of real
property.
The following officials were present:
Mayor Pugh
Trustee Gallelli
Trustee Habib
Trustee Horowitz
Trustee Simon
Village Manager Janine King
Village Attorney Linda Whitehead
Secretary to the Village Manager Bryan Healy
Four invited representatives also attended the executive session. No action was taken.
On a motion by Trustee Habib, seconded by Trustee Simon, the Board voted unanimously to adjourn the
Executive Session at 1:00 PM.
Submitted by,
Bryan Healy, Secretary to the Village Manager
