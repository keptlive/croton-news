# Fire Department to Get Sprinklers During EMS Annex Construction
### The fire chief announced that the entire building will be upgraded with sprinklers as part of the EMS annex project.

The Croton-on-Hudson Fire Council met on November 26, 2024, at the Harmon Firehouse. Mayor Pugh informed the council that the EMS annex is expected to be completed in the summer of 2025. In addition, Fire Chief Karpoff announced that the entire building would be getting sprinklers as part of the upgrades.

The meeting also included a moment of silence in honor of departed members, especially Ex-Captain Ralph Croteau Sr., and Jimmy Winters, who recently passed away.

# Fire Department Approves New Members, Addresses Training Compliance
### The Croton-on-Hudson Fire Department accepted new probationary members and discussed training requirements at its November meeting.

At the November 26th meeting, the Fire Council approved several new probationary active members for the Ladder Company. Christian Gaona, Bennett Turner, Alexander Jones, and Brothi Portnoy were all accepted. The council also noted that all interior members must requalify annually for bailout training.

Chief Karpoff expressed concern over low sign-up numbers for the training and noted that captains should follow up with their members to ensure they are up to date. He also mentioned that annual compliance training and bailout training for 2025 are already being planned to avoid the disorganization of this year.

# Fire Department to Limit Spending on Holiday Events
### The Croton-on-Hudson Fire Department will limit spending on the Candy Cane Run and holiday party.

At the November 26th meeting, the Fire Council voted to limit the budget for the annual Candy Cane Run to $1,000. A separate motion also passed to limit the holiday party budget to $6,500. The date for the Holiday Party at the Cortlandt Yacht Club is still to be determined.

The council also discussed and approved a motion to allow members to run for multiple officer positions, provided they meet the requirements of those positions.
