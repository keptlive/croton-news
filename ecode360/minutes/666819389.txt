Resolution:
On motion of TRUSTEE Horowitz, seconded by TRUSTEE Simon, the following resolution was
unanimously adopted by the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, New York:
Resolution #77-2022
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED: that upon the recommendation of the Village
Manager, the Board of Trustees hereby approves the Memorandum of Agreement between the
Village of Croton-on-Hudson and Local 456, International Brotherhood of Teamsters, covering
the period June 1, 2022 – May 31, 2027, and authorizes the expenditure of funds consistent
therewith; and
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED: that the Village Manager is authorized to execute a collective
bargaining agreement covering the period from June 1, 2022, through May 31, 2027, that is
consistent with the terms of the Memorandum of Agreement.
