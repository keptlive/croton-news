An Executive Session of the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, NY was
held on Monday, August 22, 2022, in the Georgianna Grant Meeting Room of the Stanley H.
Kellerhouse Municipal Building.
On a motion by Trustee Horowitz, seconded by Trustee Gallelli, the Board voted unanimously to
enter into an executive session at 6:47 PM to discuss the proposed acquisition, sale or lease of
real property.
The following officials were present:
Mayor Pugh
Trustee Gallelli
Trustee Horowitz
Trustee Rosales
Trustee Simon
Village Manager Healy
Village Attorney Whitehead
Village Engineer O’Connor
No action was taken.
On a motion by Trustee Rosales, seconded by Trustee Horowitz, the Board voted unanimously to
adjourn the Executive Session at 7:18 PM.
Submitted by,
Bryan Healy, Village Manager
