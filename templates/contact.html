Corrections

If you believe an article contains a factual error, please email editor@croton.news with:

  • The article URL
  • The specific statement you believe is incorrect
  • The correct information and your source

We investigate all correction requests and publish corrections promptly. See our editorial policy for details.

Tips

Have a story tip about Croton village government? We're especially interested in:

  • Documents or records that shed light on government decisions
  • Context that helps explain what happened at a public meeting
  • Upcoming meetings or hearings the public should know about

For Officials

Village officials, board members, and committee chairs are welcome to submit clarifications or additional context at any time. We will note official responses in our coverage.
