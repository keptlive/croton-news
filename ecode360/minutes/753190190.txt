Recreation Advisory Committee Meeting
Monday, May 12, 2025
Present:

Debi Braddick, Emily Boglioli, John Sasso, Frances Catanio,
Pete Sedlmair, Nora Nickelson, Bryan Healy, John Bouchard
and Mark Duncan.

Meeting Called to Order: The meeting opened at 7:00 p.m.
Approval of Minutes: The April minutes were approved by Debi.
Dobbs Park Comments from Residents: The Recreation Advisory
Committee looked over the 60 plus comments submitted by residents
regarding the new Dobbs Park Playground. Christina Alvarez-Arnold
handed out suggestions for accessibility standards and engagement
equipment. The committee agreed that these suggestions were also
important.
Ideas For Dobbs: The Recreation Advisory Committee shared a few ideas
for the new playground at Dobbs Park. One of them was to add some
challenging equipment to the playground. Another idea that was
recommended was to add some additional benches facing the playground
area.
Request & Proposal to the Heart Committee: The Recreation Advisory
proposed the “Pocket Park” be named after John Giglio prior to the Heart
Committee informing them that the designated person must be someone that
has been deceased for at least 5 years. Debi will write a memo to the Village
Board in favor of the park being named after John Giglio.
Radnor Avenue Parcel for Potential Veterans Monument: The Recreation
Advisory Committee identified a parcel of land on Radnor that possibly
could be used for the Veterans. They suggested a small monument and
benches for reflection for the Radnor Avenue parcel. They feel it would be a
nice dedication and a way to thank the Veterans for their service.
Adjournment: The meeting was adjourned at 8:00 p.m.
The next regular meeting is scheduled for Monday, June 9th @ 7:00 p.m.
