3. PUBLIC HEARING
Motion to open a Public Hearing to hear comments on Local Law
Introductory No. 8 of 2021 to amend the Zoning Chapter of the Village
Code, Section 230, to eliminate the prohibition against video games and
arcade machines, update notification procedures for sheds and update
penalties for offenses was made by Trustee Horowitz.
Motion was
seconded by Trustee Gallelli and approved with a 5-0 vote.
Adan Decker, 13 Ackerman Court, Croton on Hudson, stated that he
supports repealing the current Arcade Law, this is a terrible law and
everyone in the Village that he has spoken with seems to agree with him.
Dr. Hans Tokke, 124 Grand Street, Croton on Hudson, Co-Owner of
Croton Corners stated that he supports the amendment and gave a

Croton EMS should receive a new Ambulance.
While the call volume in our village has started to increase, not having an
additional unit is starting to take a toll on our mutual aid agencies. Last
night at approximately 11pm Croton EMS was dispatched to three calls
within thirty minutes of each other, two of which went out just minutes
apart, being down one ambulance makes us more reliant on our mutual
aid partners and we feel that we should be self-sufficient. Adding this new
ambulance to replace an aging one will only help us serve our community
better, less out of service time, less money required in maintenance, a
great resource for our community, as well as agency pride and boosting
moral. As we have seen COVID come into our community over the last
two years it has taken a toll on not just us but our ambulances as well.
Earlier this year we spoke on how the increase of calls has grown
significantly. Croton EMS is looking at a call breaking year at almost
1,000 calls of service for the year, our previous call volume pre-COVID
was around 650-725 and a second reliable ambulance will only help us
maintain our goal in providing the best care to our community members.

separating from the Fire Department in 2008, and
WHEREAS Croton EMS maintains two ambulances to provide emergency medical
care to residents of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, and
WHEREAS these ambulances are also contractually bound to serve residents of the
Mount Airy-Quaker Bridge Fire Protection District within the Town of Cortlandt; and

WHEREAS Ambulance 55B1, a 2010 Ford, is currently out of service due to multiple

WHEREAS the Village Mechanic has advised that these issues will be costly and
time-consuming to fix, and
WHEREAS the Village is currently slated to replace Ambulance 55B1 next year as
part of its capital plan, and
WHEREAS Croton EMS has ascertained a price quote for a replacement demo
ambulance in the amount of $194,154, and
WHEREAS the vendor, VCI Emergency Vehicle Specialists of Holbrook, New York,
has offered to pay $10,000 for the current Ambulance 55B1,
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED: that the Village Board of Trustees hereby
declares the current situation involving Croton EMS and the ability to provide
quality emergency care to Village residents to be an emergency under New York
State GML § 103(4); and
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED: the Village Manager is authorized to accept the
proposal from VCI Emergency Vehicle Specialists for a replacement ambulance in
the amount of $194,154,
AND BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED: that the Village Treasurer is authorized to transfer
$184,154 from Contingent Account A1990.4000 to A4540.2010,
AND BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED: that the payment of $184,154 shall be charged to
A4540.2010: EMS - Vehicles.

Trustee Gallelli stated that based on our mechanic’s assessment of the condition of
the existing ambulance, this makes the most sense.
Trustee Simon stated that we received ten/eleven years from this ambulance which
is not too far from the average and the fact that we can get some revenue on the
trade-in is beneficial.
