An Executive Session of the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, NY was held on
Monday, September 21, 2020 via Zoom videoconference, pursuant to Executive Order 202.1 issued by
Governor Andrew Cuomo on March 12, 2020.
On a motion by Trustee Habib, seconded by Trustee Simmons, the Board voted unanimously to enter into
an executive session at 5:31 PM for discussions regarding proposed, pending or current legislation.
The following officials were present:
Mayor Pugh
Trustee Gallelli
Trustee Habib
Trustee Horowitz
Trustee Simmons
Village Manager Janine King
Village Attorney Linda Whitehead
Village Engineer Daniel O’Connor
Secretary to the Village Manager Bryan Healy
No action was taken.
On a motion by Trustee Habib, seconded by Trustee Horowitz, the Board voted unanimously to adjourn
the Executive Session at 6:07 PM.
Submitted by,
Bryan Healy, Secretary to the Village Manager
