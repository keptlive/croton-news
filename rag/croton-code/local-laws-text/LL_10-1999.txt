Croton on Hudson
Village
Local Law No. 10 of the year 1999.

A local law repealing Local Law #1 of 1999 which made certain amendments to the
Zoning Law concerning nonconforming uses and special permits.
Be It enacted by the Board of Trustees
(Name of Legislative Body)

of the Village as follows:

Section 1. Local Law #1 of 1999 is hereby repealed in its entirety.

Section 2. This local law shall become effective upon filing in
the office of the Secretary of State.
