A Work Session of the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, NY was held on
Wednesday, March 19, 2025, at 7:00 PM in the Georgianna Grant Meeting Room of the Stanley H.
Kellerhouse Municipal Building.
The following officials were present:
Mayor Pugh
Trustee Slippen
Trustee Simon
Trustee Nicholson

Trustee Politi
Village Manager Bryan Healy
Village Attorney Joshua B. Subin

On motion of TRUSTEE SIMON, seconded by TRUSTEE NICHOLSON, the following resolution
was adopted by the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, New York, with a 5-0
vote:
Resolution #56-2025
WHEREAS, the Village and the New York State Department of Transportation (NYSDOT) have
entered into a snow and ice agreement which ended on June 30, 2024; and
WHEREAS, the Village has received amendments to this agreement reflecting the severity of the
winter for the 2022-2023, 2023-2024 and 2024-2025 seasons; and
WHEREAS, the Village Board adopted Resolution 34-2025 to approve these agreements on
February 26, 2025; and
WHEREAS, the NYSDOT has now notified the Village that the amounts listed on the 2023-2024
and 2024-2025 agreements should have been higher due to an increase in the equipment allowance,
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED: that the Board of Trustees hereby authorizes the Village
Manager to execute the amended Municipal Snow and Ice Agreement Adjustments with the New
York State Department of Transportation for the years 2023-2024 and 2024-2025.
The Board heard a presentation from Superintendent of Public Frank Balbi regarding stormwater
management, reviewed the requirements to undertake the Mayor’s Monarch Pledge and to become
a Bee City, reviewed proposed changes to Chapter 208, Trees, of the Village Code, continued the
discussion on Local Law Introductory No. 14 of 2024 to consolidate assessment with the Town of
Cortlandt, discussed revisions to the uses which require a special permit in the Zoning Code, and
reviewed the Board’s list of 2025 Legislative Priorities. A recording of the work session may be
viewed by visiting the Village website.
Respectfully Summitted,
Bryan Healy, Village Manager
