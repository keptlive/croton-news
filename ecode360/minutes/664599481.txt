Unfinished Business:
J. Munson requests permission to order badge. Motion J. Karpoff/2nd G. Tucci to order badges
– CARRIED.
W. Skiba asked about recent Mutual Aid Member applications. J. Munson stated NYS Law
requires a member to work or live in one jurisdiction and to live or work in the other, and the
two requestors do neither of these for the other department in question. J. Munson also states
that a “occasional” ride-along status can be granted, too, however the one member whom
made the request has conducted himself inappropriately and has reportedly been riding with
the other department without permission, therefore he will not receive permission to do as he
requested.
Special Business: None
New Business:
J. Streany states that WECO has suggested the Council reconsider the By-Laws applicable to
Chiefs and Officers so that former officers can fill ranks once again. A discussion followed.
J. Streany also states that the department should look at it’s officer structure and consider
changing it. A discussion followed and a follow-up correspondence to membership will be
forthcoming.
