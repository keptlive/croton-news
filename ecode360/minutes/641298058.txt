An Executive Session of the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, NY was held on
Monday, January 10, 2022 at 9:25 PM via Zoom Conference call in accordance with the Chapter 417 of
the Laws of 2021 in light of the public health and safety concerns associated with the COVID-19
pandemic.
On a motion by Trustee Simon, seconded by Trustee Gallelli, the Board voted unanimously to enter into
an executive session at 9:25 PM to discuss matters of personnel.
The following officials were present:
Mayor Pugh
Trustee Horowitz
Trustee Simon
Village Clerk DiSanto

Trustee Gallelli
Trustee Rosales
Village Manager Healy
Asst. to Village Manager Morzello

Five invited guests joined the executive session. No action was taken.
On a motion by Trustee Simon, seconded by Trustee Gallelli, the Board voted unanimously to adjourn the
Executive Session at 9:45 PM.
Respectfully submitted by,
Thomas C. Morzello, Assistant to the Village Manager
