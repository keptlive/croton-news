Village Treasurer Tucker explained the following:

4
5
6
7

Tax Levy - is the amount established when a Village adopts its Budget. The tax cap
is a cap on the increase of that Tax Levy based on the previous year’s Levy, this is
also based on the State’s assigned growth rate based on the growth of the
Assessment Roll as well as an inflation factor.

8
9
10
11
12
13
14
15
16

Assessment Roll - is the Roll of all the parcels in the Village, each property is
assessed according to the market value, the State has guidelines on how
assessments are done, but a level of discretion is given to every Assessor in
preparing an accurate assessed value on a piece of property, the roll is updated
every year based on building permits, new construction, or demolition, the Village
publishes a Tentative Assessment Roll on February 1st, after the Roll is published a
Grievance Day is held for property owners to grieve/contest the assessed value of
their property, Village’s Final Assessment Roll is published on April 1st and that Roll
is used in tandem with the Village Budget to produce the Village’s Tax Bill.

17
18

Tax Rate - is determined once a Budget is established, once the Final Assessment
Roll is established a taxable value is established for each parcel in the Village.

19
20
21
22
23
24
25

Village Treasurer Tucker emphasized that this proposal is not a Revaluation, a
Revaluation is when an Assessing Unit attempts to overhaul the entire Assessment
Roll and bring all properties closer to market values, the last time a Revaluation
was done was in 1967. Village Treasurer Tucker stated that what this Local Law is
proposing is that the Village would no longer be an Assessing Unit and would use
the Town of Cortlandt’s Assessment Roll. Village Treasurer Tucker stated that
Senior, Veterans, and Star Exemptions would continue to exist.
