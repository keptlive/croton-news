Section One. Section 90-1 1, Establishment of Lien, of Chapter 90, Buildings, Unsafe, of the

§90-11. Establishment ofLien.
If such owner or persons served with the notice provided for in § 90-3 above shall fail
to py the costs and expenses incurreð by the village within 30 days after being notifieð
