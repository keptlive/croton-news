Village of Croton-on-Hudson
Local Law No. 3 of the year 2015

A local law to amend Chapters 72 and 168

Be it enacted by the Board of Trustees of the
Village of Croton-on-Hudson as follows:

Section 1. Chapter 72, Section 3, Letter D(5) of the Code of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson is hereby
amended to read as follows:
D(5). The parks and playgrounds of the Village of Croton-on-Hudsoh during the times and
in accordance with the rules and regulations prepared by the Village Manager or duly
designated representative, approved by the Village Board and filed with the Village Manager.

Sectiori 2. Chapter 168, Section 1 of the Code of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson is hereby amended to
read as follows:
1. Any park, playground, beach or other recreation area of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson
shall be open for use only in accordance with the schedule of hours posted on the facility in
question and as filed in the office of the Recreation Department. The yillage Manager or his
duly designated representative may prescribe rules for the use of any such area between such
hours as he may deem proper on application by groups and individuals pursuant to permits
granted in conforhiance with the rules prescribed.
Section 3. Chapter 168, Section 3 of the Code of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson is hereby amended to
read as follows:
i
3. No person, except pursuant to a permit as aforesaid or except a village official on village
business, shall occupy, use or be present iri or upon any such park, playground, beach or
other such recreation area at any time other than those prescribed in the schedule filed in the
office of the Recreation Department or posted at the facility. Any use, presence of
occupancy without the hours so listed shall constitute a violation of this article.

Sectiori 3. This Locial Law shall take effect iniinediately upon filing with the Secretary of State.

On motion of TRUSTEE GALLELLI, seconded by TRUSTEE SLIPPEN, the following
resolution was adopted unariimously by the Bbard of Trustees of the Village of Crotonon-Hudson, New York; with a 4-0 vote (Trustee Levitt not present at the time of this
vote)
WHEREAS, the Village would like to amend chapters 72 and 168 of the Village Code to
reflect language consistent with current staffing; and
,
WHEREAS, Local Law Introductory No. 3 of 2015 has been drafted to reflect such
language; and
'
WHEREAS, a Public Hearing was held and closed on June 1, 2015,
NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED: that the Village Board of Trustees hereby
adopts Local Law Introductory No. 3 of 2015 to amend chapters 72 and 168 of the
Village Code to reflect language consistent with Current staffing, which upon adoption
will become Local Law No. 3 pf 2015.
