A Work Session of the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, NY was held on
Monday, November 20, 2023, at 7:00 PM in the Georgianna Grant Meeting Room of the Stanley H.
Kellerhouse Municipal Building.
The following officials were present:
Mayor Pugh
Trustee Horowitz
Trustee Simon
Trustee Nicholson
Trustee Murtaugh

Village Manager Healy
Emily Mancini, Assistant to the
Village Manager

Mayor Pugh announced that Trustee Ann Gallelli has resigned from the Village of Croton-on-Hudson
Board of Trustees due to health reasons, and that he has appointed Ian Murtaugh to fill the remainder of
her term, which will expire in December of 2024. Trustee Murtaugh took the oath of office with the
Village Clerk on November 20, 2023.
Discussed at the work session was future recreational uses and opportunities at Gouveia Park, rules and
regulations governing the use of Village Parks and Facilities, residential parking near the Croton River,
and utilizing the Village shuttle bus for a local holiday shopping event.
On motion of TRUSTEE SIMON, seconded by TRUSTEE NICHOLSON, the Village Board moved to
refer a model tree ordinance provided by Westchester County to the Conservation Advisory Council for
review and comment, with a vote of 5-0.
Respectfully Summitted,
Emily Mancini, Assistant to the Village Manager
