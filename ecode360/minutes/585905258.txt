An Executive Session of the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, NY was held on
Tuesday, January 19, 2021 via Zoom videoconference, pursuant to Executive Order 202.1 issued by
Governor Andrew Cuomo on March 12, 2020.
On a motion by Trustee Gallelli, seconded by Trustee Simon, the Board voted unanimously to enter into
an executive session at 5:30 PM for discussions regarding the proposed acquisition, sale or lease of real
property.
The following officials were present:
Mayor Pugh
Trustee Gallelli
Trustee Habib
Trustee Horowitz
Trustee Simon
Village Manager Janine King
Village Attorney Linda Whitehead
Secretary to the Village Manager Bryan Healy
No action was taken.
On a motion by Trustee Horowitz, seconded by Trustee Habib, the Board voted unanimously to adjourn
the Executive Session at 5:57 PM.
Submitted by,
Bryan Healy, Secretary to the Village Manager
