# Croton Bike Share Program Eyes Expansion, Celebrates Successful Year
Croton-on-Hudson's Project MOVER bike share program is considering new hub locations and planning a spring group ride to celebrate a successful year.

Croton's Bicycle and Pedestrian Committee heard that the village was the top municipality and top hub for the regional bike share program in October. According to meeting minutes from November 20, 2025, program managers are eager to work with the village on hub expansion.

Potential new locations include Sinasqua Park and North Riverside Avenue near the footbridge. The committee also suggested considering a location in the northern part of the village, possibly near Food Town.

The bike share program will enter a "hibernation" period starting December 19th and is slated to reopen the first week of April. To celebrate, the committee discussed organizing a group ride to Croton Point Park or another location, with all bikes rented for the event.

As of the meeting, 10% of bike share vouchers have been used by Croton-based riders, with 369 vouchers still available.

# Croton to Explore "Slow Down" Campaign Expansion
The Croton-on-Hudson Bicycle and Pedestrian Committee is looking for ways to expand its "Slow Down Croton" awareness campaign.

At their November 20, 2025 meeting, committee members discussed ideas and proposals to further expand the campaign. The topic will be added to the agenda for their January meeting.

# Croton Schools Plan "Walk, Ride, and Roll to School" Day
Croton-Harmon School District is planning a "Walk, Ride, and Roll to School" day in Spring 2026 at Croton Elementary School (CET) and Pierre Van Cortlandt Middle School (PVC).

According to the November 20, 2025 meeting minutes of the Croton-on-Hudson Bicycle and Pedestrian Committee, the committee received a message from Anamika, the Board of Education liaison, regarding the proposed event. A bike rodeo is also planned for CET. The committee will follow up with Anamika on next steps and add the item to their January agenda.
