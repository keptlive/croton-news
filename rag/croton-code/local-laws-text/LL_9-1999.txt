Local Law No. 9 of the year 1999

A local law amending the zoning designations of certain
parcels of land within the Village of Croton on Hudson

Be It enacted by

the

Board of Trustees
(Name of Legislative Body)

of the

County

of Westchester

Village

Section 1. The 1979 Village of Croton on Hudson Zoning Law is
hereby amended as follows:
By changing from Two-Family Residence RB District to
Limited Office O-1 District Section 79, Subsection 5,
Block 5, Lot 1 as shown on the Tax Maps and Tax Assessment
Rolls of the Village.
Section 2. The Official Zoning Map of the Village of Croton on Hudson, NY,
is hereby amended to reflect the foregoing changes.
Section 3. This local law shall take effect immediately upon filing
with the Secretary of State.
