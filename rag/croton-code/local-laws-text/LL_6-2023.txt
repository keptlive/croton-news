A LOCAL LAW TO AMEND CHAPTER 215 OF THE CODE OF THE VILLAGE OF
CROTON-ON-HUDSON REGARDING SPEED LIMITS
Be it enacted by the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson as follows:
Section One. Chapter 215, Vehicles and Traffic, of the Code of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson
is hereby amended to read as follows:
§ 215-5 Speed Limits.
The maximum speed at which vehicles may proceed on or along any streets or highways within
the Village is hereby established at 25 miles per hour, except that the speed limit for vehicles
proceeding on or along those streets or parts of streets described in Schedule II (§ 215-50) shall
be as indicated in said schedule.
Section Two. Chapter 215, Vehicles and Traffic, of the Code of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson
is hereby amended to read as follows:
§ 215-50 Schedule II: Speed Limits.
In accordance with the provisions of § 215-5, speed limits other than 25 miles per hour are
established as indicated upon the following streets or parts of streets:

On motion of TRUSTEE SIMON seconded by TRUSTEE HOROWITZ, the following resolution
was adopted by the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, New York with a 4-1
vote. Trustee Horowitz voting Nay.
Resolution #83 -2023
WHEREAS the New York State Legislature recently enacted legislation permitting Villages to
lower their Village-wide speed limit from 30 MPH to 25 MPH, and
WHEREAS the Bicycle-Pedestrian Committee has studied this legislation and is supportive of
making such a change for the Village, and
WHEREAS the legislation requires that the Village undertake a traffic engineering study before
enacting such a change, and
WHEREAS DTS Provident Design Engineering, LLP, a licensed Professional Engineering firm in
the State of New York, has conducted a detailed Traffic Engineering Study which makes
recommendations and provides support for the lowering of the Village-wide speed limit from 30
MPH to 25 MPH, and
WHEREAS Local Law Introductory No. 7 of 2023 has been drafted for such purposes, and
WHEREAS a Public Hearing was opened and closed on April 24, 2023,
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED that the Village Board of Trustees hereby adopts Local
Law Introductory No. 7 of 2023 to amend Chapter 215 of the Village Code, Vehicles and Traffic,
regarding Speed Limits, which upon adoption becomes Local Law No. 6 of 2023.
