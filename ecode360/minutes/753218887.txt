DRAFT COMMITTEE GUIDELINES
John shared draft committee guidelines under consideration by the Board of Trustees and invited
comments to relay back to the Board.
Members and liaisons discussed the draft guidelines, including:
•
•
•
•
•

The proposed role of the Village Manager as a centralized repository for committee
correspondence and recommendations, and how that interacts with the liaison system
and agenda setting for Board meetings.
Expectations around meeting minutes, including the distinction between minutes and
transcripts, and the practical limits on volunteer time.
Tone and framing of administrative compliance language, including concern that certain
wording may read as punitive rather than appreciative of volunteer service.
A request that the guidelines reflect reciprocity and respect for volunteer work, including
language indicating that recommendations will be reviewed and seriously considered,
while not promising adoption.
A discussion of the proposed limitation on serving on more than one committee, including
a suggested exception when committees have unfilled seats, and a suggestion to include
a future reevaluation provision.

Mayor Pugh noted that the centralized correspondence approach reflects a trustee preference for
consistent information flow and records, and that formal staff support is typically associated with
statutory bodies such as the Planning Board and Zoning Board of Appeals due to procedural and
legal considerations. Mayor Pugh agreed to bring the feedback back to the Board.

EARTH DAY 2026
The Council continued its discussion regarding the format, location, and date for the 2026 Earth
Day event, noting the need to decide soon in order to preserve venue availability and establish a
rain date.
Key points included:
•
•

Members discussed returning the event to Vassallo Park to reduce logistical burdens and
parking constraints, and to better align with a refreshed, more focused mission for the
event.
Members discussed timing, including proximity to Earth Day and potential overlap with a
neighboring community event, and the potential benefits of a slightly later date for
planning and weather.

•

The Council reached consensus to aim for Saturday, May 2nd, 2026 at Vassallo Park,
with Saturday, May 9th, 2026 as the rain date.
