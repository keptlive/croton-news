Reports of the Chiefs
P. Dinkler (2081)
Thanks to everyone for supporting him another year and he looks forward to leading the
department once again. Reminder to all that COVID is still a risk and all safety precautions
need to be followed. Thanked A. Conti, H. Lockwood & J. O’Leary for their many years on the
Fire Council.
J. Munson (2082)
Thanks to everyone for their continued support and commitment to the department. Apparatus
repairs to E119, R18 and T10 in progress or recently completed. Committee for new E118
reports progress is being made on new apparatus. Members must remember to perform
“Doorway Triage” at calls to make sure there is no known COVID risk prior to entering a scene.
J. Karpoff (2083)
Working with DPW to repair/replace exterior lights at Harmon. Awaiting replacement of a
broken bay door controller button for TL44’s bay and for E119’s bay (button has been broken
10+ years). DES is offering minimal training classes and those offered are filling up fast so sign
up as soon as you can if interested in a class. An electric vehicle online class is being offered,
check emails.
Question from D. Vlad regarding freezer issue at HFH and addressing ongoing lock and door
issues along exterior of Harmon Fire House. Question from J. Streany about status of iPads and
PCs for use of IAR – Chief Karpoff reports a full replacement/upgrade for all is in the works.
Question from W. Vlad about missing iPad for E120, Chiefs will look into it.
Reports of Committees
BY-LAWS Proposal from Chemical Engine to allow out-of-district Chief Officers – discussion
followed. Proposal from Columbian H&L to modify wording for Articles 2 & 3 to include “Must
be in Good Standing.” Both are attached for Companies to review and provide input.
LOSAP T. Dinkler reports 56 members met requirements in 2020. A discussion followed
regarding member statuses of “exempt,” etc. Discussion regarding proposed amendment for
2020 LOSAP due to COVID and the village, Mayor Pugh will look into it.
FUND DRIVE T. Dinkler reports 2021 mailer going out soon, will complete 2020 report when
he returns from vacation.
BIG BUCKS D. Vlad asked if we will hold a Big Bucks Raffle this year in conjunction with
Summerfest – if it occurs. A discussion followed, a motion was made by D. Vlad/2nd B. Powers
to request permission from Village to hold said raffle with the potential of an October drawing
date if necessary – CARRIED.
