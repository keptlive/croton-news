F. Streany asked members to please be mindful of their handwriting on department documents and to
clearly write member ID numbers and signatures.

B. Powers asked for clarification of which members can and cannot vote in 2023 Chief’s Elections. A
discussion followed. A list is attached to this document of members eligibility status. A Motion was made
by M. Feminella / 2nd W. Skiba and CARRIED to allow members that meet the following to vote at
12/7/23 elections;
1. Members with less than 20 years of Active Service must have made 5% or more of calls in 2022 and,
2. Members with less than 20 years of Active Service must be up to date on physical requirements and,
3. Members with less than 20 years of Active Service must have completed required compliance training
prior to this date (11/28/23) OR
4. Members with 20 or more years of Active Service – regardless of #1, #2, #3 above – are allowed to
vote AND
5. Members that transferred their status from Social to Active in 2023 are allowed to vote.
