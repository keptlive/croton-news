DISCUSSION:
Trustee Gallelli stated that the Board put a lot of time and effort into this Budget
and hopes that the residents realize the seriousness to which the Board has taken
into putting together a good Budget that will ensure that we continue to provide
quality services to our residents while keeping it under the “Tax Cap”. Trustee
Gallelli thanked Village Manager King, Treasurer Bullock and our Department Heads
for working extremely hard on this Budget.
Trustee Simon stated that the Board made some aggressive cuts, found new
revenue sources, used some of our “Reserve Fund”, to the extent appropriate, and
incorporated some of our future Federal funding which we know will be forthcoming
soon. Trustee Simon thanked Village Manager King, staff and our Department Heads
for their hard work as well as our residents who hung in with us as we made these
challenging and difficult decisions. Trustee Simon said that he believes that the
Board has delivered a good Budget that will carry us through the year ahead.
Trustee Murtaugh thanked Village staff and Department Heads for working diligently
and cooperatively with the Board, we have forced our Departments to do more with
less and be more flexible; our DPW, Water, Sewer, and Recreation Departments as
well as our Justice Court showed a reduction in their Budget from last year, and our
Department of Public Works continues to save the Village a substantial amount of
money by using staff to perform the work that we might have had to contract out.
Trustee Murtaugh said that this is a direct result of hiring a Superintendent of Public
Works who is a Professional Engineer as well as staff who are well versed in
construction. Trustee Murtaugh stated that we have borrowed from our “Fund
Balance” pretty aggressively but that allowed us to stay under the “Tax Cap” which
demonstrates that we take this very seriously, the Board did “bite the bullet” and
purchased some new equipment because we understand that prices are not going
down and it is a way to ensure that we keep our Village running smoothly.
Mayor Pugh stated that this Budget is over a half a million dollars less than our prepandemic 2019 Budget, it also reduces Village Debt by approximately 1.8 million
dollars thanks to the Debt that is maturing and expiring. Mayor Pugh stated that

WHEREAS Local Law No. 3 of 2003 was approved at a regular Village Board Meeting
held on October 7, 2003, and
WHEREAS this Local Law called for the setting of Village fees through Board
Resolutions, and
WHEREAS it is to the benefit of both the Village and the general public to have all
fees conveniently placed in one schedule, which may be amended from time to
time, and
WHEREAS a master fee schedule was adopted on October 7, 2003 and has since
been modified a number of times,
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED: that the Village Board of Trustees hereby adopts
the attached updated master fee schedule effective June 1, 2021.
DISCUSSION:
Trustee Murtaugh stated that the Board paid a lot of attention to fees, no fee was
frivolously adjusted without some serious thought and consideration on the impact
to our residents, but in some cases the Village does have to charge for certain
activities, and it is necessary to keep the Village whole and running properly.
Trustee Simon stated that the vast majority of fees have not changed and the ones
that were increased were only increased after the most careful consideration and
specific criteria that was brought to the Board by our Village Manager and
Department Heads.
