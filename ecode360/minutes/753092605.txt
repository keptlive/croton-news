An Executive Session of the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, New York,
was held on August 21, 2024, in the Georgianna Grant Meeting Room of the Stanley H.
Kellerhouse Municipal Building.
Present:
Mayor Pugh
Trustee Simon
Trustee Murtaugh
Trustee Nicholson
Trustee Politi
Village Manager Bryan Healy
On a motion by Trustee Nicholson, seconded by Trustee Murtaugh, the Board voted
unanimously to enter into an executive session at 6:37 PM to discuss the employment history of
a particular individual.
No formal action was taken during this session.
On a motion by Trustee Simon, seconded by Trustee Nicholson, the Board voted unanimously to
adjourn the executive session at 7:00 PM.
Submitted by:
Bryan Healy, Village Manager
