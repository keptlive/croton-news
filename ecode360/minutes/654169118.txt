3. PUBLIC HEARING
Motion to open a Public Hearing to consider a Local Law Introductory No.
4 of 2022, to repeal Chapter 141 of the Village Code entitled “Housing
Standards” was made by Trustee Rosales. Motion was seconded by
Trustee Horowitz and approved with a 5-0 vote.
Village Manager Healy explained that this law was originally adopted in
1982 in conjunction with the “Urban County Community Development
Program Housing Standard Code”, in the forty years since this law was
adopted, New York State adopted a Statewide “Property Maintenance
Code” as well as a “Uniform Fire Prevention and Building Code” and this
law is bringing our code up to date to meet those new standards.
There being no comments to come before, a motion to close the Public
Hearing was made by Trustee Horowitz. Motion was seconded by Trustee
Simon and approved with a 5-0 vote.

RESOLUTION #34 OF 2020
On motion of TRUSTEE HOROWITZ, seconded by TRUSTEE GALLELLI, the following
resolution was adopted by the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-onHudson, New York, with a 5-0 vote.
WHEREAS the Village would like to repeal Chapter 141 of the Village Code, Housing
Standards, as it has become obsolete; and
WHEREAS the Village Board is considering the adoption of Local Law Introductory
No. 4 of 2022, which has been drafted to reflect such language; and
WHEREAS a Public Hearing on Local Law Introductory No. 4 of 2022 was opened
and closed on March 21, 2022,
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED: that the Village Board of Trustees hereby
adopts Local Law Introductory No. 4 of 2022 to repeal Chapter 141 of the Village
Code, Housing Standards, which upon adoption becomes Local Law No. 4 of 2022.

WHEREAS the Village would like to amend Chapter 230 of the Village Code, Zoning,
to add provisions related to cannabis shops, and
WHEREAS the Village Board is considering the adoption of Local Law Introductory
No. 5 of 2022, which has been drafted for such purposes, and
WHEREAS the Village has completed Part 1 of the Short Environmental Assessment
Form (EAF) and a Coastal Assessment Form (CAF),
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED AS FOLLOWS:
1. The Village Board hereby determines that the Proposed Action is an Unlisted
Action.
2. The Village Board hereby declares itself to be the Lead Agency for SEQRA
purposes in connection with the Proposed Action.
3. The Village Board hereby issues the EAF and CAF which it has before it in
connection with the Proposed Action.
4. The Village Board hereby refers to the Village Planning Board the Draft Law
for a report back to the Village Board in accordance with Village law.
5. The Village Board hereby refers the Draft Law, the EAF and the CAF to the
Village Waterfront Advisory Committee for a recommendation back to the
Village Board in accordance with Village Law.
6. The Village Board hereby refers the Draft Law, the EAF and the CAF the
Westchester County Planning Board for review in accordance with law.
7. The Village Board hereby directs Village staff to make any other circulations
and notifications regarding the Proposed Action as may be required by law.
DISCUSSION:
Trustee Simon stated that in the draft Local Law it refers to the “C1” District, but in
the “EAF” and “CAF” it also refers to the “C2”, “CIRA” and “CIRB” Districts and
asked Village Attorney Whitehead to explain.
Village Attorney Whitehead stated that the permitted uses are listed in the “C1”
Zoning District, the other three Districts refer back to uses permitted in the “C1”
District so the change in the Code are only made in the “C1”, but are immediately
effective in the other three Districts.
Trustee Simon stated that the law requires that these uses be five-hundred feet
from a school and two-hundred feet from a religious institution, but on the map, it
