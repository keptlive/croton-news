An Executive Session of the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, NY was held on
Tuesday, April 27, 2021 at the Stanley H. Kellerhouse Municipal Building, 1 Van Wyck Street, Crotonon-Hudson, NY 10520.
On a motion by Trustee Simon, seconded by Trustee Horowitz, the Board voted unanimously to enter into
an executive session at 4:45 PM for discussions related to personnel.
The following officials were present:
Mayor Pugh
Trustee Gallelli
Trustee Horowitz
Trustee Murtaugh
Trustee Simon
Village Manager Janine King
Two invited individuals were also in attendance. No action was taken.
On a motion by Trustee Gallelli, seconded by Trustee Murtaugh, the Board voted unanimously to adjourn
the Executive Session at 7:00 PM.
Submitted by,
Janine King, Village Manager
