VILLAGE OF CROTON-ON-HUDSON
BOARD OF TRUSTEES
LOCAL LAW NO. 15 OF 2023
A LOCAL LAW TO AMEND CHAPTER 123, FIREARMS, OF THE VILLAGE CODE
Be it enacted by the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson as follows:
Section One. Chapter 123, Firearms, of the Code of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, §123-3 is
hereby amended to read as follows:
§ 123-3. Exemptions.
This chapter shall not apply to:
A. The authorized use of a pistol, rifle or target range regularly operated and maintained by a law
enforcement agency.
B. Law enforcement officers in the discharge of their duties or other persons in the defense of
person or property.
C. persons lawfully engaged in historical reenactments, educational programming involving
historical weapons of warfare, or motion picture or theatrical productions under the conditions
outlined in § 123-4.
D. Persons participating in military ceremonies, funerals, and honor guards.
Section Two. A new Section 4 of Chapter 123 entitled "Approval required" is hereby added to the
Code of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson to read as follows:
§ 123-4. Approval required
A. Coordinators or solo performers of historical reenactments, educational programming
involving historical weapons of warfare, or motion picture or theatrical productions that
discharge firearm(s) within the Village of Croton-on-Hudson shall first be required to
obtain approval from the Chief of Police.
B. Application for such approval shall be made in writing to the Chief of Police
no less than 30 calendar days in advance of the date of the event and shall include the
following information:
1) The name, address and telephone number of the armorer responsible for the event,
or reenactment, or entertainment group;
2) A list of every person possessing a firearm who intends to participate in a
reenactment or other form of entertainment and discharge the firearm on property
within the Village of Croton-on-Hudson;
3) The date, time of day and location at which the event is to be held.

Section Three. A new Section 5 of Chapter 123 entitled "Notice required" is hereby added to the
Code of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson to read as follows:
§ 123-5. Notice required.
Notice of any historical reenactments, educational programming involving historical weapons of
warfare, or motion picture or theatrical productions that will involve discharging firearm(s) within
the Village of Croton-on-Hudson shall be mailed not less than seven days prior to the date of the
event to all owners of property abutting and within 200 feet of the property on which the event is
to be held.
Section Four. Severability
If any section, subsection, clause, phrase or other portion of this Local Law is, for any reason,
declared invalid, in whole or in part, by any court, agency, commission, legislative body or other
authority of competent jurisdiction, such portion shall be deemed a separate, distinct and
independent portion. Such declaration shall not affect the validity of the remaining portions
hereof, which other portions shall continue in full force and effect.
Section Five. Effective Date
This local law shall take effect immediately upon filing in the office of the New York State
Secretary of State in accordance with section 27 of the Municipal Home Rule Law.

On motion of TRUSTEE SIMON, seconded by TRUSTEE GALLELLI, the
following resolution was adopted by the Board of Trustees of the Village of Crotonon-Hudson, New York, with a 5-0 vote.
Resolution #188-2023
WHEREAS New York State recently enacted legislation relating to the usage of
firearms in historical reenactments, military funeral salutes, and other similar
activities, and
WHEREAS the Village would like to amend Chapter 123, Firearms, of the Village
Code to reflect these changes, and
WHEREAS the Village Board of Trustees is considering the adoption of Local Law
Introductory No. 15 of 2023, which has been drafted for such purposes, and
WHEREAS a Public Hearing on Local Law Introductory No. 15 of 2023 was opened
and closed on August 28, 2023,
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED that the Village Board of Trustees hereby
adopts Local Law Introductory No. 15 of 2023 to amend Chapter 123, Firearms, of
the Village Code, which upon adoption becomes Local Law No. 15 of 2023.
