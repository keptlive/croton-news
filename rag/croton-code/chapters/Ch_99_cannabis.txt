     § 99-1. Legislative intent.

     It is the intent of this article that the Village of Croton-on-Hudson opt out of having on-site cannabis
     consumption establishments licensed to operate or established within the Village of Croton-on-Hudson.

     § 99-2. Authority.

     This article is adopted pursuant to New York Cannabis Law § 131, which expressly authorizes villages to
     opt out of allowing on-site cannabis consumption establishments to be licensed or established within their
     boundaries.

     § 99-3. Local cannabis on-site consumption opt-out.

     A.     The Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, County of Westchester, hereby opts out
            of having on-site cannabis consumption establishments licensed or established within the boundaries
            of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, Westchester County, New York.

     B.     Pursuant to the New York Cannabis Law § 131, the Village of Croton-on-Hudson hereby requests the
            Cannabis Control Board to prohibit the establishment of on-site consumption licenses contained in
            Article Four of the New York State Cannabis Law (Chapter 7-A of the Consolidated Laws) within
            the jurisdiction of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, Westchester County, New York.
