A Work Session of the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, NY was held on Monday,
January 10, 2022 at 7 PM via Zoom Conference call in accordance with the Chapter 417 of the Laws of
2021 in light of the public health and safety concerns associated with the COVID-19 pandemic.
The following officials were present:
Mayor Pugh
Village Manager Healy
Clerk DiSanto
Assistant to the Village Manager
Morzello
Attorney Whitehead

Trustee Gallelli
Trustee Rosales
Trustee Horowitz
Trustee Simon

Discussed at this work session was the
Village Manager’s response to the omicron surge, a review of Planning Board recommendations on the
zoning petition for the North End Gateway District, an update from AKRF regarding the South Riverside
Area zoning study, a discussion on existing deed restrictions for property adjacent to 72 Thompson
Avenue, and a review of the timeline for the proposed solar canopy project to be located at Hudson
National Golf Club and discussion on future steps.
