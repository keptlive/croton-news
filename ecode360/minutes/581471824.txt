An Executive Session of the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, NY was held on
Monday, December 21, 2020 via Zoom videoconference, pursuant to Executive Order 202.1 issued by
Governor Andrew Cuomo on March 12, 2020.
On a motion by Trustee Habib, seconded by Trustee Simon, the Board voted unanimously to enter into an
executive session at 5:30 PM for discussions regarding the proposed acquisition, sale or lease of real
property and discussions that could imperil public safety if disclosed publicly.
The following officials were present:
Mayor Pugh
Trustee Gallelli
Trustee Habib
Trustee Horowitz
Trustee Simon
Village Manager Janine King
Village Clerk Paula DiSanto
Village Attorney Linda Whitehead
Secretary to the Village Manager Bryan Healy
No action was taken.
On a motion by Trustee Habib, seconded by Trustee Simon, the Board voted unanimously to adjourn the
Executive Session at 6:24 PM.
Submitted by,
Bryan Healy, Secretary to the Village Manager
