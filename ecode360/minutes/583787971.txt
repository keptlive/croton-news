An Executive Session of the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, NY was held on
Tuesday, January 5, 2021 via Zoom videoconference, pursuant to Executive Order 202.1 issued by
Governor Andrew Cuomo on March 12, 2020.
On a motion by Trustee Simon, seconded by Trustee Habib, the Board voted unanimously to enter into an
executive session at 4:03 PM for discussions regarding the proposed acquisition, sale or lease of real
property.
The following officials were present:
Mayor Pugh
Trustee Gallelli
Trustee Habib
Trustee Horowitz
Trustee Simon
Village Manager Janine King
Village Attorney Linda Whitehead
Secretary to the Village Manager Bryan Healy
Two invited representatives also attended the executive session. No action was taken.
Trustee Habib left the meeting at 5:45 PM. Trustee Horowitz left the meeting at 5:53 PM.
On a motion by Trustee Simon, seconded by Trustee Gallelli, the Board voted unanimously to adjourn the
Executive Session at 5:54 PM.
Submitted by,
Bryan Healy, Secretary to the Village Manager
