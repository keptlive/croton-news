RESPONSES TO QUESTIONS SUBMITTED BY E-MAIL
E-Mail from Ed and Debi Braddick stating that they are against residential
parking permits on Young Avenue.
E-Mail from Jan Klier stating that even though there are fewer commuters
then when they first asked for residential parking permits on Young
Avenue, he has witnessed employees parking on Young Avenue and
walking over to their businesses and thinks that even though we may not
need this now we could need it in the future
E-mail from Philippe Rayer said that he watched the Work Session on
June 22nd when the Bicycle Pedestrian Committee met with the Board and
commented that in Europe speed bumps are used to control speed.
E-mail from Grace Bender asking that the hours of the Dog Park be
extended. Village Manager King advised that the hours were reduced to
help the neighbors who are dealing with working from home.
E-mail from Toni Senecal asking about selling alcohol in the parks.
Village Manager King advised that in the past we have lifted the “no
alcohol in parks” restriction on concert nights but not at any other time.
Mayor Pugh stated that while we’ve allowed alcohol in the past during
events, we are not in that situation right now and we are not trying to
have people congregate in that fashion; the idea of the parks, at this
time, is so that people can exercise and get fresh air. Mayor Pugh stated
that because of the other issues we have seen with the very intense use
of the parks during the Pandemic with respect to garbage, lack of social
distancing and illegal swimming we do not think adding alcohol to the
mix improves any of that and does not feel it makes any sense to revise
our long standing policy.

Resolution #110 of 2020
On motion of TRUSTEE HOROWITZ, seconded by TRUSTEE SIMMONS, the following
resolution was adopted by the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson,
New York with the following Roll Call Vote: Trustee Habib Aye, Trustee Simmons
Aye, Trustee Gallelli Aye, Trustee Horowitz Aye, Mayor Pugh Aye.
WHEREAS, on December 16, 2019, the Village Board of Trustees adopted a
resolution affirming its support for a residential parking permit system in the
Harmon neighborhood on the following streets: Hastings Avenue and Young
Avenue; and
WHEREAS, the Village’s elected state representatives, Assemblywoman Sandra
Galef and State Senator Peter Harckham, have introduced companion bills in the
state legislature to enact the residential parking permit system on the
aforementioned streets; and
WHEREAS, the Village is required to formally submit a home rule request to the
legislature,
NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED: that the Village Board of Trustees hereby
requests a residential parking permit system be enacted in the Village of Croton-onHudson under the state’s home rule authority; and
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED: that a copy of this home rule request be sent to State
Assemblywoman Sandra Galef and State Senator Peter Harckham.
DISCUSSION

7

Trustee Gallelli asked how this would affect parishioners attending “Good Shepherd”
services at the corner of Young and Benedict.
Village Manager King stated that a certain percentage of spaces would be
designated and remain open to non-residents as well as residents. Village Manager
King said that anyone attending church services would be able to park there or they
would have to find another street to park.
Village Attorney Whitehead stated that we can make it Monday through Friday only
which would resolve the issue of commuters.
Village Manager King stated that she believes this came about because of the
businesses and not the commuters but our elected representatives are bringing it
forward using the commuter issue.
Mayor Pugh stated that this still has to be passed by the State Legislature and if it is
passed then we can adjust the days and times.
Village Attorney Whitehead added that the Board would still have to adopt a Local
Law which is where the Board can hammer out the details.
Trustee Simmons asked if this is approved by the State Legislature can it be put on
hold until the economy improves.
Village Attorney Whitehead stated that there may be a timeframe and understands
there are not a lot of commuters at this time but believes we will not receive the
“Home Rule” legislation for a while and the Village still has to pass a Local Law if
they choose to move forward.
