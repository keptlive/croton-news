Resolution #140 of 2020
On motion of TRUSTEE HABIB and seconded by TRUSTEE SIMMONS the following
resolution was adopted by the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson,
New York with the following Roll Call Vote: Trustee Habib Aye, Trustee Simmons
Aye, Trustee Gallelli Aye, Trustee Horowitz Aye, Mayor Pugh Aye.
WHEREAS, Chapter 197-15 of the Village Code, Snow and Ice on Sidewalks, provides
for the property owner or lessee to keep the adjoining sidewalk free from snow and
ice, subject to certain parameters specified in the law; and
WHEREAS, Chapter 197-15, subsection D, provides for the Village to cause the snow
and ice to be removed from the sidewalk if not done by the homeowner within the
prescribed 12-hour period; and
HEREAS, Chapter 197-15, subsection E, provides for the Village to charge the
property owner for the cost of the removal of the snow and ice; and
WHEREAS, last year, the Village contracted with La Teja Contractors & Landscaping,
Inc. to perform this work; and
WHEREAS, La Teja has agreed to hold their rates from last year for this work $65/hour for snow shoveling, $85/hour for snow blowing, $125/hour for using a skid
steer and $30/bag for use of calcium chloride,
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED: that the Village Manager is hereby authorized to
enter into a contract with La Teja Contractors & Landscaping, Inc. for snow removal
services for the 2020-2021 winter season.

Trustee Gallelli advised that Croton has an 80.3% response rate from those
that have self-responded to the 2020 Census. Trustee Gallelli advised that
there are still enumerators out in the field in Croton that are reaching out to
households that have not responded and those households will be counted
and added to Croton’s total as well.
Trustee Gallelli reminded everyone of the “Run against Hunger” event that
will be held virtually, anyone interested in participating in the event can go to
their web site at runagainsthunger.com for more information.
Trustee Gallelli advised that the Westchester County Board of Elections has
been sending out information regarding the upcoming election; one of the
post cards with early voting information has a bar code that will expedite a
voter’s process at the polls, but emphasized that all voters will still be
allowed to vote if they do not have the post card with them at the time of
voting.
Trustee Gallelli advised that the Village will be holding its second annual Tag
Sale this Saturday, from 10am to 4:00pm, the map indicating all those
participating in the Tag Sale will be available tomorrow.
Trustee Habib stated that this was probably the most challenging summers
that has confronted local governments and the changes that our staff made
throughout Croton should not be overlooked. Trustee Habib said that during
the recent re-opening of Senasqua Park people were complying to the social
distancing regulations and wearing masks and they were enjoying our park
and it made him very proud of what we have been able to do for our
community. Trustee Habib thanked the entire staff for all their efforts.
Mayor Pugh appointed Christine Wagner as the Chair of the Zoning Board of
Appeals; Peter Sedlmair to the Recreation Advisory Committee, and Aliyah
Simpson to the Diversity and Inclusion Committee.
There being no further business to come before the Board, a motion to close
the meeting was made by Trustee Habib. Motion was seconded by Trustee
Gallelli and approved with a 5-0 vote. 7:04pm
