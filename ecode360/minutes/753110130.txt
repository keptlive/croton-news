MEETING SUMMARY

Croton IDEA Advisory 08/02
02 Aug 2024 / 2:45 PM / MUNICIPAL BUILDING CONFERENCE ROOM
Attending: Cara, Cristina, Faith, Marlena, Nora, Sherry
Committee Business
Chair gave a budget update. We were delighted to announce BOE liaisons
Anamika Bhatnagar and Sarah Carrier. A new proposal for youth committee
membership was favorably discussed. All agreed the CHHS student
collaboration for Juneteenth added greatly to the events. A subcommittee
was established to pursue youth membership further.
Civic Education Proposal
The committee reviewed a Croton resident’s Civic Education proposal.
Discussion included comments from LHC. While all agreed on the worthiness of
the proposal to increase voter eduction, the committee ultimately voted against
taking on the program. Activities such as registering voters and circulating
candidate information was deemed outside IDEA’s scope.
Croton Pride Day Proposal
We examined the estimated cost for Village personnel at the proposed event. It
clearly exceed IDEA’s budget so other avenues were discussed. Committee
members voted in favor of co-producing the event outside of Village personnel
expenses.
