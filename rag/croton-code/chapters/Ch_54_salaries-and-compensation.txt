§ 54-1.                Compensation of Mayor.
     § 54-2.                Compensation of Trustees.


     [HISTORY: Adopted by the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson 5-20-1974 by
     L.L. No. 1-1974. Amendments noted where applicable.]


     § 54-1. Compensation of Mayor. [Amended 8-19-1985 by L.L. No. 6-1985; 2-20-2001 by L.L. No.
     2-200115]

     The Mayor of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, New York, shall be compensated at the rate of $5,000 per
     annum.

     § 54-2. Compensation of Trustees.16 [Amended 8-19-1985 by L.L No. 6-1985; 2-20-2001 by L.L. No.
     2-200117]

     Each Trustee of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, New York, shall be compensated at the rate of $3,000
     per annum.


     15. Editor's Note: This local law provided that it shall take effect 6-1-2002.
     16. Editor's Note: Information concerning the salaries and compensation of other Village officers and employees is available in the office
         of the Village Clerk.
     17. Editor's Note: This local law provided that it shall take effect 6-1-2002.
