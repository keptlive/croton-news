A Work Session of the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, NY was held on
Monday, June 22, 2020, at 6:00 PM via Zoom videoconference, pursuant to Executive Order
202.1 issued by Governor Andrew Cuomo on March 12, 2020.
The following officials were present:
Mayor Pugh
Trustee Habib
Trustee Simmons
Supt. of Public Works Balbi

Trustee Gallelli
Trustee Horowitz
Village Manager King
Sec. to the Manager Healy

The following resolutions were adopted by the Board of Trustees:
On motion of TRUSTEE HABIB, seconded by TRUSTEE HOROWITZ, the following
resolution was adopted by the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, New York
by a vote of 5-0:
Resolution #107-2020
WHEREAS, the Village Board of Trustees would like to amend the Street Vendor License
Policy in recognition of the graduation events being held in the Village; and
WHEREAS, this was discussed at the June 22, 2020 work session,
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED: that the Street Vendor License Policy is amended as
follows:
1. Street vendor licenses shall be permitted to be issued on June 25, 2020, and June 26,
2020, for the Upper Village and Harmon areas only.
2. The hours of these licenses shall be from 1 to 9 p.m.
