         a. Motion to open a Public Hearing on Local Law Introductory No. 2 of
            2022 to amend Chapter 230 of the Code of the Village of Croton-on-
            Hudson, entitled “Zoning”, to establish use and bulk/area regulations
            pertaining to the “North End Gateway” District was made by Trustee
            Simon. Motion was seconded by Trustee Rosales and approved with a
            5-0 vote.

             Village Attorney Whitehead explained that the Zoning Code
             amendment will allow for either all residential or mixed use within the
             “North End Gateway” District, similar to what has been allowed in the
             “Harmon Gateway” and the “Municipal Place Gateway” Districts. Village
             Attorney Whitehead stated that the Petitioner, who is proposing a
             project in the “North End Gateway” District, petitioned for this
             amendment to allow for a mixed use project.

             On behalf of the Matra Group, a representative from the law firm of
             Zarin and Steinmetz advised that the proposal at 1380 Albany Post

Resolution #13 of 2022

On motion of TRUSTEE HOROWITZ, seconded by TRUSTEE GALLELLI, the following
resolution was adopted by the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-
Hudson, New York with the following Roll Call vote: Trustee Rosales Aye, Trustee
Simon Aye, Trustee Gallelli Aye, Trustee Horowitz Aye, Mayor Pugh Aye.

WHEREAS the Village received a request for consistency from the New York State
Department of Transportation regarding for comments regarding the sealing of
cracks and mastic contract to extend the service life and rideability of local roads
throughout the region, and

WHEREAS on January 4, 2022, the Village Board referred the request to the
Waterfront Advisory Committee, and

WHEREAS the Village Board has received a recommendation of consistency from
the Waterfront Advisory Committee, and

WHEREAS the Village Board must make its own determination of consistency with
the LWRP policy standards and conditions, and

WHEREAS on January 18, 2022, the Village Board reviewed the LWRP policy
standards and conditions,

NOW THEREFORE BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED as follows:

The Village Board of Trustees found the following LWRP policies to be applicable and
finds the Proposed Action to be consistent with each of these applicable policies:

POLICY 7B: Materials that can degrade water quality and degrade or destroy the
ecological system of the Croton River and Bay significant fish and wildlife habitat
and the Haverstraw Bay significant fish and wildlife habitat shall not be disposed of
                                         7
Village Trustees Board Meeting
February 7, 2022
Page 8




or allowed to drain in, or on land within, the area of influence in the significant fish
and wildlife habitats.

POLICY 7G: Such activities must not cause degradation of water quality or impact
identified significant fish and wildlife habitats.

POLICY 8: Protect fish and wildlife resources in the coastal area from the
introduction of hazardous wastes and other pollutants which bio-accumulate in the
food chain, or which cause significant sub lethal or lethal effect on those resources.

POLICY 30B: Storage and disposal of all materials shall be monitored by the state
to assure there will be no discharge or leaching of materials into coastal waters.

POLICY 37: Best Management Practices will be utilized to minimize the non-point
discharge of excess nutrients, organic and eroded soils into coastal waters.

The remaining policies and conditions of the LWRP not specifically discussed above
were reviewed and found not to be applicable.

Resolution #15 of 2022

WHEREAS the Village of Croton-on-Hudson budgets for part-time park rangers and
school crossing guards in the Auxiliary Police Services account, and

WHEREAS, continuing effects of the COVID-19 pandemic resulted in park rangers
being assigned less work than budgeted, and

WHEREAS two resignations by long-time school crossing guards permitted

purchase needed police equipment, and

WHEREAS the reallocation of these funds at this time would help to keep the 2022-
2023 budget within the tax cap parameters,

NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED that the Village Board hereby authorizes the
Village Treasurer to transfer $17,798.92 from A3189.1100 (Personnel Services P/T)
to the following account: A3120.4000 – Police - Equipment: $17,798.92
