MINUTES OF THE WATER CONTROL COMMISSION
WEDNESDAY, APRIL 21, 202, 4:30 P.M.
PRESENT: Mr. Mark Goldfarb, Deputy Chair
Ms. Gail Sasso
Dr. Merrily Besvinick
Mr. Leo Wiegman (arrived 4:34 p.m.)
Absent:
Mr. Stuart Greenbaum
Also Present: Dan O’Connor, Village Engineer
1. Call to order:
Deputy Chair Goldfarb called the Water Control Commission meeting of April 21, 2021 to order at 4:30
p.m.
2. OLD BUSINESS
a) Historic Hudson Valley—525 South Riverside Avenue --Application for Wetlands Permit
for site improvements including construction of concrete sidewalks, maintenance of
historic brick walkway and stormwater management, landscape improvements, utility
improvements, and roof replacements, some of which will occur in the 120 ft.
wetland/watercourse buffer. Declaration of Lead Agency and referral of application to
Waterfront Advisory Committee for its review and recommendation on consistency with
the LWRP (Local Waterfront Revitalization Program).
Deputy Chairman Goldfarb asked for a motion to declare the Water Control Commission Lead Agency for
Historic Hudson Valley’s application for a wetlands permit. Ms. Sasso made the motion, seconded by Dr.
Besvinick, and the motion carried in favor by a vote of 3-0 (Mr. Wiegman not present yet).
Deputy Chairman Goldfarb asked for a motion to refer the application to the Waterfront Advisory
Committee for its recommendation on consistency with the LWRP. Ms. Sasso made the motion,
seconded by Dr. Besvinick, and the motion carried in favor by a vote of 3-0 (Mr. Wiegman not present yet)
Deputy Chairman made a motion to approve the minutes of March 17, 2021, seconded by Dr. Besvinick,
and the motion carried by a vote of 4-0, all in favor.
3. ADJOURNMENT
Dr. Besvinick made a motion to adjourn the meeting, seconded by Ms. Sasso, and the motion carried by
a vote of 4-0. The meeting was adjourned at 4:35 p.m.
