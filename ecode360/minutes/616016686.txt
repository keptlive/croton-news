Motion to open a Public Hearing on Local Law Introductory #10 of 2021 to
amend Chapter 168 of the Village Code entitled “Parks and Recreation
Areas” to prohibit the smoking of cannabis in parks and recreation areas
was made by Trustee Horowitz. Motion was seconded by Trustee
Simon and approved with a 3-0 vote.
COMMENTS RECEIVED VIA E-MAIL
Jessa Mittleman, 14 Ridge Road, Croton on Hudson, asked why the Board
is doing this since the State has de-criminalized Cannabis.
Village Manager King explained that State law says that anywhere where
cigarette smoking is prohibited, Cannabis can be prohibited as well and
this is being done to make sure that it is clear to everyone.
Mayor Pugh stated that our Village Code already prohibits smoking in
public parks, and on advice of our Village Counsel, and as a matter of best
practices and transparency, rather than people having to impute that
meaning into the Code, it should be clearly spelled out.
Village Attorney Whitehead stated that this will provide clarity so that
someone reading the Code will know that smoking includes Cannabis.
There being no further comments to come before the Board a motion to
close the Public Hearing was made by Trustee Simon. Motion was
seconded by Trustee Horowitz and approved with a 3-0 vote.

Resolution #124 of 2021
On motion of Trustee Simon and seconded by Trustee Horowitz, the following
resolution was adopted by the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-onHudson, New York with a 3-0 vote.
WHEREAS Hudson National Golf Club (HNGC) has applied for an amended special
permit and site plan approval for modifications to their ancillary storage area; and
WHEREAS HNGC received its initial special permit for an annual membership golf
club on July 25, 1994, site plan approval on July 26, 1994, an amended special
permit on April 19, 1999, and amended site plan approval on March 2, 1999; and
WHEREAS on April 5, 2021, the Village Board declared its intent to serve as Lead
Agency for SEQRA purposes and directed the staff to circulate its intent to all
involved agencies; and
WHEREAS the 30-day window to send comments passed with no objections
received,
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED: that the Village Board of Trustees hereby
declares itself Lead Agency for SEQRA purposes in connection with the proposed
action,
AND BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED: that the Village Board of Trustees hereby refers
the special permit application and CAF to the Waterfront Advisory Committee for a
recommendation of consistency with the Local Waterfront Revitalization Program
(LWRP).
