An Executive Session of the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, NY was
held on Monday, August 21, 2023, in the Georgianna Grant Meeting Room of the Stanley H.
Kellerhouse Municipal Building.
On a motion by Trustee Horowitz, seconded by Trustee Simon, the Board voted unanimously to enter into
an executive session at 7:29 PM to discuss matters of personnel.
The following officials were present:
Mayor Pugh
Trustee Horowitz
Trustee Simon

Trustee Gallelli
Trustee Nicholson
Village Manager Healy

No action was taken.
On a motion by Trustee Simon, seconded by Trustee Gallelli, the Board voted unanimously to adjourn the
Executive Session at 8:04 PM.
