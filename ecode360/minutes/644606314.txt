A Special Meeting of the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, NY was held on
Monday, January 24, 2022 at 7 PM via Zoom Conference call in accordance with the Chapter 1 of the
Laws of 2022 in light of the public health and safety concerns associated with the COVID-19 pandemic.
The following officials were present:
Mayor Pugh
Village Manager Healy
Trustee Simon
Assistant to the Village Manager
Morzello

Trustee Gallelli
Trustee Rosales
Trustee Horowitz

The special meeting was called to allow members of the community speak on the proposed Solar Array
Project at the Hudson National Golf Club for up to 10 minutes each. In total 14 members of the public
spoke.
A video of the meeting can be found at: https://play.champds.com/crotononhudsonny/event/735.
On a motion by Trustee Simon, seconded by Trustee Gallelli, the Board voted unanimously to adjourn the
Special Meeting at 9 PM.
Respectfully submitted
