A Work Session of the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, NY was held on
Monday, October 23, 2023, at 7:00 PM in the Georgianna Grant Meeting Room of the Stanley H.
Kellerhouse Municipal Building.
The following officials were present:
Mayor Pugh
Trustee Horowitz
Trustee Simon
Trustee Nicholson
Trustee Gallelli

Village Manager Healy
Emily Mancini, Assistant to the
Village Manager

Discussed at the work session was Accessory Dwelling Unit (ADU) legislation and the adoption of an
updated Statement of Inclusivity for the Village.
The following resolution was adopted by the Board of Trustees at the work session:
On motion of TRUSTEE SIMON, seconded by TRUSTEE GALLELLI, the following resolution was
adopted by the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, New York, with a 5-0 vote:
Resolution #218-2023
WHEREAS, the Village Board is committed to promoting openness, inclusion, freedom and mutual
understanding by ensuring all people, regardless of race, religion, disability, sexual orientation, gender
identity, national origin or immigration status may live free from violence, harassment or prejudice;
and
WHEREAS, the Village Board is further committed to supporting the visibility, dignity, and equality
for all people in the community,
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED: that the Statement of Inclusivity of October 23, 2023 is
approved and adopted in order to foster and advance the goals of making the Village of Croton-onHudson a just, equitable, diverse and inclusive community.
Statement of Inclusivity
October 23, 2023
The Village of Croton-on-Hudson affirms the human rights of all who visit, work or live in our Village.
We aim to be an inclusive and safe place for all people regardless of race, ethnicity, nationality, gender
identity, sexual identity, faith, disability, language, socioeconomic status and immigration status.
Dated: October 23, 2023
Respectfully Summitted,
Emily Mancini, Assistant to the Village Manager
