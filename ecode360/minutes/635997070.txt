ABSENT:

ALSO PRESENT: Mr. Daniel O’Connor, P.E. Village Engineer
1) Chairman Kauderer called the Waterfront Advisory Committee meeting of September 1,
2021 to order at 4:30 p.m.
2) REFERRALS
a) Referral from Village Board on NYSDOT Bridge Washing/Deck sealing along
Route 9.
The WAC members reviewed the letter dated August 3, 2021 from the NYSDOT, and the
memo, dated August12, 2021 from the NYS Department of State on the proposed bridge
washing/deck sealing of the two bridges along Route 9.
There was no CAF submitted for this application.
The WAC reviewed the 44 LWRP policies and identified the applicable policies as noted in the
memo attached.
MOTION: Ms. Gallelli made a motion for a recommendation of consistency, seconded by Mr.
Simon, and the motion carried, in favor, by a vote of 3-0 (Mr. Greenbaum and Dr. Besvinick
were absent).
3. APPROVAL OF MINUTES
Mr. Simon made a motion to approve the minutes of the WAC meeting of July 28, 2021,
seconded by Ms. Gallelli, and the motion carried, in favor, by a vote of 3-0 (Mr. Greenbaum and
Dr. Besvinick absent).
4. ADJOURNMENT:
There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned at 4:50 p.m.
