New Business:
K. Goldstein expressed concern that with all the DPW vehicles parked at the fuel pad, it is
difficult for apparatus to safely maneuver in the lot. A discussion followed, J. Kempter will
speak to DPW management about it.
J. Streany proposed the By-Law committee look into switching the cutoff date for officer
nominiations to September of each year. Motion J. Karpoff/2nd D. Bennett to move forward
with proposal and to have committee write it up – CARRIED. (D. Vlad voted “Nay).
J. Munson brought up proposal to add additional hazmat training for Chief Officers to be
compliant with OSHA regulations. A discussion followed and the subject will be discussed at a
further time.
W. Skiba proposed the By-Law committee review the wording for officer qualifications to make
it more easily understandable and streamlined. Motion W. Skiba/2nd J. Streany to have
committee review these By-Laws – CARRIED (D. Bennett voted “Nay).
Motion J. Streany/2nd D. Vlad to allow P. Dinkler to pick date/time/venue for annual Council
Meeting – CARRIED.
Treasurer’s Report: P. Dinkler reports account has a balance of $67,479.79.

Motion to Adjourn by J. Streany/2nd D. Vlad – CARRIED.

Meeting Adjourned at 21:34.
