What you can send us:
  • News tips — something happening in the village, schools, or local government that we should cover
  • Fact-check requests — a Facebook post, news article, Substack editorial, flyer, or public claim you'd like us to verify
  • Corrections — something we got wrong in an article (we take accuracy seriously)
  • Documents — public records, meeting materials, or other evidence that supports a story
How we handle submissions:
  • Tips are anonymous by default — contact info is optional
  • We verify before publishing — submissions inform our reporting, not replace it
  • We protect sources — we never reveal who sent a tip without permission
  • Fact-checks cite primary sources with footnotes so you can verify our work too
