An Executive Session of the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, New York,
was held on November 25, 2024, in the Georgianna Grant Meeting Room of the Stanley H.
Kellerhouse Municipal Building.
Present:
Mayor Pugh
Trustee Simon
Trustee Nicholson
Trustee Politi
Village Manager Bryan Healy
Also present:
Two invited guests
A motion to close the special meeting and enter into an executive session to discuss the proposed
acquisition, sale or lease of real property when publicity would substantially affect the value
thereof was made by Trustee Simon. The motion was seconded by Trustee Nicholson and approved
with a 4-0 vote. The meeting was adjourned at 6:04 PM.
No formal action was taken during this session.
On a motion by Trustee Simon, seconded by Trustee Nicholson, the Board voted unanimously to
adjourn the executive session at 7:30 PM.
Submitted by:
Bryan Healy, Village Manager
