‭●‬ ‭Fire Records Management‬
‭○‬ ‭Still in process of getting call data backlog updated into system.‬
‭○‬ ‭We have 99% of March and April finalized, just tracking down missing info from‬
‭poorly completed call sheets.‬
‭○‬ ‭We are now putting May and June sheets into the system, these sheets and all calls‬
‭going forwards should be quicker to process because we were able to get CAD‬
‭data for them, which we weren't for half of March and all of April. We have about‬
‭half of both May and June entered in. I anticipate sending out updated call‬
‭numbers through end of June sometime this weekend, as well as working on‬
‭getting July entered in as well. I hope to have call numbers through the end of‬
‭October sent out by December 16th‬
‭○‬ ‭For any call I have been on since July, I have put at least 85% of the info for those‬
‭calls directly into the system, those calls just need the info from the individual rig‬
‭sheets. 2082 and 2083 are also now directly entering their incident reports directly‬
‭into the system, both current calls and working on putting their backlog of call‬
‭reports in digitally.‬
‭○‬ ‭Just an FYI, currently, the new records system cannot directly import ALL of the‬
‭data we'd like from the County's CAD system, this won't be resolved until after‬
‭the County switches to its new CAD system, which is at least a year away.‬
‭Currently, our system cannot import the individual rig in and out times, among‬
‭other data. We do get the call location, the dispatch times and most of the‬

‭dispatcher notes. As such, it remains vital, that call sheets be completed properly,‬
‭because it wastes a lot of people's time chasing down call sheet info. These are‬
‭important government reports and must be treated as such.‬
‭○‬ ‭We will not be moving to all digital incident reports until January 2026. This is‬
‭because FEMA has begun rolling out its replacement for the NFIRS v5.0 fire‬
‭reporting system, a system that rolled out in 1999 and is largely based on a 1976‬
‭NFPA standard. The entire thing is being replaced by NERIS (National‬
‭Emergency Response Information System, which has been beta tested by over 60‬
‭departments of various sizes around the US for over 2 years. Starting in 2025 and‬
‭by January 2026, every department in the US will have to transition over to the‬
‭new system.‬
‭○‬ ‭ESO, our records management system vendor has stated that with the huge‬
‭changes in NERIS over NFIRS, that they are developing an entirely new incident‬
‭reporting module, which won't begin to roll out for live testing until early Spring‬
‭2025. I would rather us not be on the front lines of finding the bugs in this new‬
‭module, so we're going to hold off on switching over. As this will be an entirely‬
‭different user interface between the current NFIRS module and the upcoming‬
‭NERIS module, it doesn't make sense to get everyone trained up on a module that‬
‭will be going away just as soon as they get proficient with it.‬
‭●‬ ‭Received records dump from NYS OFPC for all members, working on verifying, a bunch‬
‭of members aren't on record with NYS as members of CFD. So far, we have found‬
‭several instances where the state system has no record of a member's class (typically‬
‭pre-2000) or we have no copy of the class cert. All members need to go into the State's‬
‭LMS training website and update their department membership records. Also, if they are‬
‭a career FF, they should work to get as much of their training documented in LMS as‬
‭possible.‬
‭●‬ ‭All members are responsible for submitting copies of their certs from completed county‬
‭and state classes to their company officers AND to the Chief's office, as well as keeping‬
