Mayor Pugh stated that historically this has been something that we do

the steadfastness of the Board we found over $300,000 in Operating
Budget savings and made some very dramatic changes to the Capital

On motion of TRUSTEE HABIB, seconded by TRUSTEE SIMMONS, the following
resolution was adopted by the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson,
New York with the following vote: 5-0.
WHEREAS, the State Legislature and the Governor enacted legislation that
establishes a “property tax cap” on the amount that a local government’s property
tax levy can increase each year; and
WHEREAS, this law has been effective for local governments since 2012; and
WHEREAS, the state legislation provides for local governments to override the cap
to protect the Village from unforeseen financial circumstances; and
WHEREAS, Local Law No. 2 of 2020 to override the “property tax cap” law was
adopted by the Village Board of Trustees at their meeting on January 21, 2020; and
WHEREAS, the Village of Croton-on-Hudson is not anticipating adopting a budget
exceeding the “property tax cap” law enacted by State Legislature and would like to
repeal Local Law No. 2 of 2020, and
WHEREAS, a Public Hearing was held and closed on April 13, 2020,
NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED: that the Village Board of Trustees hereby
adopts Local Law Introductory No. 3 of 2020 to repeal the prior tax cap override
Local Law 2 of 2020 and reinstates the tax levy limits imposed by General Municipal
Law §3-c which upon adoption will become Local Law No. 6 of 2020.
DISCUSSION
Mayor Pugh stated that being able to do this is a great accomplishment in these
unprecedented times.
