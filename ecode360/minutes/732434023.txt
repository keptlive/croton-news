3. PUBLIC HEARINGS
Public Hearing #1
A motion to open a Public Hearing to consider the telecommunications
Special Permit renewal request from M&B Towers, for the personal wireless
facilities and cell tower at 26 Veterans Plaza was made by Trustee Simon.
The motion was seconded by Trustee Nicholson and approved with a 4-0
vote.
David Kenny, from the law firm of Snyder and Snyder, stated that the
application before the Board this evening is for a renewal of their existing
facility, a Structural Inspection Report was included with the application
that showed that the facility meets all the required structural standards as

Village Board of Trustees
May 22, 2023
Page 2

well as meeting all FCC Rules and Requirements regarding radio frequency
exposure.
Trustee Nicholson asked if upgrades are available to the current
technology that could help with service in the Village.
Laurel Mooney from M&B Towers explained that there are standards that
regulate the amounts that can be “put out”, this particular tower covers
Route 9 and the train tracks, and another site may be needed elsewhere to
improve service.
Trustee Simon stated that the Village is about to embark on a
Telecommunications Master Plan, we are interested in new technologies,
as well as identifying different locations around the Village that we might
take advantage of.
Trustee Gallelli asked if the facility is at its maximum capacity.
Ms. Mooney stated that they still have the capacity for more equipment.
Trustee Nicholson asked if the position on the tower matters in terms of
service.
Ms. Mooney stated that height is important especially in this area where
the topography is very hilly so being at the top is a better position.
There being no comments to come before the Board a motion to close the
Public Hearing was made by Trustee Simon. The motion was seconded by
Trustee Nicholson and approved with a 4-0 vote.

On motion of TRUSTEE SIMON, seconded by TRUSTEE NICHOLSON, the following
resolution was adopted by the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson,
New York with a 4-0 vote.
WHEREAS at a Village Board Meeting on November 3, 2008, a Telecommunications
Special Permit was issued to Homeland Towers LLC, Nextel of New York, New
Cingular Wireless, and Omnipoint Communications for the construction of a tower
upon which there would be a collocation of personal wireless facilities at 26 Veterans
Plaza, and

Village Board of Trustees
May 22, 2023
Page 3

WHEREAS this special permit was renewed on November 25, 2013, and October 1,
2018, and
WHEREAS the current permit expires on November 3, 2023, and must be renewed in
accordance with the Telecommunications Chapter of the Village Code, and
WHEREAS a special permit renewal request has been submitted by M&B Towers, LLC
(f/k/a Homeland Towers, LLC), along with the special permit renewal fee, a copy of
the Removal Bond, an FCC RF Exposure Compliance Report, and the latest approved
Structural Analysis Report, and
WHEREAS a public hearing on the permit renewal was opened and closed on May
22, 2023,
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED that the Village Board of Trustees hereby issue a
renewal of the Telecommunications Special Permit to M&B Towers, LLC (f/k/a Homeland
Towers, LLC), T-Mobile Northeast LLC, (f/k/a Omnipoint Communications, Inc.) and AT&T
(f/k/a New Cingular Wireless) for the collocation of personal wireless facilities at 26 Veterans
Plaza with the following conditions:
1. This permit expires on November 3, 2028; and must be renewed in
accordance with the Telecommunications Chapter of the Village Code.
2. That the Telecommunications facility shall at all times during the life of the
facility comply with the applicable FCC regulations pertaining to radio
frequency emissions, including such regulations as applicable to
cumulative radio frequency emissions, any necessary mitigation measures
to ensure compliance shall follow FCC requirements, procedures, and
protocols.
3. That in accordance with section 206-5(B) of the Village Code antennas
shall be subject to state and federal regulations pertaining to nonionizing
radiation and other health hazards related to such facilities. The owner of
the antenna shall submit to the Board of Trustees evidence of compliance
with the FCC standards on a yearly basis. If new, more restrictive
standards are adopted, the antennas shall be made to comply, or
continued operations may be restricted or prohibited by the Board of
Trustees. The cost of verification of compliance shall be borne by the
owner and operator of the personal wireless facility.
4. That the monopole, including mounted equipment, and foundation be
inspected annually by a qualified individual and a report on the inspection

Village Board of Trustees
May 22, 2023
Page 4

results be submitted to the Village within 30 days of the inspection.
5. That the area for the Personal Wireless Facilities shall be properly
maintained, including the removing of overgrowth, litter, etc.
6. That the Village may switch to a different Emergency Services
communication system in the future and that the PWS Wireless systems
must be operated to prohibit interference, including implementation of any
FCC re-banding plan, to current and future Village wireless systems.
7. That, as required in the Telecommunications Chapter of the Village Code,
the applicant must post a bond in an amount to be determined by the
Village Engineer to cover the costs of removing and disposing of the tower,
antenna, and associated facilities. In the event that a tower is not
removed within 90 days of the cessation of operations at a site, the tower
and associated facilities may be removed by the Village and the costs of
removal assessed against the property, the bond or both.
8. That proper radio frequency and antenna location warning signs be posted
and maintained at the PWS facility compound at 26 Veterans Plaza.
