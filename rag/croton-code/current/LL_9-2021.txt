Be it enacted by the Board ofTrustees ofthe Village of Croton-on-Hudson as follows:
Section 1: The Code of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson is hereby amended lo enact a new
Chapter 99 lo read as follows:
Chapter 99,
Cannabis On-site Consumption Opt-out
§ 99-1 Legislative Intent
It is the intenl of this local law that the Village of Croton-on-Hudson opt out of having on-site
cannabis consumption establishments licensed to operate or established within the Village of
Crolon-on-Hudson.
§ 99-2 Authority
This local law is adopted pursuani to New York Cannabis Law § 131, which expressly authorizes
villages to opt-out of allowing on-site cannabis consumption establishments lo be licensed or
established within their boundaries.
§ 99-3 Local Cannabis On-Site Consumption Opt-Out
A. The Board ofTrustees of the Village of Crolon-on-Hudson, County of Westchester, hereby
opts-out of having on-site cannabis consumption establishments licensed or established
within the boundaries of the Village of Crolon-on-Hudson, Westchester County, New
York.
B. Pursuant lo the New York Cannabis Law § 131, the Village of Crolon-on-Hudson hereby
requests the Cannabis Control Board to prohibit the establishment of on-site consumption
licenses contained in Article Four of the New York Slate Cannabis Law (Chapter 7-A of
the Consolidated Laws) wilhin the jurisdiction of the Village of Crolon-of-Hudson,
Westchester County, New York.

On motion of TRUSTEE GALLELLI, seconded by TRUSTEE SIMON, the
following resolution was adopted by the Board ofTrustees ofthe Village of Crotonon-Hudson, New York with a 5-0 vote.
Resolution #152-2021
WHEREAS the Village would like to adopt Chapter 99 of the Village Code,
Cannabis On-Site Consumption Opt-Out, to prohibit on-site cannabis consumption
establishments within the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, and
WHEREAS Local Law Introductory No. 11 of 2021 has been drafted for such
purposes, and
WHEREAS a Public Hearing was opened and closed on September 20, 2021,
NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED: that the Village Board ofTrustees hereby
adopts Local Law Introductory No. 11 of 2021 to adopt Chapter 99 of the Village
Code, Cannabis On-Site Consumption Opt-Out, which upon adoption becomes
Local LawNo. 9 of2021.
