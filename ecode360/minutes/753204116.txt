b. RESOLUTIONS:
Resolution #256-2025
On motion of TRUSTEE SIMON, seconded by TRUSTEE POLITI, the following
resolution was adopted by the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson,
New York with a 5-0 vote.
WHEREAS, Chapter 197-15 of the Village Code, Snow and Ice on Sidewalks, provides
for the property owner or lessee to keep the adjoining sidewalk free from snow and
ice, subject to certain parameters specified in the law; and
WHEREAS, Chapter 197-15, subsection D, provides for the Village to cause the snow
and ice to be removed from the sidewalk if not done by the homeowner within the
prescribed 12-hour period; and
WHEREAS, on October 23, 2024, the Village Board of Trustees approved an
agreement with New England Property Maintenance, of Mahopac New York, for snow
removal services for the 2024-2025 winter season; and
WHEREAS, the contractor has agreed to maintain the same schedule of rates for the
2025-2026 winter season,
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED: that the Village Manager is hereby authorized to
enter into a contract with New England Property Maintenance, of Mahopac, New York,
for snow removal services for the 2025-2026 winter season at the following rates:
Minimum charge per property: $70
Snow removal of 4 inches or less: $0.20 per square foot
Snow removal of greater than 4 inches: $0.50 per square foot
Use of calcium chloride (for icy conditions): $70 per bag

7. REPORTS
Trustee Slippen thanked the Village Manager and Board for accommodating
early voting in spite of the disruption it causes staff.
Trustee Politi thanked everyone for coming out to vote, encouraged everyone
to come out to see the screening of the “Arrivals” on Saturday Nov 8, advised
that the Library is holding their annual bake and book sale this weekend.
Trustee Politi stated that she is concerned for the families impacted by the
shut-down but a lot of work is being done by the Croton Food Pantry and
Croton Caring.
Trustee Nicholson thanked everyone for coming out to vote, the Croton Food
Pantry will be holding an emergency food pantry this Saturday, from 9am to
11am in the Holy Name of Mary Church basement.
Trustee Simon advised that the Bicycle and Pedestrian Committee met last
Thursday and launched the new version of “Slow Down Croton”, they also
worked on locations for our next shared road signs, we heard a briefing on
Project Mover’s next move which will take a winter pause, the Rotary’s food
packing program on Fridays has increased from 140 bags of food to 209 bags
and he believes that number will grow, the Rotary will also be holding a Turkey
Trot on November 27th, all funds collected will be donated to the Maria Fareri
Hospital, the Planning Board met and approved the signage for “Mondays”,
and a bike skills class will be held on November 22nd at 10am at Village Hall.
Mayor Pugh thanked everyone who participated in the recent election, and
invited everyone to the Veterans Day Ceremony on Tuesday, November 11th
at 11am at Veterans Corners.
Village Manager Healy advised that depending on the weather the Veterans
ceremony could be held indoors at Holy Name of Mary Church. Village Manager
Healy responded to a concern regarding our Police Officers being used to direct
