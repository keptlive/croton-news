An Executive Session of the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, NY was held on
Tuesday, September 15, 2020 via Zoom videoconference, pursuant to Executive Order 202.1 issued by
Governor Andrew Cuomo on March 12, 2020.
On a motion by Trustee Gallelli, seconded by Trustee Horowitz, the Board voted unanimously to enter
into an executive session at 5:04 PM to discuss matters of public safety.
The following officials were present:
Mayor Pugh
Trustee Gallelli
Trustee Habib
Trustee Horowitz
Trustee Simmons
Village Manager Janine King
Village Clerk Paula DiSanto
Secretary to the Village Manager Bryan Healy
Chief of Police John Nikitopoulos
DPW Superintendent Frank Balbi
No action was taken.
On a motion by Trustee Gallelli, seconded by Trustee Horowitz, the Board voted unanimously to adjourn
the Executive Session at 5:32 PM.
Submitted by,
Bryan Healy, Secretary to the Village Manager
