Section 1. The Mayor of the Village of Croton on Hudson, New York,
shall be compensated at the rate of five thousand dollars
($5,000.) per annum.
Section 2. Each Trustee of the Village of Croton on Hudson, New York,
shall be compensated at the rate of three thousand dollars
($3,000.) per annum.
Section 3. This local law shall take effect June 1, 2002.
