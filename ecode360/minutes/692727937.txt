Resolution #147-2022
On motion of TRUSTEE SIMON, seconded by TRUSTEE ROSALES, the following
resolution was adopted by the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-onHudson, New York, with a 5-0 vote.
WHEREAS the Village would like to amend Chapter 215 of the Village Code, Vehicles
and Traffic, to revise the time limit parking within the residential permit parking
system zones from 2 hours to 4 hours between the hours of 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM on
weekdays, and
WHEREAS Local Law Introductory No. 13 of 2022 has been drafted to reflect such
language, and
WHEREAS a Public Hearing on Local Law Introductory No. 13 was opened and
closed on October 17, 2022,
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED that the Village Board of Trustees hereby
adopts Local Law Introductory No. 13 of 2022 to revise the time limit parking within
the residential permit parking system zones from 2 hours to 4 hours between the
hours of 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM on weekdays, which upon adoption becomes Local
Law No. 10 of 2022.
DISCUSSION:
Trustee Simon stated that we had representatives from the Harmon business
community tell us that this is not the total solution to all of the issues they face as
businesses and employers, but he believes this is an incremental and useful step as
the Board continues to look at other parking initiatives in that area.

Trustee Horowitz stated that this will be welcomed by the merchants, and it is good
that we are doing it.
Trustee Gallelli stated that the comments we received at prior meetings were

Mayor Pugh stated that the points made in the written comment are appreciated

Public Hearing #2
A Motion to open a Public Hearing to consider Local Law Introductory No.
14 of 2022 increasing the income eligibility level for the senior property
tax exemption was made by TRUSTEE ROSALES. Motion was seconded by
TRUSTEE HOROWITZ and approved with a 5-0 vote.
Chris O’Keefe, 36 Farrington Road, Croton on Hudson, asked what the
income eligibility is being increased to and how is that in par with other
Westchester communities.
Village Attorney Whitehead stated that New York State sets the maximum
income limits, and the Village is proposing the $50,000 a year limit, this
amount has not been increased in over ten years, and most municipalities
throughout Westchester are doing the same.
There being no further comments to come before the Board a motion to
close the Public Hearing was made by Trustee Rosales. Motion was
seconded by Trustee Simon and approved with a 5-0 vote.
Resolution #148-2022
On motion of TRUSTEE HOROWITZ, seconded by TRUSTEE GALLELLI, the following
resolution was adopted by the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-onHudson, New York with a 5-0 vote.

3

Village Trustees Board Meeting
October 17, 2022

WHEREAS pursuant to § 467 of the Real Property Tax Law, the Village of Crotonon-Hudson maintains a senior tax exemption at the maximum level of 50% for
those senior citizens earning $29,000/year or less, and
WHEREAS on August 8, 2022, New York State adopted legislation increasing the
income eligibility level for the senior property tax exemption from $29,000 to
$50,000, and
WHEREAS the option to increase the income eligibility level for the senior property
tax exemption must be exercised through adoption of a local law, ordinance, or
resolution after public hearing, and
WHEREAS Local Law Introductory No. 14 of 2022 has been drafted to reflect such
language, and
WHEREAS a Public Hearing on Local Law Introductory No. 14 was opened and
closed on October 17, 2022,
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED that the Village Board of Trustees hereby
adopts Local Law Introductory No. 14 of 2022 to increase the income eligibility level
for the senior property tax exemption, which upon adoption becomes Local Law No.
11 of 2022.
DISCUSSION:
Trustee Gallelli stated that she believes it is appropriate for the Village to follow the
lead of New York State and our surrounding communities.
Mayor Pugh added that this same New York State Legislation also allows a similar
increase in the maximum eligible income for people with disabilities and the Board
will be exploring that as well.
Village Manager Healy advised that we currently have thirty-five households
participating in the current program and our best estimate is that this may double
the number of those eligible to participate.
