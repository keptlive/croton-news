The following resolution was adopted by the Board of Trustees:
On motion of TRUSTEE GALLELLI, seconded by TRUSTEE MURTAUGH, the following
resolution was adopted by the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, New York
with the following roll call vote: Trustee Horowitz Aye, Trustee Murtaugh Aye, Trustee Gallelli
Aye, Trustee Simon Aye, Mayor Pugh Aye.
Resolution #95-2021
WHEREAS, Local Law No. 3 of 2003 was approved at a regular Village Board meeting held on
October 7, 2003; and
WHEREAS, this local law called for the setting of Village fees through board resolutions; and
WHEREAS, the Board of Trustees would like to amend the Fee Schedule to revise the fees for
vendors selling in Village parks,
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED: that the Village Board of Trustees hereby adopts the
following fee schedule in relation to vendors selling in Village parks:
Vendor selling alcoholic beverages: $200 per day.
Vendor selling food or other items: $100 for first day, $200 for two days, $250 for three
days, $300 for four days, $325 for five days, $350 for six days, $375 for seven days and
$400 for eight days.
Also discussed at this work session was the proposed Indian Brook-Croton Gorge Watershed
Overlay Zone, a proposed mural at Senasqua Park, the survey results regarding the residential
parking permit system on Young and Hastings Avenues and the Marijuana Regulation &
Taxation Act and its possible impact on Croton-on-Hudson.
