A Work Session of the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, NY was held on
Wednesday, March 20, 2024, at 7:00 PM in the Georgianna Grant Meeting Room of the Stanley H.
Kellerhouse Municipal Building.
The following officials were present:
Mayor Pugh
Trustee Murtaugh
Trustee Simon
Trustee Nicholson
Trustee Politi

Village Manager Healy (via Zoom)
Emily Giovanni, Assistant to the
Village Manager
Linda Whitehead, Village Attorney

Discussed at the work session was the Conservation Advisory Council's submission on amendments
to Chapter 208, Trees, of the Village Code, discussion on adopting legislation to establish an Indian
Brook-Croton Gorge Inter-Municipal Watershed Protection Overlay District, and the survey results
on the residential parking permit system near the Croton River.
The following resolution was adopted by the Board of Trustees at the work session:
On motion of TRUSTEE SIMON, seconded by TRUSTEE NICHOLSON, the following resolution
was adopted by the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton- on-Hudson, New York, with a 5-0
vote:
Resolution #56-2024
WHEREAS, the Village of Croton-on-Hudson has the practice of establishing Annual Legislative
Priorities to streamline responses to legislative proposals affecting the Village and optimize the
allocation of limited advocacy resources; and
WHEREAS, the Village Board of Trustees adopted Resolution #54-2024 on March 13, 2024 to
approve a list of 2024 Legislative Program and Project Priorities; and
WHEREAS, the Village Board of Trustees desires to update its adopted 2024 Legislative Program
and Project Priorities to reflect its support for New York State Senate Bill S2966, requiring certain
watercraft, aircraft, and trains to be zero emissions, the study of certain clean energy sources, and
certain monetary incentives for clean energy vehicles,
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED: that the Village of Croton-on-Hudson adopts and
approves the updated 2024 Legislative Program and Project Priorities attached and incorporated
herein by this reference,
AND BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED: that the Village Clerk is hereby directed to send this
resolution and legislative packet to the federal, state and county legislators representing the Village
of Croton-on-Hudson.
Dated: March 20, 2024
Respectfully Summitted,
Emily Giovanni, Assistant to the Village Manager
