An Executive Session of the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, New York,
was held on June 25 2025, in the Georgianna Grant Meeting Room of the Stanley H. Kellerhouse
Municipal Building.
Present:
Mayor Pugh
Trustee Simon
Trustee Nicholson
Trustee Slippen
Village Manager Bryan Healy
Trustee Politi was absent.
A motion to enter into an executive session to discuss matters of personnel involving specific
individuals was made by Trustee Simon. The motion was seconded by Trustee Nicholson and
approved with a 4-0 vote at 9:25 PM.
No formal action was taken during this session.
On a motion by Trustee Simon, seconded by Trustee Nicholson, the Board voted unanimously to
adjourn the executive session at 9:48 PM.
Submitted by:
Bryan Healy, Village Manager
