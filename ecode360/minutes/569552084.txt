Reports of the Chiefs
P. Dinkler (2081)
- Thank you to everyone for their participation in recent alarm responses.
- PPE is available for membership and should be used for COVID-19 prevention.
- Do not go to the scene of any incident and follow the directives that have been put in place
regarding COVID-19 precautions.
- R. Cusano brought up SCBA/Mask/Beard issue, a discussion followed.
J. Munson (2082)
- Members that are having trouble coping with current pandemic, that are stressed or
depressed can contact him to be referred to someone to help them. Please don’t hold things in
and seek assistance if you need it.
- E120 is currently at HVFE for routine maintenance and repairs. E119, T10, E118, R18 & TL44
all recently received repairs and are back. U14 will go out next for some work. R. Cusano
brought up TL44 front end issue still seems to exist.
- Members should go to the www.perma.org website and take annually required training now to
get it done. Other websites and online training encouraged. Send certificates/proof of
completion to Department Secretary for filing/credit.
- 2020-2021 Budget has been adopted, new radio project has been approved. All apparatus will
receive new fireground radios and chargers.
- Marine 12 is being worked on and should be in service soon.
