DISCUSSION
Trustee Horowitz referred to the General Orders Manual and asked the Village
Manager to explain the difference between “Fitness for Duty” (#13) which states
that a member does not consume intoxicants to the extent that a member
becomes unfit for duty” and “Performance on Duty-Prohibited Conduct (#40)

Village Board of Trustees
July 10, 2023
Page 4

which states that “consuming intoxicants while in uniform on or off duty” is
prohibited.
General Orders Manual:
https://play.champds.com/ATT/crotononhudsonny/202306/f3b619a6fdba293792e234e5e99ad366099d10e7.pdf
Village Manager Healy explained that #40 is when an officer is off duty and is
still in uniform, they would not be allowed to go to a bar and start drinking, and
#13 is that an officer cannot show up for duty intoxicated or impaired.
Trustee Nicholson asked if the Village Board needs to reaffirm the rules and
regulations if the Chief makes a change to the Manual.
Village Manager Healy advised that the Chief is empowered to update the
General Orders Manual as needed, the changes are made by the Chief and then
shared with the Department and each member must acknowledge in writing that
they received that change.

7. PUBLIC COMMENTS ON AGENDA ITEMS
Scott Giordano, 11 Hastings Avenue, stated that he enjoyed working on
the LOT A Task Force and is comfortable with what is being proposed.
Signe Bergstrom, 228 Hessian Hills Road, Croton on Hudson, stated
that she looks forward to the process outlined in the proposal from
Televate, but she continues to be concerned that the Village’s Code
covering cell towers does not protect residents and that the draft code
submitted to the Board last year has not yet been considered. Ms.
Bergstrom stated that she is also concerned that the Citizen Advisory
Committee could be diminished if the Board approves this proposal.
Ms. Bergstrom stated that Federal law grants State and Local
Governments the general authority to regulate the placement and
operation of cell towers within their jurisdictions and encouraged the
Board to enact regulations to give them the powers to do so. Ms.
Bergstrom advised that she along with several of her neighbors hired
Mr. Campanelli, a respected attorney in the field of cell towers, and
encouraged the Board to review Mr. Campanelli’s recommendations.
Trustee Simon stated that his expectation is that we will have a full
review of the Code as part of Televate’s process, but he would also like
them to take a look at the work that Mr. Campanelli has done on behalf
of the neighbors.
Village Attorney Whitehead stated that their firm has been working with

Resolution 164 of 2023
On motion of TRUSTEE SIMON, seconded by TRUSTEE HOROWITZ, the following
resolution was adopted by the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-onHudson, New York, with a 5-0 vote.
WHEREAS the Village of Croton-on-Hudson desires to improve wireless services
in the Village, and
WHEREAS on May 18, 2023, the Village issued a Request for Proposals (RFP)
seeking a professional consultant to complete a Wireless Infrastructure Master
Plan Study to advise the Village on potential options to improve wireless
infrastructure and make recommendations for updating the Village Code
regarding Telecommunications, and
WHEREAS a total of three responses to the RFP were received by the due date of
June 8, 2023, and
WHEREAS the Village Manager has reviewed the proposals and provided
references, and recommends that Televate be selected to conduct the Wireless
Infrastructure Master Plan Study for the Village of Croton-on-Hudson,
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED that the Village Board of Trustees hereby
authorizes the Village Manager to accept the proposal from Televate in the
amount of $31,253.
DISCUSSION:
Trustee Simon stated that this is a strong proposal that he hopes will ensure
that whatever we do will be the best solution possible for the community and for

Village Board of Trustees
July 10, 2023
Page 8

our first responders who significantly rely on this technology. Trustee Simon
stated that he likes their approach of working with the public and our Task Force
as well as putting together a public survey so everyone can report to them what
their own issues are with respect to cellular service.
Trustee Nicholson stated that the proposal seems to be very thorough and
supports surveying Village residents as well as updating our code to include 5G
and small cells and asked the Village Manager to address the issue of the Village
providing locations for potential additional sites.
Village Manager Healy stated that there are a number of sites that we have
identified as being potential locations and Televate will look at those in greater
detail.
