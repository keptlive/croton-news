VILLAGE FLOWER VOTE / RE-VOTE
A. The revote of the EARTH DAY flower election via the village newsletter yielded a
landslide DAFFODIL win.
1. 32 Phlox
2. 32 Black-Eyed Susan
3. 33 Violet
4. 34 Sunflower
5. 56 Daffodils
B. However, a robust discussion ensued as to whether the EARTH DAY votes
should be added VILLAGE NEWSLETTER votes before certification, and
whether the convention delegates from Earth Day could or should be released to
vote as they pleased. After much debate, the extraordinary support for the
Daffodil caucus and the bulb coalition became clear and all other candidates
literally went to seed, except for the Phlox, which, citing but not proving election
irregularities, vowed to run again in 2028.
