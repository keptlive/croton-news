# Croton Village Board Shelves Court Study After Resident Pushback
### Residents voiced strong opposition to a proposed study on consolidating the Croton-on-Hudson Justice Court with the Town of Cortlandt.

The Croton-on-Hudson Board of Trustees voted down a proposal to study the efficiency of the village's Justice Court at their meeting on March 11, 2026, after hearing strong opposition from residents. The proposal, which would have cost $19,750, was met with concerns about potential loss of local services and increased expenses for residents and police. The vote was 0-5 against the resolution.

Several residents spoke during the public comment period, urging the board to reconsider the study. Ali Jaffery, of 72 Irving Avenue, a candidate for Village Justice, opposed moving court services to Cortlandt. Casey Raskob, Village Prosecutor, argued that the court operates efficiently and returns money to the village through fines.

"It is the Board’s intent to put this aside and how and under what circumstances the Board returns to it is unknown, but there is no proposal for studying the operations of the court at this time," said Trustee Simon. Mayor Pugh stated that he does not foresee proposing any study in the future.

### What's Next
*   The Board of Trustees will continue to discuss ways to improve village services and efficiency.

# Croton Approves $1.8 Million Fire Engine Purchase
### The Croton-on-Hudson Fire Department will replace a 20-year-old pumper with a new Seagrave Marauder.

The Croton-on-Hudson Board of Trustees approved the purchase of a new fire engine at their March 11, 2026 meeting. The Seagrave Marauder pumper will replace Engine 119, a 2006 Seagrave pumper. The total cost is $1,789,929.

The purchase is in compliance with Houston-Galveston Area Council (HGAC) Contract FS12-23D01, of which the Village is a member organization. According to the resolution, payment for the new Engine 119 will be due upon delivery of the apparatus to the Village of Croton-on-Hudson.

"Trustee Simon advised that we additionally learned that it will take about four years from the time we put in the order till it is delivered," according to the meeting minutes.

# Croton to Upgrade Village Website with New Features
### The Village of Croton-on-Hudson is upgrading its website to a new platform with enhanced capabilities, including a Citizen Request Tracker.

The Croton-on-Hudson Board of Trustees approved an $8,000 upgrade to the village website at their March 11, 2026 meeting. The upgrade, provided by CivicPlus of Manhattan, Kansas, will include a new platform and added capabilities, such as a Citizen Request Tracker. The village website was last redesigned in 2018.

"Trustee Slippen stated that our website is an integral part of our overall communications strategy and she is glad that we are taking the necessary steps to keep it up to date," according to the meeting minutes.

The board authorized the Village Treasurer to transfer $8,000 from Contingency Account A1990.4000 to Publicity – Contractual account A6410.4000.

# Croton Approves Waterfront Consistency Review for Half Moon Bay Bridge Project
### The Croton-on-Hudson Board of Trustees has declared the proposed rehabilitation of the Half Moon Bay Bridge consistent with the Village’s Local Waterfront Revitalization Program.

At the March 11, 2026 meeting, the Croton-on-Hudson Board of Trustees reviewed the proposed project for consistency with the policies of the LWRP. The Waterfront Advisory Committee (WAC) evaluated the Coastal Assessment Form and the policies of the LWRP and determined that several policies are relevant to the proposed project.

The WAC found that the specifications for the proposed work are protective of the environment and that the project will extend the life of the infrastructure in a manner consistent with the LWRP.
