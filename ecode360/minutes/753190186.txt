Recreation Advisory Committee Meeting
Monday, January 13, 2025
Present:

Debi Braddick, Emily Boglioli, John Sasso, Karen Pecora,
Francis Catanio, Pete Sedlmair, Kyle Raum, Nora Nicholson,
John Bouchard and Mark Duncan.

Meeting Called to Order: The meeting opened at 7:00 p.m. by Debi. And the
Committee welcomed new member John Sasso
Approval of Minutes: The December minutes were approved.
Master Fee Schedule: The Recreation Advisory Board reviewed the master
fee schedule for the Recreation Department. The Recreation Advisory
Committee agreed that the pricing for Day Camp and Tiny Tots Camp
should be raised ($10/$5 per session and $25/$5 for full season). The
Recreation Advisory Committee felt that all of the other fees were adequate.
Bench Donation @ Black Rock: A local Girl Scout troop is interested in
donating a bench at Black Rock Park for one of there projects. They would
like to put the bench either near the Bocce Court or the designated spot
facing the river (near the building). The Recreation Advisory Committee
thought that it would be great if the bench could be placed by the Bocce
Courts so there could be seating for those using the courts. Once we hear
back from the Girl Scouts, we will proceed with the bench.
Miscellaneous: The next meeting will be at 7:00pm on February 10th and be
held in the main meeting back room. This is because of the early voting
taking place in the Community Room from February 1st – 12th.
Adjournment: The meeting was adjourned at 7:405 p.m.
The next regular meeting is scheduled for Tuesday, February 10th, 2025 @ 7:00
p.m.
