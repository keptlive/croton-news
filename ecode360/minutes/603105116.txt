An Executive Session of the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, NY was held on
Thursday, May 13, 2021 at the Stanley H. Kellerhouse Municipal Building, 1 Van Wyck Street, Crotonon-Hudson, NY 10520.
On a motion by Trustee Horowitz, seconded by Trustee Gallelli, the Board voted unanimously to enter
into an executive session at 5:32 PM for discussions related to personnel.
The following officials were present:
Mayor Pugh
Trustee Gallelli
Trustee Horowitz
Trustee Murtaugh
Trustee Simon
Village Manager Janine King
Secretary to the Village Manager Bryan Healy
One invited guest was also present. No action was taken.
On a motion by Trustee Gallelli, seconded by Trustee Simon, the Board voted unanimously to adjourn the
Executive Session at 6:35 PM.
Submitted by,
Bryan Healy, Secretary to the Village Manager
