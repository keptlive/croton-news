An Executive Session of the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, NY was held on
Monday, August 30, 2021 at the Stanley H. Kellerhouse Municipal Building, 1 Van Wyck Street, Crotonon-Hudson, NY 10520.
On a motion by Trustee Gallelli, seconded by Trustee Murtaugh, the Board voted unanimously to enter
into an executive session at 8:19 PM regarding matters of personnel.
The following officials were present:
Mayor Pugh
Trustee Gallelli
Trustee Horowitz
Trustee Murtaugh
Trustee Simon
Village Manager Bryan Healy
Chief of Police John Nikitopoulos
No action was taken.
On a motion by Trustee Simon, seconded by Trustee Murtaugh, the Board voted unanimously to adjourn
the Executive Session at 8:39 PM.
Submitted by,
Bryan Healy, Village Manager
