An Executive Session of the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, NY was
held on Monday, May 8, 2023, in the Georgianna Grant Meeting Room of the Stanley H.
Kellerhouse Municipal Building.
On a motion by Trustee Horowitz, seconded by Trustee Nicholson, the Board voted unanimously
to enter into an executive session at 8:28 PM to discuss matters of personnel related to specific
individuals.
The following officials were present:
Mayor Pugh
Trustee Gallelli
Trustee Horowitz
Trustee Nicholson
Village Manager Healy
No action was taken.
On a motion by Trustee Nicholson, seconded by Trustee Horowitz, the Board voted unanimously
to adjourn the Executive Session at 9:37 PM
Submitted by,
Bryan Healy, Village Manager
