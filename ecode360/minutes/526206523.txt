A Work Session of the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, NY was held on Monday,
January 6, 2020 at 7:30 PM at the Municipal Building, 1 Van Wyck Street, Croton-on-Hudson, NY
10520.
The following officials were present:
Mayor Pugh
Trustee Habib
Trustee Simmons
Village Manager King

Trustee Gallelli
Trustee Horowitz
Treasurer Bullock

The following resolution was adopted by the Board of Trustees at the work session:
On motion of TRUSTEE GALLELLI, seconded by TRUSTEE HABIB, the following resolution was
adopted unanimously by the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, New York:
WHEREAS, the Hudson Valley Gateway Chamber of Commerce and the Croton Business Council would
like to host a jazz and blues event entitled “Big Easy on the Hudson”,
BE IT RESOLVED: that the Board of Trustees hereby approves the event with the following stipulations:
1. The event shall be held on Saturday, April 25th, from noon to 7 PM; the rain date shall be
Saturday, May 9th.
2. The event shall be held on Grand Street between Old Post Road South and Mount Airy Road.
3. The event organizers shall be responsible for the full cost of the police and DPW overtime as
outlined by the Chief of Police and Superintendent of Public Works.
4. The vehicle usage fees shall be waived for this event.
Respectfully Submitted,
Janine King, Village Manager
