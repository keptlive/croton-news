V I L L A G E OF CROTON-ON-HUDSON
BOARD OF TRUSTEES
LOCAL LAW NO. 08-2024
A LOCAL LAW AMENDING CHAPTER 108, DOGS, AND CHAPTER 168,
PARKS AND RECREATION AREAS, TO PERMIT LEASHED DOGS IN GOUVEIA
PARK
Be it enacted by the Board of Trustees ofthe Village of Croton-on-Hudson as follows:
Section One. Section 108-6, Regulations and restrictions, of the Code of the Village of Crotonon-Hudson is hereby amended to add a new Subsection F. (3) to read as follows:
F. Be on any public park, playground, ballfield, or school property or on the property of another
person without the consent of such other person, whether or not restrained by a chain or leash;
provided, however, that the following exceptions apply:
(3) Gouveia Park: Dog owners may walk their leashed dogs, with a leash no more than six feet
in length, within Gouveia Park only during posted hours that the park is open.
Section Two. Section 168-8, Prohibited acts, of the Code of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson is
hereby amended to add a new Subsection D. (3) to read as follows:
D. Permit or allow any dog or other domestic animal within the aforesaid facilities; provided,
however, that the following exceptions apply:
(3) Gouveia Park: Dog ovmers may walk their leashed dogs, with a leash no more than six
feet in length, within Gouveia Park only during posted hours that the park is open.
Section Three. Severability
If any section, subsection, clause, phrase or other portion of this Local Law is, for any reason,
declared invalid, in whole or in part, by any court, agency, commission, legislative body or other
authority of competent jurisdiction, such portion shall be deemed a separate, distinct and
independent portion. Such declaration shall not affect the validity of the remaining portions
hereof, which other portions shall continue in lull force and effect.
Section Four, Effective Date
This local law shall take effect immediately upon filing in the office of the New York State
Secretary of State in accordance with section 27 of the Municipal Home Rule Law.

On motion of TRUSTEE SIMON, seconded by TRUSTEE NICHOLSON, the
following resolution was adopted by the Board of Trustees of the Village of Crotonon-Hudson, New York with a 5-0 vote.
Resolution #69-2024
WHEREAS in accordance with the recommendation put forth by the Recreation
Advisory Committee, the Village of Croton-on-Hudson would like to amend Chapter
108, Dogs, and 168, Parks and Recreation Areas, ofthe Village Code to permit leashed
dogs in Gouveia Park, and
WHEREAS Local Law Introductory No. 6 of2024 was drafted for such purposes, and
WHEREAS the Village Board held a public hearing to consider Local Law
Introductory No. 6 of 2024, which was opened and closed on April 10, 2024,
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED that the Village Board of Trustees hereby
adopts Local Law Introductory No. 6 of 2024, to amend Chapters 108 and 168
of the Village Code to permit leashed dogs in Gouveia Park, which upon adoption
becomes Local Law No. 8 of 2024.
