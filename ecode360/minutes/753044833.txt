An Executive Session of the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, NY was held
on Thursday, April 18, 2024, in the Georgianna Grant Meeting Room of the Stanley H.
Kellerhouse Municipal Building.
On a motion by Trustee Simon, seconded by Trustee Nicholson, the Board voted unanimously to
enter into an executive session at 9:04 PM to discuss the proposed acquisition, sale or lease of real
property when publicity would substantially affect the value thereof.
The following officials were present:
Mayor Pugh
Trustee Murtaugh
Trustee Nicholson
Trustee Politi
Trustee Simon
Village Manager Healy
No action was taken.
On a motion by Trustee Simon, seconded by Trustee Murtaugh, the Board voted unanimously to
adjourn the Executive Session at 9:12 PM.
Submitted by,
Bryan Healy, Village Manager
