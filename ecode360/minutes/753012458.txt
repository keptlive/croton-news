An Executive Session of the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, NY was
held on Wednesday, January 10, 2024, in the Georgianna Grant Meeting Room of the Stanley H.
Kellerhouse Municipal Building.

On a motion by Trustee Simon, seconded by Trustee Politi, the Board voted unanimously to
enter into an executive session at 9:30 PM to discuss matters of personnel related to specific
individuals.

The following officials were present:

       Mayor Pugh
       Trustee Murtaugh
       Trustee Nicholson
       Trustee Politi
       Trustee Simon
       Village Manager Healy

No action was taken.

On a motion by Trustee Simon, seconded by Trustee Murtaugh, the Board voted unanimously to
adjourn the Executive Session at 9:51 PM.


Submitted by,
Bryan Healy, Village Manager
