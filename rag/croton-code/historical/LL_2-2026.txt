WIIEREAS, under this law, the total amount to be raiseð through property taxes chargeð on the

whichever is less, with some exceptions; and
WFIEREAS, the state Iegis1ation provides for local govermnents to override the cap to protect the

WIIEREAS, Local Law Introductory No. 1 of2026 has been drafied to override the property tax
cap law enacted by State Legislature if necessary; and

upcoming fiscal year; and
