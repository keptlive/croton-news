An Executive Session of the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, NY was
held on Tuesday, May 3, 2022, in the Georgianna Grant Meeting Room of the Stanley H.
Kellerhouse Municipal Building.
On a motion by Trustee Horowitz, seconded by Trustee Simon, the Board voted unanimously to
enter into an executive session at 8:19 PM for discussions related to matters of personnel.
The following officials were present:
Mayor Pugh
Trustee Gallelli
Trustee Horowitz
Trustee Rosales
Trustee Simon
Village Manager Bryan Healy
No action was taken.
On a motion by Trustee Simon, seconded by Trustee Rosales, the Board voted unanimously to
adjourn the Executive Session at 8:40 PM.
Submitted by,
Bryan Healy, Village Manager
