1. CALL TO ORDER:
Acting Chairman Krisky called the Planning Board meeting of December 14, 2021 to
order at 8:00 p.m.
2. NEW BUSINESS:
a) Weekes, Matilda – 50 Young Avenue (Sec. 67.19 Block 6 Lot 5) – Application for
approval of an accessory apartment
PRESENT: Ms. Matilda Weekes was present in support of the application. Ms. Weekes
is requesting an accessory apartment to rent for profit. Mr. Haynes asked if she was a
new homeowner and Ms. Weeks said that she is. The apartment has never before
been rented. There is no tenant in the space right now. The square footage of the
space is within the guidelines. There is plenty of parking and Ms. Weeks’ family has
one car. Acting Chairman Krisky confirmed that the neighbors within 100 feet have
been notified of the application. There were no comments or questions from neighbors
in response to the mailing that was sent out.
MOTION: Mr. Haynes made a motion to approve the application, barring conditions
number 5 and 6 as they do not apply. The motion was seconded by Mr. Ghegan, and
carried by a vote of 3-0.
