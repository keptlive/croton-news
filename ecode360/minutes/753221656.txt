A Work Session of the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, NY was held on
Wednesday, January 14, 2026, at 7:00 PM in the Georgianna Grant Meeting Room of the Stanley
H. Kellerhouse Municipal Building.

The following officials were present:

             Mayor Pugh                 Trustee Slippen
             Trustee Nachtaler          Village Manager Bryan Healy
             Trustee Nicholson          Village Attorney Joshua Subin
             Trustee Simon

On motion of TRUSTEE SIMON, seconded by TRUSTEE NICHOLSON, the Board of Trustees of
the Village of Croton-on-Hudson approved the following board and committee appointments by a
vote of 5-0:

 Eva Thaddeus             Conservation Advisory Council                   December 2027
                          Chair                                           December 2026
 Ellen Elchlepp           Arts & Humanities Advisory Council              December 2026
 Lenore Edman             Arts & Humanities Advisory Council              December 2027
 James Nadeau             Bicycle-Pedestrian Committee                    December 2027
 Dan Ozaruk               Bicycle-Pedestrian Committee                    December 2028
 Manuel Coronel Jr.       Police Advisory Committee                       December 2026
 Jan Johnsen              Advisory Board for the Visual Environment       December 2027

During the work session, the Board also discussed their site visit to the DPW Garage with
Superintendent of Public Works Frank Balbi, received a presentation on the Village’s bond rating
from financial advisor Beth Ferguson, reviewed the draft budget priorities survey for the upcoming
fiscal year, discussed the recommendations presented by the Recreation Advisory Committee and
discussed a proposed local law to create the local office of Village Auditor.

Respectfully submitted,
