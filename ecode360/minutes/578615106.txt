2.2

QUALIFICATIONS

All members seeking to hold the position of Second Assistant Chief, First Assistant Chief or Chief on
January 1, 2013, but prior to holding the same position on or after January 1, 2019 must meet the
following qualifications, unless he or she has held this position previously.

2.2A

QUALIFICATIONS OF SECOND ASSISTANT CHIEF

In order to be nominated for the office of Second Assistant Chief, the nominee must meet the following
requirements;

1. Reside within the Village of Croton-on-Hudson or the Fire Protection Districts covered by
the Croton Volunteer Fire Department.
2. Complete one full year as Captain from the nominating company.
3. Completion of NYS Fire Officer I (or equivalent determined by NYS OFPC).
4. Completion of NYS Fire Behavior & Arson Awareness (or equivalent determined by NYS
OFPC).

5. Completion of any three of the following courses:
a. Accident Victim Extrication Training
b. Aircraft Rescue & Fire Fighting
c. Truck Company Operations or Ladder Company Operations
d. Rescue Technician – Basic or Rescue Operations
e. Firefighter II or Advanced Firefighter Training
f. Firefighter Assist & Search Team
g. Hazardous Materials Technician – Basic
h. Completion of NIMS I-300
NOTE: Any OFPC or NFA Officer Development Course or fire training course may be substituted for
any of the above.
2.2B QUALIFICATIONS OF FIRST ASSISTANT CHIEF
In order to be nominated for the office of First Assistant Chief, the nominee must meet the following
requirements;
1. Complete one full year as Second Assistant Chief.
2. Reside within the Village of Croton-on-Hudson or the Fire Protection Districts covered by
the Croton Volunteer Fire Department.
3. Completion of any three of the following courses:
a. Accident Victim Extrication Training
b. Aircraft Rescue & Fire Fighting
c. Truck Company Operations or Ladder Company Operations

New York State Certificates of other Nationally Recognized Training will be accepted for members who
want to be nominated for Officer’s positions. Should a nominee have training certification from another
State, then those out-of-state certificates must be presented to the Fire Council prior to nominations for
approval.

3.0

LINE OFFICERS

The Line Officers of the various companies of the Croton Volunteer Fire Department will consist of a
Captain, at least one Lieutenant, and no more than two Lieutenants. Each Company may elect one
Engineer per apparatus (not including Marine 52); however the Engineers will not fall into the Chain of
Command of the membership, as they will only be responsible for their apparatus and its corresponding
equipment.

3.1

NOMINATIONS AND ELECTION

All newly elected Line Officers will assume their respective offices on January 1st (00:00:00 hours) of
each year, and a term is one year, ending on December 31st (23:59:59 hours).
There is no limit to the number of years that an individual may serve as a company officer.

All members seeking to hold the position of Engineer, Second Lieutenant, First Lieutenant or Captain

3.2

ENGINEER

An Engineer for each of that company’s apparatus may be elected each year in accordance with the
above terms of office. In order to be nominated for hold the position of Engineer, a member must meet
the following requirements;

A. Be an active member of the Company to which the appointment is being made.
B. Be at least 21 years of age.
C. Be a member in good standing of their company for no less than two years.
D. Served as a Driver on the assigned apparatus for no less than one year.
E. Possess a valid NYS Driver’s License.
F. Qualify as either an Exterior or Interior Firefighter.
In addition to the above, they must also have completed the following training prior to being appointed
elected the position of Engineer;

A. NYS Apparatus Operator – Emergency Vehicle Operations or equivalent.
B. NYS Apparatus Operator – Pump or equivalent (Engine & Ladder Companies only).
C. NYS Apparatus Operator – Aerial Devices or equivalent (Ladder Company only).
The Engineer will be held responsible to the Captain for the care, condition, and cleanliness of the
apparatus and other equipment assigned to the Company.

3.3

LIEUTENANT

Each company may choose to elect up to two Lieutenants each year. In order to be nominated hold the
position for the office of Lieutenant, a member must meet the following requirements;
A. Be at least 21 years of age.
B. Be a member in Good Standing of their company for no less than two years.
C. Be an Interior Firefighter as outlined in Article 7 of the department’s By-Laws.
In addition to the above, they must also have completed the following training prior to assuming the
position of Lieutenant;

A. NYS Intro to Fire Officer (or equivalent determined by NYS OFPC).
B. NIMS I-200.
C. Completion of any one of the following:
1. Accident Victim Extrication Training
2. Aircraft Rescue & Fire Fighting
3. Truck Company Operations or Ladder Company Operations
4. Rescue Technician – Basic or Rescue Operations
5. Firefighter II or Advanced Firefighter Training
6. Firefighter Assist & Search Team
7. Hazardous Materials Technician – Basic
NOTE: Any OFPC or NFA Officer Development Course or fire training course
may be substituted for any of the above with the exception of NIMS I-200, which

3.4

CAPTAIN

In order to hold the position be nominated for the office of Captain, a member must meet the following
requirements;

A. Be at least 21 years of age.
B. Be a member in Good Standing of their company for no less than two years.
C. Be an Interior Firefighter as outlined in Article 7 of the department’s By-Laws.
D. Complete no less than two one years as a Lieutenant in their company prior to taking office
being nominated.
In addition to the above, they must also have completed the following training prior to assuming the
position of Captain;

A. Completion of Lieutenant training requirements for that company.
B. Completion of no less than two full terms as Lieutenant.
C. Completion of any two of the following:
1. Accident Victim Extrication Training
2. Aircraft Rescue & Fire Fighting
3. Truck Company Operations or Ladder Company Operations
4. Rescue Technician – Basic or Rescue Operations
5. Firefighter II or Advanced Firefighter Training
6. Firefighter Assist & Search Team
7. Hazardous Materials Technician – Basic
NOTE: Any OFPC or NFA Officer Development Course or fire training course
may be substituted for any of the above. Any other course that a member
completes that is not listed above must be approved by the Fire Council.
Captains of companies will have absolute command of same, be responsible for the condition of
apparatus and quarters, and the training of company personnel.
Captains will authorize, with permission from the Chief, all drivers within their company. They will
oversee new driver training and, working with the Engineer(s), ensure all drivers complete annual
requalification.
Captains will deliver reports to the Chief as required.
