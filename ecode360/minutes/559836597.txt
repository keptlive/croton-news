An Executive Session of the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, NY was held on
Monday, August 3, 2020 via Zoom videoconference, pursuant to Executive Order 202.1 issued by
Governor Andrew Cuomo on March 12, 2020.
On a motion by Trustee Habib, seconded by Trustee Simmons, the Board voted unanimously to enter into
an executive session at 5:35 PM to discuss matters of personnel and the lease of real property.
The following officials were present:
Mayor Pugh
Trustee Gallelli
Trustee Habib
Trustee Horowitz
Trustee Simmons
Village Manager Janine King
Secretary to the Manager Bryan Healy
Village Attorney Linda Whitehead
No action was taken.
On a motion by Trustee Gallelli, seconded by Trustee Simmons, the Board voted unanimously to adjourn
the Executive Session at 6:25 PM.
Submitted by,
Bryan Healy, Secretary to the Village Manager
