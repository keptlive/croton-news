Meeting Notes: Idea Committee
Date: 1/9
Attendees: Cristina, Will, Millie, Cara, Kyla, Mr. Riley, Romy, Vanessa, Faith

Key Points
•

•

•

•

•

Community Incident: A racist incident occurred on December 11th involving Millie's
children at Shoprite. This incident has generated significant community support and
highlighted underlying issues of racism and discrimination in Croton.
"Croton Welcomes Everyone" Initiative: The committee has an existing initiative,
"Croton Welcomes Everyone," which aims to create safe spaces for people of
diverse backgrounds. This initiative faces limitations due to its status as a village
committee, preventing direct outreach and mandating business participation.
"Croton Loves All" Campaign Proposal: Millie proposed a "Croton Loves All"
campaign, aiming to build belonging through love, using public visibility (signs,
decals, apparel), digital presence, and partnerships. This campaign is a direct
response to the racist incident and seeks to promote healing and visibility.
Budget Limitations: The Idea Committee has a remaining budget of $200 for the
current fiscal year (ending May 30th). Future initiatives requiring significant funding
will need to be part of the next fiscal year's budget request.
Public Comment Protocol: A decision was made to establish a public comment
period at future meetings to address interruptions.

Decisions Made
•

•

•
•

Public Comment Protocol: The committee will establish a public comment period
at future meetings, allowing non-committee members three minutes to address
agenda items. This rule will be publicly posted.
"Croton Loves All" Campaign Support: The Idea Committee, as a government
organization, cannot directly run Millie's "Croton Loves All" campaign or provide
direct financial resources for it. However, individual committee members can
support it as private citizens, and the Village Board can endorse the campaign
through a support statement or proclamation.
Budget Request for FY 26/27: The committee will apply for a budget for fiscal year
2026/2027 by the end of February.
Next Meeting Focus: The next meeting will be a working session to discuss event
planning and budget requests for the upcoming fiscal year.

•

Lunar New Year Event: The committee will explore organizing a Lunar New Year
celebration, specifically looking into bringing in a lion dancer from the Asian Arts
New York Chinese Cultural Center.
