An Executive Session of the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, NY was held
on Thursday, February 29, 2024, in the Georgianna Grant Meeting Room of the Stanley H.
Kellerhouse Municipal Building.
On a motion by Trustee Simon, seconded by Trustee Nicholson, the Board voted unanimously to
enter to enter into an Executive Session at 6:32 PM to discuss the proposed acquisition, sale or
lease of real property when publicity would substantially affect the value thereof.
The following officials were present:
Mayor Pugh
Trustee Murtaugh
Trustee Nicholson
Trustee Politi (joined via Zoom)
Trustee Simon
Village Manager Healy
Six invited individuals were also in attendance.
On a motion by Trustee Simon, seconded by Trustee Nicholson, the Board voted unanimously to
adjourn the meeting at 7:51pm.
Submitted by,
Bryan Healy, Village Manager
