A Work Session of the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton­on­Hudson, NY was held on
Wednesday, October 15, 2025, at 7:00 PM in the Georgianna Grant Meeting Room of the Stanley
H. Kellerhouse Municipal Building.
The following officials were present:
Mayor Pugh
Trustee Nicholson
Trustee Simon

Trustee Slippen
(arrived at 7:15 PM)
Village Manager Bryan Healy

Trustee Politi was absent.
On motion of TRUSTEE SIMON, seconded by TRUSTEE NICHOLSON, the following resolution
was adopted by the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton­on­Hudson, New York by a 3­0 vote
(Trustees Politi and Slippen were absent):
Resolution #243­2025
WHEREAS, the Village issued an Invitation to Bid on July 31, 2025, for the installation and
commissioning of a solar canopy at the DPW Garage; and
WHEREAS, this project is to be funded through grants awarded to the Village by the New York
State Energy Development and Research Authority (NYSERDA); and
WHEREAS, one bid was received at the time of the bid opening on September 4, 2025; and
WHEREAS, the required bid bond or cash deposit was not submitted, which is considered a material
defect in the bid response,
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED: that the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton­on­
Hudson hereby rejects the bid received on September 4, 2025, for the DPW Garage solar canopy
project.
During the work session, the Board also received an update from Superintendent of Public Works
Frank Balbi on department operations and projects, discussed an expansion of the residential
parking permit system on Hastings and Young avenues, and discussed a proposed Length of
Service Award Program (LOSAP) for Croton EMS.
Respectfully submitted,
Bryan Healy, Village Manager
