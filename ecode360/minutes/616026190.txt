3. PUBLIC HEARINGS
Motion to open a Public Hearing to amend the “Special Permit” application
from “Hudson National Golf Club” for modifications to their ancillary
storage area was made by Trustee Horowitz. Motion was seconded by
Trustee Simon and approved with a 4-0 vote. (Mayor Pugh, not present at
the time of the vote)
Alan Milton (no address given) stated that “Hudson National Golf Club”
has provided documents to the Planning Board that demonstrate that
there has not been any significant changes with respect to either the
original approved disturbance area or the use of an ancillary storage area;
the open storage shed was enlarged in 2014 and is depicted on the
approved 2015 grading plan and the portable storage bins were added
later to prevent loose material from moving around on site; their revised

Village Trustees Board Meeting
July 6, 2021
Page 2

“Site Plan” includes new drainage facilities as well as additional screening
in two areas.
Mr. Milton stated that Chazen’s letter indicates that
“Hudson National Golf Club” has adequately addressed all technical
comments and that they have no further comments on the application.
Ralph Mastromonaco, Consulting Engineer for “Hudson National Golf Club”
stated that the storage area that they are proposing is slightly larger, but
they are planning on adding a stormwater retention basin and an erosion
control basin. Mr. Mastromonaco stated that earlier this year they looked
at the original 1996 drainage study and at that time the area tributary to
Prickly Pear Hill Road was 57 acres, today it is 55.9 acres which indicates
that nothing has changed since 1996. Mr. Mastromonaco stated that
while we were doing research on this project in an attempt to try to find
why residents were complaining that they were seeing unusual amounts
of water, they found that when they built one of the homes on Prickly
Pear Hill Road, they took part of the flow and instead of having it run
down the backyards of those homes, they constructed a device to bring
the flow to the front yards which he believes may explain what is
happening now. Mr. Mastromonaco stated that while “Hudson National
Golf Club” does not think they did anything wrong during that period of
time, they are proposing taking the water and instead of having it run
across Prickly Pear Hill Road, they will create a swale on the North side of
Pricky Pear Hill Road where it will travel down the north side, enter the
drainage system at “Albany Post Road” which should lead to an
improvement for the homes on the south side of Prickly Pear Hill Road
and have no affect on the those on the north side.
Trustee Murtaugh asked how deep is the proposed swale?
Mr. Mastromonaco stated that it will be one foot deep and two feet wide.
Trustee Murtaugh asked if this will be sufficient for the type of flow that
comes down that hill.
Mr. Mastromonaco responded by saying yes, because that gutter will only
be taking part of the flow that comes down the hill; what they are also
proposing when they build the retention basis, is to capture that water

side of their property which will remain undeveloped.
Trustee Murtaugh asked what impact the north side swale would face if

Mr. Mastromonaco stated that they would have to have the same carrying
capacity.
Brett Scales, “Hudson National Golf Club” Superintendent, stated that
since the golf course was built in 1995 the storage area has always been

attention, but he believes the relationship changed after “Hudson National
Golf Club” made an application for solar and then many unfounded
allegations were made.
Spencer Grant, 4 Prickly Pear Hill Road, Croton on Hudson, stated that he
feels that “Hudson National Golf Club” is being mean spirited to the
neighbors, and have skated over the issues and problems that they have

and erosion on Albany Post Road. Mr. Grant stated that the golf club
could put the storage area in another location, but they want it there
because it suits them, but it does not suit the neighbors of Prickly Pear
Hill Road and he does not understand why they cannot put it somewhere
else.
John O’Connell, 115 Old Post Road North, Croton on Hudson, stated that

Trustee Murtaugh wished Treasurer Bullock well on her retirement. Trustee

entrance road to “Van Cortlandt Manor” and thanked Congressman Jones and
his team for including these in this new earmark as well as thanking staff for
preparing the information that was needed. Trustee Simon wished Treasurer
Sandra Bullock the best on her retirement.

the Village and wished her well on her retirement. Trustee Gallelli advised
that the Planning Board has been working hard on a lot of big projects as
