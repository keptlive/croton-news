Recreation Advisory Committee Meeting
Monday, April 14, 2025
Present:

Debi Braddick, Emily Boglioli, Kyle Raum, John Sasso, Pete
Sedlmair, Frances Catanio, Nora Nickleson, Bryan Healy,
Maria Slippen, John Bouchard and Mark Duncan

Meeting Called to Order: The meeting opened at 8:30 p.m. by Debi. This
meeting followed the Dobbs Park Playground Presentation.
Approval of Minutes: The March minutes were approved.
Park Visit Report. The Recreation Advisory Committee visited the Black
Rock Park (Dog park and Bocce area) and the Duck Pond Park. They
reported that both of the parks that they visited looked good. Village
Manager, Bryan Healey mentioned that two benches for the Bocce Court
area have been approved in the 2025-2026 budget.
Scheduling Visits to other Parks: The Recreation Advisory was asked to
visit the rest of the Village’s parks; Senasqua, Harrison Street Playground,
Sunset Park, Gouveia Park, Silver Lake and Vassallo Park. Debi asked that
everyone visit each park in the next couple of months.
Parks Update: Park Foreman, John Bouchard, updated everyone on the
upgrade of the new drainage located on the third base side (outside the
playing area) at Manes field. This will help with the water that is coming
from the wooded area. It is expected to be complete in the next few weeks.
Spring/Summer Registration Update: Mark informed the Recreation
advisory that the Spring/Summer registration is going well. Currently, Day
Camp session 1 & 3 is at capacity (60 per session) with a couple remaining
spots in session 2. Also, 84 of the 90 Canoe/kayak racks at Echo Canoe
Launch are rented with 31 of them being rented at the Senior discount
($180).
Adjournment: The meeting was adjourned at 8:50 p.m.
The next regular meeting is scheduled for Monday, May 12, 2025 @ 7:00 p.m.
