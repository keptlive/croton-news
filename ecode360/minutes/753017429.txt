MEETING SUMMARY

Croton IDEA Advisory 01/08
08 JANUARY 2024 / 10 AM / MUNICIPAL BUILDING CONFERENCE ROOM

The DI Committee meeting centered on topics related to transparency, committee
identity, inclusive communications, community group updates, and community
engagement strategies.
First, the committee emphasized transparency by deciding to produce and share
meeting notes on the Croton website. A rotating note-taker from the committee
would be responsible for taking notes, ensuring accuracy, and facilitating
transparency. The committee also concluded deliberation on a name change,
“Inclusivity, Diversity, Equity, and Accessibility Advisory Committee" (IDEA Advisory
Committee), and a related memo to Village Trustees. They discussed language
adjustments for clarity and planned to propose the name change.
The Committee addressed the need for inclusive and accessible communications,
suggesting the creation of a Village Communications Manager with
Spanish-language competency. Drafting language for the job description and
advocating for its inclusion in postings were agreed upon, along with requesting
transparency into the final job description.
Updates on community groups included supporting a film screening organized by
Croton Pride, addressing gender-inclusive restroom issues, and connecting with
Open Arms to welcome asylum seekers. The committee also proposed a “Listening
Tour" to gather information from diverse community members and prioritize
initiatives.
Concerning issues raised by the community, the committee discussed ways to
support a member concerned about antisemitism. They decided to meet separately
with the individual to understand their goals and needs and explore potential
support mechanisms, considering the Democracy Circles model as a possible tool.
