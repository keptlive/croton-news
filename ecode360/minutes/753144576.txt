A Work Session of the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, NY was held on
Wednesday, April 2, 2025, at 7:00 PM in the Georgianna Grant Meeting Room of the Stanley H.
Kellerhouse Municipal Building.
The following officials were present:
Mayor Pugh
Trustee Slippen
Trustee Simon
Trustee Nicholson

Village Manager Bryan Healy
Village Treasurer Daniel Tucker

The following official was absent:
Trustee Politi
On motion of TRUSTEE SIMON, seconded by TRUSTEE NICHOLSON, the Board of Trustees of
the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, New York, voted 4-0 to refer information on Project Mover,
including a sample local law from the Village of Ossining, to the Bicycle-Pedestrian Committee for
a comprehensive review and report back to the Board of Trustees.
The Board also conducted a review of the Department of Public Works proposed budget for 20252026 at the work session.
Respectfully Summitted,
Bryan Healy, Village Manager
