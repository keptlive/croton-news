CORRESPONDENCE
a. Letter from Vincent Sapienza, Commissioner of the New York City
Department of Environmental Protection dated July 13, 2020 regarding
the water temperature in the Croton River.
Village Manager King stated that in his letter he gives a history of the
Croton Dam and how they released water, in his letter he explained
that for about a twenty year period, when they were building the
filtration system in the Bronx, New York City was not using the water
from the Dam and during that time the water was warmer. Village
Manager King stated that now that New York City is using the water
there is less water to run over the top of the Dam and they are
releasing seventy-five million gallons of water per day because of the
“Hydrilla issue”. Village Manager King stated that it seems that not
much is going to happen this year and next year while they are
treating the “Hydrilla” and hopefully after that period we can start
experiencing a little bit of warmer water.

Resolution #117 of 2020
On motion of TRUSTEE GALLELLI, seconded by TRUSTEE HOROWITZ, the following
resolution was adopted by the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson,
New York with the following Roll Call Vote: Trustee Habib Aye, Trustee Simmons
Aye, Trustee Gallelli Aye, Trustee Horowitz Aye, Mayor Pugh Aye.
WHEREAS, the Village of Croton-on-Hudson has entered into an agreement with
Northwind Kennel of Bedford, New York, to provide shelter for stray dogs on an
annual basis; and
WHEREAS, pursuant to Section 114 of the Agriculture & Markets Law of the State of
New York, each municipality that issues dog licenses shall establish and maintain a
shelter for dogs, or may contract with an agency to provide such service; and
WHEREAS, the cost for such shelter services is $1,500; and
WHEREAS, the term of the agreement is July 2, 2020, through July 1, 2021,
NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED: that the Board of Trustees hereby affirms the
executed agreement with Northwind Kennel of Bedford, New York, to provide
shelter services to the Village for seized and stray dogs at a cost of $1,500 for the
period of July 2, 2020, through July 1, 2021;
AND BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED: that funding for such expense is available in the
following account: A3510.4000.
DISCUSSION:
Trustee Gallelli explained that the reason why we are required to do this is because
the Village issues Dog Licenses.
Village Manager King advised that the SPCA of Westchester County is still shut down
for renovations and that this fee is less than what they were paying the SPCA.

WHEREAS, the Village would like to amend Chapter 168 of the Village Code, Parks
and Recreation Areas, to prohibit the use of remote-controlled aircraft in Villageowned parks; and
WHEREAS, Local Law Introductory No. 6 of 2020 has been drafted to reflect such
language,
NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED: that the Village Board of Trustees hereby calls
for a Public Hearing on August 3, 2020, at 6 PM to consider Local Law Introductory
No. 6 of 2020 to amend Chapter 168 of the Village Code, Parks and Recreation
Areas, and
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED: that such public hearing shall be held via
videoconference in accordance with the rules and regulations outlined in Executive
Order 202.1 issued by Governor Andrew Cuomo.
DISCUSSION:
Village Manager King stated that while this has not been an issue, this is more of a

could cause an issue.
