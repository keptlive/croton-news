LOCAL LAW NO. 6-2020
A LOCAL LAW TO REPEAL LOCAL LAW NUMBER 2 OF 2020 AND TO REPEAL
THE OVERRIDE OF THE TAX LEVY LIMIT ESTABLISHED IN GENERAL
MUNICIPAL LAW §3-c
Be it enacted by the Village of Croton-on-Hudson as follows:
SECTION 1: LEGISLATIVE INTENT: This Local Law shall be known as "Local Law
Number 6 of 2020". It is the intent of this Local Law to repeal Local Law Number 2 of 2020, and
to thus reinstate the limits on the amount of real property taxes that may be levied by the Village
of Croton-on-Hudson pursuant to General Municipal Law §3-c.
SECTION 2: EFFECT: This Local Law hereby thus repeals in its entirety said Local Law
Number 2 of 2020, and declares the same of no force or effect, and thus the Village of Crotonon-Hudson repeals the prior tax cap override local law of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson and
reinstates the tax levy limits imposed by General Municipal Law §3-c.
SECTION 3: SEVERABILITV: If any portion this Local Law, or the application thereof to
any person, entity, or circumstance, shall be determined by any court or tribunal of competent
jurisdiction to be invalid or unenforceable, such determination shall be confined in its operation
to the invalid part hereof, or in its application to such person, entity, or circumstance as is
directly involved in the controversy in which such determination shall have been rendered, and
the remainder of this Local Law shall not be impaired thereby and such determination shall not
be deemed or construed to apply to other persons, entities, or circumstances.
SECTION 4: EFFECTIVE DATE: This Local Law shall take effect immediately.

On motion of TRUSTEE Habib, seconded by TRUSTEE Simmons, the following resolution was
unanimously adopted by the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, New York
with a vote of 5-0.
Resolution #58-2020
WHEREAS, the State Legislature and the Governor enacted legislation that establishes a
"property tax cap" on the amount that a local government's property tax levy can increase each
year; and
WHEREAS, this law has been effective for local govemments since 2012; and
WHEREAS, the state legislation provides for local govemments to override the cap to protect
the Village from unforeseen fmancial circumstances; and
WHEREAS, Local Law No. 2 of 2020 to override the "property tax cap" law was adopted by the
ViUage Board of Trustees at their meeting on January 21, 2020; and
WHEREAS, the Village of Croton-on-Hudson is not anticipating adopting a budget exceeding
the "property tax cap" law enacted by State Legislature and would like to repeal Local Law No.
2 of 2020, and
WHEREAS, a Public Hearing was held and closed on April 13, 2020,
NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED: that the Village Board of Tmstees hereby adopts
Local Law Introductory No. 3 of 2020 to repeal the prior tax cap override Local Law 2 of 2020
and reinstates the tax levy limits imposed by General Municipal Law §3-c which upon adoption
will become Local Law No. 6 of 2020.

Dated: April 13,2020
