On motion of TRUSTEE SIMON, seconded by TRUSTEE POLITI, the following
resolution was adopted by the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-onHudson, New York with a 5-0 vote.
WHEREAS, the Village of Croton-on-Hudson has been working with AT&T
Wireless in consideration of a lease agreement and special permit to colocate a wireless facility at the Stanley H. Kellerhouse Municipal Building, 1
Van Wyck Street; and
WHEREAS, this application is considered an Eligible Facilities Request under
federal law, which requires the Village Board to render a decision within 60 days
of receiving the application; and
WHEREAS, a Short Environmental Assessment Form and a Coastal Assessment
Form have been drafted in connection with the Proposed Action,
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED as follows:
1. The Village Board hereby determines that the Proposed Action is an Unlisted
Action under SEQRA;
2. The Village Board hereby declares itself Lead Agency for SEQRA purposes;
3. The Village Board hereby refers the necessary documentation to the Village
Waterfront Advisory Committee for a recommendation of consistency with the
Village’s Local Waterfront Revitalization Program (LWRP).
AND BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED: that a public hearing on the special permit
application for the co-location of an AT&T Wireless facility at the Stanley H.
Kellerhouse Municipal Building be scheduled for August 20, 2025, at 7:00 PM in
the Georgianna Grant Meeting Room of the Stanley H. Kellerhouse Municipal
Building.
Resolution #184-2025
On motion of TRUSTEE SIMON, seconded by TRUSTEE POLITI, the following
resolution was adopted by the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-onHudson, New York with a 5-0 vote.
WHEREAS, the New York State Legislature has passed three essential bills
during the 2025 Legislative Session related to emergency medical services
(EMS) in New York State; and

Village Board of Trustees Work Session
July 23, 2025
Page 4

WHEREAS, A.2177/S.1515 excludes EMS expenditures from the real property
tax cap, giving municipalities the flexibility to properly fund EMS services
without compromising other critical needs; and
WHEREAS, A.8086-A/S.7501-A requires counties, cities, towns, and villages to
collaboratively develop comprehensive county EMS system plans to assess
needs, identify service gaps, and guide future investments; and
WHEREAS, A.34/S.31 provides toll exemptions on the NYS Thruway for
emergency vehicles, enabling faster emergency response times and reducing
operational costs for EMS providers; and
WHEREAS, the three bills are critical to rescuing and revitalizing EMS across the
state; and
WHEREAS, the New York Conference of Mayors (NYCOM) has circulated a letter
for all municipalities to join as signatories requesting Governor Kathy Hochul to
sign these bills into law without delay,
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED: that the Village of Croton-on-Hudson Board
of Trustees authorizes the Village Manager to join as a signatory to the letter
circulated by NYCOM.

Resolution #186-2025
On motion of TRUSTEE SIMON, seconded by TRUSTEE POLITI, the following
resolution was adopted by the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-onHudson, New York with a 5-0 vote.
RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING SUBMISSION OF A WESTCHESTER COUNTY
STORMWATER PROGRAM GRANT APPLICATION FOR DRAINAGE IMPROVEMENTS
ALONG BROOK STREET AND TERRACE PLACE
WHEREAS, to improve stormwater drainage and reduce flooding, the Village of
Croton-on-Hudson is looking to improve the drainage along Brook Street and
Terrace Place (“Project”); and
WHEREAS, the Village of Croton-on-Hudson is seeking funding under the
“Westchester County Flood Mitigation Program”; and
WHEREAS, the cost estimate for the Project is $1.402 million,
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED: that the Board of Trustees of the Village of
Croton-on-Hudson hereby authorizes the Village Manager to take all necessary
action to submit an application for $701,000 in the Westchester County Flood
Mitigation Program to improve the drainage along Brook Street and Terrace
Place, and, upon the funding of said amount, commits to provide the match of
$701,000.

Resolution #183-2025
On motion of TRUSTEE SIMON, seconded by TRUSTEE NICHOLSON, the
following resolution was adopted by the Board of Trustees of the Village of
Croton-on-Hudson, New York with a 5-0 vote.
WHEREAS, the Village of Croton-on-Hudson has discovered a leak within the
sanitary sewer main on Ridge Road; and
WHEREAS, the Village is responsible for the maintenance and upkeep of the
sanitary sewer system; and
WHEREAS, DPW has received an estimate for the relining of the sanitary
sewer main in the amount of $42,200; and
WHEREAS, General Municipal Law Section 103(4) allows for the Village Board
to let a contract without public bidding where necessary due to a public
emergency arising out of an unforeseen occurrence or condition; and
WHEREAS, the Superintendent of Public Works has recommended that the
Village proceed with this work on an emergency basis,
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED: that the Village Board hereby declares
the relining of the sanitary sewer main on Ridge Road to be an emergency;
and
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED: the Village Manager is authorized to accept the
proposal from Insituform, of Charlton, Massachusetts, in the amount of
$42,200 to complete this work; and
