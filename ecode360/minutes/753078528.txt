MEETING SUMMARY

Croton IDEA Advisory 06/07
07 June 2024 / 2:45 PM / MUNICIPAL BUILDING CONFERENCE ROOM
Committee members briefed each other on DEI initiatives throughout the
community, reviewed the Summerfest booth, discussed progress on Croton
Welcomes Everyone, finished Juneteenth planning, and discussed the Village’s
translation needs and possible future programming.
Summerfest review
Special thanks was given to the volunteers (IDEA members and more) who worked
at the IDEA booth. CoHWE was successfully introduced to Croton residents and
beyond with signatories to our Statement of Inclusion and 20+ email contacts.
Croton Welcomes Everyone
A preliminary CoHWE.org website has been launched. The Board of Trustees and
Manager’s office are reviewing further website content. Village lawyer will also
review.
Juneteenth
Publicity has begun including flyers, LTE in Croton Gazette, Croton Community
Calendar, Croton Newsletter and social media. Performers and refreshments were
confirmed for the picnic. CHHS StAR (Students Against Racism) Club members
joined the meeting and discussed their participation, including decoration,
day-of-event volunteering, publicity and possibly public speaking.
Translation is still needed for Village communications. Members are meeting with
Village admin and doing a cursory review of the Village website.
Future programs could include an anti-hate forum, civic education, student history
presentations, and CHHS student/alumni forum.
