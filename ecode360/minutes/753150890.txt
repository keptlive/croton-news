WATERFRONT ADVISORY COMMITTEE MEETING
Wednesday, March 5th, 2024 4:30 p.m.
Small Conference Room

PRESENT  Len Simon, Chairperson
         Stuart Greenbaum
         Steve Krisky
         Cara Politi
         Leo Wiegman (via Speakerphone)

ALSO PRESENT: Daniel O'Connor, P.E. Village Engineer

1) CALL TO ORDER

Chairman Simon called the Waterfront Advisory Committee meeting of March 5th, 2025 to order at 4:30 p.m. and welcomed new member Cara Politi.

2) REFERRALS

a) Referral from Village Board of Trustees for a Local Waterfront Revitalization Program (LWRP) Consistency Review for Local Law Introductory No. 2 of 2025, to update Chapter 230, Zoning, of the Village Code to transfer the authority to issue special permits to the Village Planning Board

The WAC reviewed the Short Environmental Assessment Form and had no changes. Steve Krisky made a motion to accept the Short Environmental Assessment Form, seconded by Leo Wiegman, and carried, 5-0.

The WAC reviewed the Coastal Assessment Form and had no changes. Stuart Greenbaum made a motion to accept the Coastal Assessment Form, seconded by Steve Krisky, and carried, 5 - 0.

The WAC reviewed the 44 LWRP policies and identified the applicable policy as noted in the memo attached. Since there is nothing in the referrals that is inconsistent, the WAC made a recommendation of consistency.

MOTION: Stuart Greenbaum made a recommendation of consistency, seconded by Steve Krisky, and the motion carried, 5 – 0.

b) Referral from the Village Board of Trustees for a Local Waterfront Revitalization Program (LWRP) Consistency Review for Local Law Introductory No. 3 of 2025, to update Chapter 116, Environmental Review, of the Village Code to provide for the listing of additional Type II actions under the State Environmental Quality Review Act (SEQRA)

The WAC reviewed the Short Environmental Assessment Form and had no changes. Steve Krisky made a motion to accept the short environmental assessment form, seconded by Stuart Greenbaum, and carried, 5-0.

The WAC reviewed the Coastal Assessment Form and had no changes. Steve Krisky made a motion to accept the Coastal Assessment Form, seconded by Stuart Greenbaum, and carried, 5 - 0.

The WAC reviewed the 44 LWRP policies and identified the applicable policy as noted in the memo attached. Since there is nothing in the referrals that is inconsistent, the WAC made a recommendation of consistency.

MOTION: Leo Wiegman made a recommendation of consistency, seconded by Steve Krisky, and the motion carried, 5 – 0.

3. APPROVAL OF MINUTES

Steve Krisky made a motion to approve the minutes of the WAC meeting of October 23rd, 2024, seconded by Stuart Greenbaum, and the motion carried by a vote of 5 - 0.

4. ADJOURNMENT:

There being no further business, Stuart Greenbaum made a motion for adjournment, seconded by Leo Wiegman, and the meeting adjourned at 4:52 p.m.

Respectfully submitted, Karen Stapleton
Secretary to the Waterfront Advisory Committee

***Minutes approved May 5, 2025 with a motion by Stuart Greenbaum, seconded by Leo Wiegman, and carried by a vote of 4 – 0, with Cara Politi being absent***

TO:    Mayor Brian Pugh and the Village Board of Trustees

FROM:  Chairman Simon, Waterfront Advisory Committee

RE:    Referral from Village Board for a Local Waterfront Revitalization Program (LWRP)
       Consistency review for Local Law Introductory 2 of 2025 amending the Zoning Law of
       the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, Chapter 230, to transfer the authority to issue special
       permits to the Village Planning Board.

DATE:  March 6, 2025

On March 5, 2025, the Waterfront Advisory Committee (WAC) reviewed for consistency with the Village's Local Waterfront Revitalization Program (LWRP) the referral listed above. Based on the Committee's review of the 44 LWRP policies, the Waterfront Advisory Committee made a recommendation of consistency with the LWRP.

The WAC reviewed the Short Environmental Assessment Form and determined that there were no changes necessary.

The WAC reviewed the Coastal Assessment Form and no changes were noted.

The committee reviewed the 44 LWRP policies and determined that the following policy is relevant:

POLICY 6: Expedite permit procedures in order to facilitate the siting of development activities at suitable locations

Having the Planning Board issue special permits will potentially save time for applicants, and would align with the practices of other local municipalities.

It is for the reasons contained in this memorandum that the WAC issues its recommendation of consistency.
