A local law changing the zoning designation of a certain parcel of land within the Village of
Croton on Hudson, amending the tax map, and expanding the area of the North End
Gateway District.

Be it enacted by the Board of Trastees of the
Village of Croton-on-Hudson as follows:

Section 1. The Village of Croton of Hudson Zoning Law is hereby amended as follows:
The parcel identified as Section 67.10, Block 2, Lot 2 as shown on the tax maps and
tax assessment rolls bf the Village is rezoned from the RA-25 Residential District to
the Commercial C-1 District.
Section 2. Section 230-20.2 Location of Gateway Areas (3) is amended to read as follows:
North end of the Village along Albany Post Road (9A), consisting of the eight lots on the
West side of Albany Post Road between Routes 9 and 9 A, and the Village boundary and
Warren Road and the one lot on the East side of Albany Post Road south of the To wn of
Cortlandt boundary located at 1 Baltic Place. This area marks the entrance to the Village
from the north along Routes 9 and 9A.
Section 3. The Section 230 Attachment E is amended to add parcel Section>67.10, Block 2, Lot 2
to the list of parcels in The North End Gateway District
Section 4. The Official Zoning Map of the Village of Croton on Hudson, NY is hereby ainended
to reflect the foregoing changes.
Section 5. This local law shall take effect immediately upon filing with the Secretary of State.

On motion Of TRUSTEE LEVITT, seconded by TRUSTEE SLIPPEN,
the following resolution was adopted by the Board of Trustees ofthe Village of
Croton-on-Hudson, New York; with a 4-1 vote (Trustee Gallelli voting Nay).
WHEREAS, the Village Board.is considering taking an action to adopt Local Law
Introductory No. .9 of 2015 to amend the zoning code ofthe village and official
zoning map by rezoning the parcel identified as Section 67.10, Block 2, Lot 2 from
RA-25 Residential District.to the Commercial C-1 District and adding this parcel to
the North End Gateway district (the proposed action); and
WHEREAS, on September 21, 2015 the Village Board issued a Negative Declaration
in connection with the Proposed Action; and
WHEREAS, on October 19, 2015 the Village Board deternnined that the Proposed
Action complies with the standards arid conditions set forth in the Village's LWRP;
and
WHEREAS, a Public Hearing was held and closed pn November 2, 2015,
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED: that the Village Board of Trustees adopts
LocalLaw Introductory No. 9 of 2015 to amend the zoning code ofthe village and
official zoning map by rezoning the parcel identified as Section 67.10, Block 2, Lot 2
from RA-25 Residential District to the Commercial C-1 District and adding this
parcel to the North End Gateway district (the proposed action), which upon
adoption will become Local Law No.11 of 2015. .
