Monday, November 22, 2021, at 7:00 PM, in the Georgianna Grant Meeting Room at the Stanley
Kellerhouse Municipal Building, Van Wyck Street, Croton-on-Hudson, NY 10520.

The following resolution was adopted by the Board of Trustees:
Resolution #187-2021
On motion of TRUSTEE HOROWITZ, seconded by TRUSTEE MURTAUGH, the following
resolution was adopted by the Board of Trustees of the Village of Croton-on-Hudson, New York:
WHEREAS, the Little League utilizes the baseball field located at Dobbs Park within the Village
pursuant to the terms of permits which are issued to it from time to time; and
WHEREAS, the Little League desires to undertake certain improvements to the Field at Little
League’s sole cost and expense and by a contractor to be retained by the Little League; and
WHEREAS, the Village is willing to allow the Little League to undertake the improvements to
the Village property subject to the terms and conditions in the agreement,
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED: that the Village Board of Trustees hereby approves
the agreement between the Village of Croton-on-Hudson and Croton Little League; and
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED: that the Village Manager is hereby authorized to execute the
agreement on behalf of the Village Board of Trustees.
Respectfully Submitted,
Thomas C. Morzello, Assistant to the Village Manager
